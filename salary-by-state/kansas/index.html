Working in Kansas: wage floor, median pay, and taxes

The median annual wage for all occupations in Kansas is $50,310 according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics May 2024 Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics survey. Kansas follows the federal floor and taxes income at two flat brackets, 5.2% and 5.58%. For anyone comparing offers across state lines, these two numbers — the median and the floor — bracket what an employer in Kansas typically pays.

Minimum wage in Kansas

As of 2025 the effective minimum wage in Kansas is $7.25 per hour. That works out to $15,080 per year at a full-time 2,080-hour schedule. Use the calculator above to convert any hourly figure — including this floor — into weekly, biweekly, monthly, and annual figures for Kansas.

Frequently asked (Kansas)

Is Kansas's minimum wage higher than the federal rate? No — it matches the federal $7.25.

What is a good salary in Kansas? Anything above the $50,310 statewide median puts a single earner above half of all workers in Kansas; household costs vary considerably by metro.

How do taxes change take-home pay in Kansas? Kansas taxes income at a flat 5.7% (2025), the same rate at every income level, so state tax scales evenly with pay rather than jumping at bracket lines.
