Working in Missouri: wage floor, median pay, and taxes

The median annual wage for all occupations in Missouri is $50,160 according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics May 2024 Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics survey. Missouri's minimum steps toward $15.00 in 2026 under the 2020 Prop B schedule, then indexes. For anyone comparing offers across state lines, these two numbers — the median and the floor — bracket what an employer in Missouri typically pays.

Minimum wage in Missouri

As of 2025 the effective minimum wage in Missouri is $13.75 per hour. That works out to $28,600 per year at a full-time 2,080-hour schedule. Use the calculator above to convert any hourly figure — including this floor — into weekly, biweekly, monthly, and annual figures for Missouri.

Frequently asked (Missouri)

Is Missouri's minimum wage higher than the federal rate? Yes — $13.75 exceeds the federal $7.25.

What is a good salary in Missouri? Anything above the $50,160 statewide median puts a single earner above half of all workers in Missouri; household costs vary considerably by metro.

How do taxes change take-home pay in Missouri? Missouri runs a graduated income tax, 0% to 4.7% for single filers in 2025, so the state's bite grows as pay rises. Net pay is gross minus federal tax, FICA, and Missouri state tax.
