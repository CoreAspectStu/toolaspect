How the Test Grade Calculator Works

Grading a test by hand is one division problem per student. This calculator does the division, converts it to a letter grade on the scale you pick, and then answers the two follow-up questions every student actually has: what letter is that, and how close was I to the next one up?

The formula

Percentage = points earned ÷ points possible × 100. The percentage then maps onto a letter scale. For "how many points for the next letter up," the calculator multiplies the next band's cutoff by points possible and rounds up — that's the smallest whole-point score that clears the bar.

How to use it

Type in your points earned and points possible, pick the scale your class uses, and read the result. Teachers can scroll to the quick-grade table, set the points possible once, and keep the grading key open while they mark. It works for quizzes, homework, midterms — anything scored in points.

A worked example

Take 21 out of 30. The division is 21 ÷ 30 = 0.70, so the percentage is 70%. On the 10-point scale with plus/minus grades, 70% lands at the bottom of the C− band (70–72.9%), worth 1.7 GPA points.

Now the follow-up. To reach a C at 73%, you'd need 73% × 30 = 21.9 points, which rounds up to 22 — one more point buys a whole letter step, since 22/30 = 73.3%. A B− at 80% takes 24 points, and a B at 83% takes 25 (24.9 rounded up). Another common one: 18 out of 25 is 72%, also a C−, and 45 out of 50 is exactly 90%, an A−.

Frequently Asked Questions

What grade is 21 out of 30?

21 out of 30 is 70%, which is a C- on the standard 10-point scale (C- covers 70-72.9%). On a simple letter scale without plus/minus grades it's a C. To reach a solid C (73%) you'd need 22 out of 30, and a B- (80%) takes 24 out of 30.

How do I calculate my test grade as a percentage?

Divide the points you earned by the points possible and multiply by 100. For example, 18 out of 25 is 18 ÷ 25 × 100 = 72%. That percentage maps to a letter grade using your class's grading scale, most commonly the 10-point scale where 90-100 is an A, 80-89 a B, 70-79 a C, and 60-69 a D.

How many points do I need for the next letter grade?

Multiply the cutoff percentage by the points possible and round up. If you have 21 out of 30 (70%, a C-) and want a C at 73%, you need 73% × 30 = 21.9, rounded up to 22 points, which scores 73.3%. The calculator shows this gap for every letter band automatically.

What is a passing grade on a test?

On the 10-point scale, 60% (a D) is technically passing in most high school and college classes, but many programs require a C or better (70%) for credit toward a major, and degree-specific courses often require higher. Always check your syllabus, because cutoffs vary by school and by teacher.

Is a 70 percent a C or a C-?

With plus/minus grading, 70-72.9% is a C-, so exactly 70% is a C-. Without plus/minus grading, anything from 70-79.9% is a plain C. The GPA difference is real: a C- is usually 1.7 while a C is 2.0 on the 4.0 scale.
