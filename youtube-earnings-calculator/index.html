How YouTube Ad Revenue Works

YouTube pays creators through Google AdSense. Your earnings depend on RPM (Revenue Per Mille = per 1,000 views). Finance and business channels earn $5-15 RPM, while entertainment and vlog channels earn $1-3 RPM. Niches like insurance and legal can earn $8-20+ RPM.

Other Revenue Streams

  • Channel Memberships: $4.99/mo per member (70/30 split)
  • Super Chat: Live stream donations during premieres
  • Sponsorships: $20-50 per 1,000 views for mid-size channels
  • Affiliate Links: Amazon, software, courses
  • Merch: T-shirts, mugs, digital products
