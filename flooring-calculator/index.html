How the Flooring Calculator Works

Enter your room dimensions or total area, set the box coverage printed on the carton of your laminate or LVP, and adjust the waste factor for your layout. The calculator returns the number of boxes to buy, the square footage of underlayment to purchase, and a total material cost. Box coverage varies by brand — typical laminate boxes cover 18–24 sq ft and LVP boxes 20–30 sq ft, so always check the carton label.

Formulas Used

  • Buying area: area × (1 + waste%)
  • Boxes: ceil(buying area ÷ sq ft per box)
  • Underlayment: same buying area (order by the roll), rolls = ceil(area ÷ roll size)
  • Cost: boxes × price per box + underlayment sq ft × price per sq ft

Worked Example

A 15 ft × 12 ft bedroom = 180 sq ft. With 10% waste you buy for 198 sq ft. If each box covers 23.8 sq ft at $45: 198 ÷ 23.8 = 8.3 → 9 boxes = $405. Underlayment at $0.35/sq ft adds $69.30 (two 100 sq ft rolls). Total materials: about $474 before trim and transitions.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much waste should I add? 5–10% for straight layouts in simple rectangular rooms; 12–15% for diagonal installs, herringbone, or rooms with lots of angles and closets.

Do I need underlayment under LVP? If the plank doesn't have pre-attached padding, yes — a thin foam or combo foam+vapor barrier. Over concrete, always use a 6-mil vapor barrier.

How many boxes of laminate do I need for 500 sq ft? At 10% waste you're buying 550 sq ft. With a typical 23.8 sq ft box, that's 24 boxes (571 sq ft on hand).

Can I return unopened boxes? Most retailers accept them within 30–90 days — buy the extra box, don't short yourself mid-install.
