How Loan Payments Work

Our loan calculator helps you understand exactly how much a personal loan, auto loan, or student loan will cost you. Enter the loan amount, interest rate (APR), and loan term to see your monthly payment, total interest, and complete amortization schedule.

Loan Payment Formula

The formula for calculating fixed-rate loan payments: M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]

  • M = Monthly payment
  • P = Principal (loan amount)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (APR ÷ 12)
  • n = Total number of monthly payments

Types of Loans

  • Personal Loans: Unsecured loans, typically 6-60 month terms, rates from 6% to 36%
  • Auto Loans: Secured by vehicle, 36-72 month terms, rates from 4% to 12%
  • Student Loans: Federal or private, 10-25 year terms, rates from 3% to 8%
  • Mortgages: Use our mortgage calculator for home loans

Tips for Getting the Best Loan Rate

  • Improve your credit score before applying — above 740 gets the best rates
  • Compare offers from multiple lenders (banks, credit unions, online lenders)
  • Choose a shorter term for lower rates and less total interest
  • Consider making a larger down payment to reduce the loan amount
  • Watch for origination fees, prepayment penalties, and other hidden costs

Understanding APR vs Interest Rate

The interest rate is the cost of borrowing money. APR includes the interest rate plus fees (origination fees, closing costs), giving the true annual cost. Always compare APRs when shopping for loans — a loan with a lower interest rate but high fees may cost more than one with a slightly higher rate and no fees.
