About the Age Calculator

Our age calculator is a free online tool that lets you determine your exact age down to the very minute. Simply enter your date of birth and instantly see your age expressed in years, months, days, hours, and minutes. Whether you need to know your precise age for official documents, health assessments, or just out of curiosity, this calculator provides accurate results in seconds. It also reveals fun facts like the day of the week you were born, your zodiac sign, and your birthstone.

The date difference calculator goes beyond simple age computation. Use it to find the exact span between any two dates — perfect for project planning, counting down to an event, or tracking milestones. Results are displayed in multiple formats including total years, months, weeks, days, and hours, giving you a comprehensive view of the time elapsed.

Planning a celebration? The birthday countdown tool tells you exactly how many days remain until your next birthday, what day of the week it falls on, and which birthday number you are celebrating. All calculations are performed entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server — so your personal information stays completely private.
