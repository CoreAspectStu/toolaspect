Payouts require a $50 balance (PayPal or direct deposit). The Rumble Creator Program — updated July 1, 2026 — pays separately, from a share of Premium revenue, for streaming creators who meet its requirements.

How the Rumble Calculator Works

Rumble monetizes differently from YouTube, and pretending otherwise produces bad forecasts. There's no published RPM table, so the honest model is a band with your own rate slotted in once you've been paid once.

The formula

Monthly earnings = views ÷ 1,000 × rate + licensing income. The rate is what you keep per thousand views after Rumble's cut — on the standard 60/40 split, a $10 gross CPM leaves you $6. The $50 threshold math divides 50 by your monthly earnings: below $50 a month, your first payout waits until the balance accumulates.

How to use it

Enter monthly views from your dashboard and start at $5 per 1,000 — mid-band. The floor and ceiling cards show the $2-$10 span so you're never anchored to one number. Got a licensing deal or flat payment? Add it on the licensing line. After your first real payout, replace the estimate with your actual rate.

A worked example

A news-commentary channel pulling 250,000 monthly views at the $5 mid-rate earns 250 × $5 = $1,250 a month, $15,000 a year. The same audience spans $500 at the floor and $2,500 at the ceiling — the spread is the geography and niche of the viewers, not luck. A smaller channel at 8,000 views and $3 per 1,000 earns $24 a month: below the $50 payout line, so the first payment arrives in month 3, when the balance reaches $72.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Rumble pay per 1,000 views?

Rumble doesn't publish a fixed rate. Third-party 2026 analyses put typical earnings at $2 to $10 per 1,000 views on a 60/40 ad revenue split — news and political commentary often clears $10, while entertainment and gaming usually lands at $2 to $5. Audience geography moves it most: US, UK, and Canadian viewers monetize far higher than other regions.

What are the requirements for the Rumble Creator Program?

Per Rumble's official page, updated July 1, 2026: to apply you need at least 100 total followers, at least 1 hour streamed via Rumble Studio in the last 30 days, and an active Rumble Premium subscription that stays active. Once in, each month requires 30 streamed hours, 5 of them to Rumble Premium, 500 hours of watch time, 75 chatters, one raid across five separate livestreams, and 20 shorts uploaded or clipped.

What is Rumble's minimum payout?

$50, paid via PayPal or direct deposit. Earnings below the threshold roll forward until they cross it. US creators need a completed W-9 in the dashboard, and payments are calculated by the end of the month following the earning period.

Do you need followers to make money on Rumble?

Not for the basic ad route — third-party analyses report you can monetize from your first upload, which is Rumble's main pitch against YouTube's 1,000-subscriber threshold. The Creator Program is different: it requires 100 followers plus active livestreaming and a Rumble Premium subscription.

How does Rumble pay compare to YouTube?

Rumble's creator share (60%) beats YouTube's standard 55%, and Rumble's RPM band of roughly $2-$10 overlaps YouTube's typical $5-$15 for US creators. YouTube pays more at scale for most niches, but monetizes nothing until you clear 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours — so small channels often start earning on Rumble first.
