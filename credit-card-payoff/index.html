The True Cost of Minimum Payments

If you only pay the minimum (usually 2-3% of balance), a $5,000 balance at 24.99% APR takes over 20 years to pay off and costs $7,000+ in interest. Even a small increase in your monthly payment can save years and thousands of dollars.

Strategies to Pay Off Faster

  • Avalanche method: Pay minimum on all cards, extra on highest APR first
  • Snowball method: Pay off smallest balance first for psychological wins
  • Balance transfer: Move to a 0% intro APR card (saves the most if you can pay before promo ends)
  • Consolidation loan: Personal loan at lower rate to pay off cards
  • Stop using the card: Can't pay it off while still adding charges

Average Credit Card Rates

  • Average APR: 24.99% (2026)
  • Store cards: 28-30%
  • Best credit (740+): 14-18%
  • 0% intro APR periods: typically 12-21 months
