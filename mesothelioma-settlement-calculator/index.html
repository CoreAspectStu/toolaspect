How the Mesothelioma Settlement Calculator Works

If you're here soon after a diagnosis, yours or a parent's, we're sorry you need this page. It's built to do one thing calmly: put what published sources report about asbestos compensation next to your own numbers, so the conversations ahead feel less like guesswork.

What this tool shows

It presents published ranges, never a prediction for your case. Mealey's Litigation Reports, the standard industry reporter, puts the average mesothelioma settlement at $1 million to $1.4 million, and about 95% of cases settle before a verdict. Alongside that benchmark, the tool estimates two things specific to you: your documented economic losses and a typical combined range for asbestos trust claims.

How the math works

Documented economic losses are the years between your age at diagnosis and your planned retirement, times your annual income, plus medical costs so far and projected. The trust range multiplies the number of trusts you may qualify for by $10,000 on the low end and $50,000 on the high end; each of the roughly 60 active trusts publishes its own schedule and pays a set percentage of it. The routes stack: trust claims, a lawsuit, and VA benefits aren't mutually exclusive.

How to use it

Most fields are self-explanatory. The trust count is the one people rarely know: an asbestos attorney builds it from your work history, and many claimants qualify for 10 or more. If you served in the military, especially the Navy, mark it. Mesothelioma from service-era asbestos qualifies for VA disability, and a 100% rating pays $3,938.58 a month in 2026, on top of any legal claim. Also worth knowing: compensation generally doesn't depend on proving an employer negligent, because most claims target product manufacturers and trusts, not employers.

A worked example

A 62-year-old with pleural mesothelioma earning $60,000 who planned to retire at 67 has $300,000 in lost earnings plus $400,000 in projected treatment costs: $700,000 in documented economic losses before pain and suffering even enters the math. The published settlement average of $1 million to $1.4 million reflects both.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average mesothelioma settlement?

Mealey's Litigation Reports, the standard industry reporter for asbestos litigation, puts the average mesothelioma settlement at $1 million to $1.4 million. About 95% of cases settle before a verdict. Where a claim lands in that range depends on diagnosis, exposure documentation, age and income, and how many defendants and trusts are involved.

How do asbestos trust funds work?

About 60 active trusts hold roughly $30 billion set aside in bankruptcy by former asbestos manufacturers. Each trust publishes a scheduled value per disease and pays a percentage of it; the Johns-Manville trust, the oldest, currently pays 5.1% of scheduled value. Payments commonly land in the low five figures per trust, but claimants usually qualify for several trusts at once, so combined recoveries often reach the low-to-mid six figures.

How long does mesothelioma compensation take?

Trust claims are the fastest route, typically paying in months rather than years. Settlements generally run 12 to 18 months, and some courts move living mesothelioma patients onto expedited dockets. Trial verdicts take 2 to 4 years plus appeals, which is one reason about 95% of cases settle instead.

What is the statute of limitations for asbestos claims?

Between 1 and 6 years from diagnosis depending on the state; most states allow 2 to 3 years. Wrongful-death claims run on their own separate clock. Filing early matters beyond the deadline itself, because exposure testimony is strongest close to diagnosis.

Do most mesothelioma cases go to trial?

No. About 95% settle before a verdict. Trials can produce far larger awards, with recent Mealey's reports putting verdict averages in the $5 million to $20 million range, skewed by a few enormous awards. But only around 5% of cases get there, trials take years, and verdicts get appealed and reduced. For most families, settlement plus trust claims is the practical path.
