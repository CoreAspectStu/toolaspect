1 cup flour = 120 grams (all-purpose, unsifted)
1 cup sugar = 200 grams (granulated white)
1 cup powdered sugar = 120 grams

Tablespoon to Milliliters Conversion

One US tablespoon equals 14.79 mL, commonly rounded to 15 mL in recipes. An Australian tablespoon is 20 mL, and a UK tablespoon is 15 mL (standardized to match the metric teaspoon × 3). If you're following an international recipe, be aware of these differences. Here's a quick reference:

1 tbsp (US) = 14.79 mL

Temperature Conversion Formulas

Temperature conversions follow exact mathematical formulas rather than simple multiplication factors:

Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
Celsius to Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15
Fahrenheit to Kelvin: K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

Common temperature reference points: Water freezes at 0°C (32°F, 273.15 K). Water boils at 100°C (212°F, 373.15 K) at sea level. Room temperature is about 20–22°C (68–72°F). Body temperature is approximately 37°C (98.6°F). For baking, a moderate oven is 180°C (350°F), and a hot oven is 220°C (425°F).

Metric vs Imperial: Understanding the Two Systems

The metric system (used by most of the world) is based on units of 10: millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers, grams, kilograms, milliliters, and liters. Conversions within the metric system are simple — just move the decimal point. The imperial system (used primarily in the United States, and the related US customary system) uses inches, feet, miles, ounces, pounds, cups, pints, quarts, and gallons. These units don't follow a consistent base-10 relationship, which is why a converter tool is so useful.

The US is one of only three countries (alongside Liberia and Myanmar) that have not fully adopted the metric system for everyday use. The UK uses a hybrid system — metric for most purposes but imperial for road distances, beer, and body weight. This means recipes, product labels, and measurements can vary by country, making unit conversion an essential everyday tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many grams are in a cup of flour?
One US cup of all-purpose flour weighs approximately 120 grams. However, this can vary from 110–130 grams depending on how the flour is scooped and packed. For consistent baking results, we recommend using a kitchen scale rather than measuring cups.

How many mL in a tablespoon?
One US tablespoon = 14.79 mL (commonly rounded to 15 mL). A metric tablespoon (used in Australia) = 20 mL. A UK tablespoon = 15 mL.

How many tablespoons in a cup?
There are 16 US tablespoons in 1 US cup. Similarly, there are 48 teaspoons in a cup and 8 fluid ounces in a cup.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?
Use the formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. For example, to convert 350°F to Celsius: (350 − 32) × 5/9 = 176.7°C. Or simply use the Temperature tab in our converter above.

Why are US and UK measurements different?
The US uses the US customary system, while the UK formerly used the imperial system (and now mostly uses metric). A US cup (236.59 mL) is smaller than a UK/imperial cup (284.13 mL). A US gallon (3.785 L) is smaller than a UK gallon (4.546 L). Always check which system a recipe uses.

What's the difference between fluid ounces and weight ounces?
Fluid ounces (fl oz) measure volume (liquids), while regular ounces (oz) measure weight. One fluid ounce of water weighs approximately 1 ounce, but this is coincidental — a fluid ounce of oil weighs less than an ounce, and a fluid ounce of honey weighs more.

How accurate are volume-to-weight conversions?
Volume-to-weight conversions depend on ingredient density, which varies by ingredient and even by brand. Our converter uses standard average densities for common ingredients, but for precision baking, always use a digital kitchen scale.
