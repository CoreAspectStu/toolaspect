The Ultimate Cooking Unit Converter

Whether you're following a recipe from another country or scaling your favorite dish, accurate unit conversion in the kitchen is essential. Our unit converter makes it easy to switch between cups, grams, tablespoons, ounces, and milliliters — the most common measurements found in recipes worldwide. Instead of guessing how many grams are in a cup of flour or how many milliliters a tablespoon holds, get precise, instant results that take the guesswork out of cooking and baking.

Baking is a science, and even small measurement errors can affect your results. A cup of all-purpose flour is approximately 120 grams, but a cup of sugar weighs around 200 grams — which is why converting by weight (grams) is far more accurate than relying on volume (cups). Our kitchen converter uses standard density values so you can trust your measurements whether you're meal-prepping, baking bread, or trying an international recipe for the first time.
