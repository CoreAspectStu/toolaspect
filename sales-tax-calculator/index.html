Sales Tax Guide

This sales tax calculator lets you quickly add sales tax to any purchase amount or reverse-calculate the original price from a tax-inclusive total. Includes current state-level rates for all 50 US states.

How to Calculate Sales Tax

Forward (add tax): Tax = Price × (Rate ÷ 100). Total = Price + Tax.

Reverse (remove tax): Original = Total ÷ (1 + Rate ÷ 100). Tax = Total − Original.

States with No Sales Tax

  • Delaware — no state or local sales tax
  • Montana — no state sales tax (local option in some areas)
  • New Hampshire — no sales tax (but has meals/rooms tax)
  • Oregon — no state sales tax (local option in some cities)
  • Alaska — no state sales tax (but local municipalities may charge)

Sales Tax vs VAT

Sales tax is only charged at the final point of sale to the consumer. VAT (Value Added Tax) is charged at each stage of production but credited back to businesses. Most countries use VAT (typically 15-25%); the US uses sales tax. When shopping internationally, always check whether prices include tax.
