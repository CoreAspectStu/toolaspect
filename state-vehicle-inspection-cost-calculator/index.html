How much a car inspection costs depends almost entirely on your state. New York caps it at $10 for the safety check, $21 with emissions in NYC, Long Island, Westchester and Rockland. Pennsylvania doesn't cap it: $35-$80 safety plus $38-$45 emissions, or $75-$120 combined. Texas ended safety inspections January 1, 2025 — you now pay a $7.50 replacement fee at registration, plus $18.50 for emissions if you're in a DFW or Houston county ($11.50 in El Paso, Travis and Williamson). Virginia runs $20 a year plus $30 emissions every two years in NoVA. Missouri checks only 10-plus-year-old cars, every two years, capped at $12. Pick your state below for the exact breakdown.

The long trend is repeal: states from Utah (2018) to Texas (2025) have dropped periodic safety checks, arguing modern vehicles fail rarely and crash data shows no measurable safety difference. Emissions programs are a separate federal-clean-air mandate and outlast the safety rolls — that's why Houston still tests while Austin's safety check is gone.

How the Car Inspection Cost Calculator Works

Inspection pricing is quirky because every state runs its own program: some set a statutory cap, some let stations charge whatever the market bears, some bundle emissions, and some check only older cars. The calculator encodes each focus state's actual rules and shows the real out-of-pocket number, per cycle and annualized.

What it computes

Pick a state and your county situation. The tool applies the state's fee structure: New York's $10 safety cap plus $11 NYVIP3 emissions; Pennsylvania's station-set bands; Texas's post-2025 regime ($7.50 replacement fee, $18.50 or $11.50 emissions by county); Virginia's $20 safety plus $30 biennial emissions; Missouri's $12 cap for qualifying vehicles plus the $24 St. Louis emissions test. Where emissions runs every two years, the annualized figure halves it so states compare fairly.

A worked example, three states

A driver in Queens: $10 safety + $11 emissions = $21, every year. A driver in Dallas County: no safety inspection anymore, but $18.50 emissions plus the $7.50 replacement fee on registration = $26 a year. A driver in Fairfax County, Virginia: $20 safety every year plus $30 emissions every other year — $50 in an emissions year, $35 a year averaged. Same annual registration ritual, three different bills.

Pennsylvania is the wildcard: nothing stops a station from charging $90 for what the next shop does for $45, so the $75-$120 combined band is the market, not the law. Call two stations. In Missouri the newest cars skip the whole exercise — under 10 years old and under 150,000 miles means no inspection at all.

Fees change: states raise caps, counties join or leave emissions programs (Bexar County, Texas adds emissions testing November 1, 2026), and legislatures keep voting on repeals. Verify against your DMV's current schedule before budgeting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a car inspection in New York?

New York caps the annual safety inspection at $10 for passenger vehicles. In the NYVIP emissions zone (New York City, Long Island, Westchester and Rockland), the OBD emissions check adds $11, so a typical inspection sticker costs $21 total. Upstate counties outside the emissions zone pay the $10 safety inspection only.

How much is a car inspection in Pennsylvania?

Pennsylvania doesn't cap the labor fee, so stations set their own prices: typically $35-$80 for the annual safety inspection plus a PennDOT sticker fee (raised to $12 in mid-2025), and $38-$45 for the emissions inspection required in about 25 mostly Pittsburgh- and Philadelphia-area counties. Realistic combined total: $75-$120.

Does Texas still require vehicle inspections?

Not the safety inspection. Since January 1, 2025, non-commercial vehicles no longer need an annual safety inspection; instead every registration renewal includes a $7.50 Inspection Program Replacement Fee. Emissions testing continues in 17 counties — the Dallas-Fort Worth and Houston metros, plus El Paso, Travis and Williamson — costing $18.50, or $11.50 in El Paso, Travis and Williamson counties. Bexar County joins November 1, 2026 at $18.50.

How much is the Virginia state inspection?

Virginia charges a maximum of $20 for the annual safety inspection ($12 for motorcycles). In the Northern Virginia emissions localities, the emissions inspection is $30 every two years, so an emissions-year total runs $50 and the annualized cost is about $35. Vehicles exempt from the emissions test still pay a $2 emissions fee at renewal.

How much is a Missouri safety inspection?

Missouri caps the safety inspection fee at $12 for passenger vehicles, but only vehicles 10 model years old or older (or with 150,000+ miles) need one — every two years at registration renewal. Newer vehicles are exempt. In the St. Louis region, an emissions inspection (about $24) is also required every two years, bringing the biennial total to about $36.

Which states still require vehicle safety inspections?

As of 2026, the states commonly requiring periodic safety inspections are Delaware, Hawaii, Louisiana, Maine, Massachusetts, Mississippi, New Hampshire, New York, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia and DC (all annual), plus Missouri (biennial, 10+ year-old vehicles). Texas eliminated its requirement January 1, 2025. Most states require emissions testing only, or nothing at all.
