What Is Ideal Body Weight?

Ideal weight formulas estimate a healthy weight for your height. The Devine formula is most commonly used in clinical settings. BMI range (18.5-24.9) gives a wider healthy window. Remember: muscle weighs more than fat, so athletic individuals may exceed "ideal" weight without being unhealthy.
