Pet Costs, Calculated

A pet is a decade-long series of numbers that arrive one at a time: the insurance quote at adoption, the food bill that scales with body weight every month, a relocation that needs the animal flown cross-country, and eventually a four-figure veterinary decision. The calculators here convert each one into a range you can check a bill or quote against, using published national data and the same arithmetic a carrier, nutritionist, or broker would use.

Start with the recurring lines: the pet insurance calculator prices monthly premiums from species, breed, age, state, and plan tier against NAPHIA's 2024 industry averages, and the dog food and cat food calculators compute daily calories and translate them into cans, cups, or ounces. The dog age calculator settles the human-years question properly, by breed size, and the cat age calculator does the same for cats with the 15/9/4 rule.

Then the episodic costs. Shipping a pet runs $275-$1,500 depending on mode and distance; a DNA test runs $60-$160 depending on what you want to know. Chronic and routine care has its own calculators too: the dog allergy treatment cost calculator prices Cytopoint, Apoquel, and immunotherapy over multiple years, the dental cleaning calculator itemizes the anesthesia-and-extractions bill, and the boarding and daycare calculators price travel and workday care by tier and frequency. And if the worst happens, the dog bite settlement calculator estimates what a claim is worth before you accept an insurer's first offer.
