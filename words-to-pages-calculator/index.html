How the Words to Pages Calculator Works

Page count isn't a property of words; it's a property of words plus a layout. The calculator applies standard words-per-page densities to your count and shows both the exact decimal and the practical rounded-up figure, since a "7.3-page" paper still physically ends on page 8.

The conventions

Double-spaced, 12-point type with 1-inch margins holds about 275 words a page, which is the number most converters and essay services use. Single-spaced doubles it to 550. One-and-a-half spacing lands between at roughly 410, and college-ruled handwritten pages hold about 175 words. All four are built in, plus a custom field if your template differs.

How to use it

Type your word count, pick the format, and read the answer. The sentence, paragraph, and read-aloud estimates (18 words per sentence, 100 per paragraph, 140 words per minute aloud) help you sanity-check pacing: a 2,000-word essay is roughly 111 sentences, 20 paragraphs, and 14 minutes of read-aloud.

A worked example

Take a 2,000-word essay due double-spaced. At 275 words per page: 2,000 ÷ 275 = 7.27, so the text runs onto an 8th page. Single-spaced at 550: 2,000 ÷ 550 = 3.64, call it 4 pages. At 1.5 spacing the same essay runs 2,000 ÷ 410 = 4.88, so 5 pages, and handwritten at 175 words per page it sprawls across 2,000 ÷ 175 = 11.4, meaning 12 sheets of paper.

Flip it around when a professor sets a page minimum: five double-spaced pages × 275 = 1,375 words to aim for, 2,050 words at 1.5 spacing, or 2,750 single-spaced. The multiplication is trivial but rarely done, which is how people end up writing 2,200 words for a 5-page assignment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pages is 2,000 words?

About 7.3 pages double-spaced, which rounds up to 8 pages, or 3.6 pages (rounds to 4) single-spaced, using the standard 275 and 550 words-per-page conventions. At 1.5 spacing it lands near 5 pages, and handwritten on college-ruled paper expect 11-12 pages.

How many pages is 1,500 words?

5.5 pages double-spaced (round up to 6) or 2.7 pages single-spaced (rounds to 3). At 1.5 spacing, about 3.7 pages. A typical five-paragraph essay assignment at this length is really asking for 6 double-spaced pages.

Why is 275 words per page the standard?

Because it's what a double-spaced page holds at 12-point type with 1-inch margins on US Letter paper. Many essay services and publishers round it to 250-300; 275 sits at the middle of that band and is the convention most word-to-page converters use. Single-spaced pages hold double that, about 550 words.

How many words is a 5-page paper?

1,375 words double-spaced, 2,050 words at 1.5 spacing, or 2,750 words single-spaced. Professors almost always mean double-spaced when they set page counts, so 1,375 is the number to hit, give or take 10%.

Does font choice change the page count?

Yes, by 10-20%. A single-spaced page holds roughly 550 words in Arial 12, about 500 in Times New Roman 12 (a narrower face), and only about 450 in Verdana. Bumping to 14-point type drops a page to roughly 400 words; squeezing to 10-point pushes it toward 650. Spacing still matters more than font: double-spacing halves capacity at any type size.

How many handwritten pages is 1,000 words?

College-ruled notebook paper holds roughly 150-200 words per page, so 1,000 words runs 5-7 handwritten pages; the calculator uses 175 as the midpoint, giving 5.7 (rounds to 6). Hand size and spacing between lines swing this more than any printed convention.
