How the Therapy Dog Cost Calculator Works

Certifying a therapy dog is a short stack of fees: a handler course, an evaluation, registration, a vet wellness exam, and some gear. The calculator prices that stack for the organization you pick, then amortizes renewals and yearly vet visits across however long you plan to volunteer — because the honest cost of a therapy dog is a per-year number, not a one-time number.

The formula

First-year all-in = course + evaluation + registration + background check + vet exam + optional classes + gear. Ongoing cost per year = renewal fee ÷ renewal interval + one vet wellness exam + a little gear replacement. Multi-year total = first year + (years − 1) × ongoing.

How to use it

Pick your organization's preset — Pet Partners or the Alliance of Therapy Dogs — and every field fills with that organization's published fees. Adjust the vet exam to what your clinic charges and add obedience classes if your dog still needs polish. Everything stays editable if you're working with a regional org.

A worked example

Pet Partners, default numbers: $80 course + $25 evaluation + $95 registration + $0 background + $75 vet + $30 gear = $305 first-year all-in. Registration renews at $70 every two years, so amortized that's $35 a year, plus $75 for the annual vet exam and about $10 of gear wear — $120 a year after the first. Over five years: $305 + 4 × $120 = $785.

Run the Alliance of Therapy Dogs preset and the first year drops to $202 — no required course, but a $17 background check and yearly dues instead of biennial. Five years there: $682. Both paths sit inside the $100-$500 first-year band that a suitable, already-trained pet needs. The number balloons past that only when the dog itself needs work: group obedience runs $50-$300 a session, and that's where a "cheap" free dog stops being cheap.

Therapy Dog vs Service Dog: Different Jobs, Different Bills

A therapy dog visits many people — hospitals, schools, libraries, courtrooms — with a volunteer handler who owns the dog. A service dog works for one person with a disability, performing trained tasks, and has public-access rights under the ADA. Therapy teams visit only where they're invited. That difference drives the entire price gap: a program-trained service dog runs $20,000-$40,000 (our service dog cost calculator prices that path), while a therapy team is a weekend of paperwork and a few hundred dollars. Neither role requires any registry by law — if a site sells you "official ADA registration," keep your card in your wallet.

Costs here are national published fees and typical office-visit ranges, not a quote. Rules about where therapy dogs may visit vary by facility and state — this page is cost information only, not legal advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a therapy dog certification cost?

About $100 to $500 all-in with a national organization, assuming you already have a calm, well-mannered dog. Pet Partners runs roughly $190-$205 in fees ($80 handler course, $15-$30 evaluation, $95 registration) plus a vet wellness exam. The Alliance of Therapy Dogs runs $72-$92 ($20 one-time, $35 annual, ~$17 background check) plus the vet visit. Add gear and optional obedience polish and a typical first year lands near $300.

How do I register my dog as a therapy dog?

Pick a national organization, complete its handler course if required, pass a team evaluation with your dog, submit a vet wellness form, and pay the registration fee. Pet Partners registrations last two years; Alliance of Therapy Dogs memberships renew annually. Renewal means another evaluation or observation and an updated vet form.

Is a therapy dog the same as a service dog?

No, and the costs couldn't be more different. A therapy dog belongs to a volunteer handler and visits hospitals, schools, and care facilities by invitation. A service dog is individually trained to perform tasks for one person with a disability and costs $20,000-$40,000 from a program. Therapy dog teams have no public-access rights under the ADA — facilities welcome them by permission, not law.

Does insurance cover therapy dog registration?

No. Health insurance doesn't pay for therapy dog evaluation or registration, and Medicare doesn't either. What you do get is liability coverage while volunteering: Pet Partners registration and ATD's annual fee both include volunteer liability insurance for sanctioned visits, which is why the annual fee exists at all.

How long does therapy dog registration last?

Pet Partners registrations last two years and renew at $70 with a new evaluation and updated health screening. Alliance of Therapy Dogs memberships renew every year at $35. Budget for one vet wellness exam per year either way, since both organizations require current health documentation.

Can any dog become a therapy dog?

Breed matters less than temperament. National organizations evaluate each dog as an individual — they're looking for a calm disposition, tolerance of clumsy petting and loud equipment, and a handler who can read stress signals. A vetted adult dog with basic obedience is the usual starting point; puppies are rarely ready.
