How Much Does It Cost to Be a Wedding Guest? 2026 Averages

💡 8-15K searches/mo💰 CPC: $2-6⏱️ 7 min read

Everyone budgets for the couple. Nobody budgets for the guests — until the third save-the-date of the summer arrives. The average wedding guest spends $610 per wedding, and if the ceremony is a flight away, that number roughly triples to $1,680. Here's the honest line-by-line breakdown, plus the shortcuts that actually move the total.

What does the average wedding guest spend?

The Knot's Guest Study, surveying 1,000 US adults, puts the average at $610 per wedding. That single number hides a huge spread, because distance is the real driver. USA Today's 2025 analysis broke the tiers out:

ScenarioTypical spendBiggest lines
Local wedding~$270Gift, attire, gas or rideshare
Out of town, driving~$8401-2 hotel nights, fuel, gift
Out of town, flying~$1,680Airfare, 2-3 hotel nights, ground transport

Attend two out-of-town weddings and a local one in a single year, which is a normal wedding-season load for people in their late twenties, and you're past $2,500 without trying. What are guests actually paying for?

Five lines make up nearly every guest's bill:

  • The gift: $75-$150 is typical, with the average cash gift near $140. Close friends and family often give $150-$300, and coworkers or plus-ones can politely stay near $50. The plate-cost rule — your gift should roughly cover what your seat costs the couple — sets a sensible floor of $50-$150 at most receptions. Our wedding gift calculator handles the bands in detail.
  • Attire: $50-$200 for guests who own something close and just need shoes or a tie, up to $150-$400 when a new outfit is required. Renting formalwear runs $75-$200 — see our suit and tux rental breakdown below.
  • Travel: gas and parking for local and driving trips, airfare ($300-$600 domestic, booked 1-3 months out) for fly-ins.
  • Lodging: wedding-block rooms commonly run $120-$200 a night; guests typically need 1 night when driving and 2-3 when flying.
  • Pre-wedding events: a $50-$75 shower gift, plus a share of the bachelorette or bachelor weekend, which averages about $1,300 per traveling attendee. Our bachelorette party cost calculator shows the full split.

How much more does the wedding party pay?

Saying yes to the honor adds $500-$1,300 to the guest tab, and often more for destination roles. Bridesmaid dresses run $150-$400 (frequently plus $50-$100 of alterations), groomsman formalwear $150-$250 rented, professional hair and makeup $100-$200 when required, and the pre-wedding events stop being optional. Multiply by two or three weddings a year and being in the party can quietly cost more than a car payment — worth a frank conversation before accepting, though most attendants say the experience is worth it.

Does a destination wedding cost guests more?

Yes — figure 3-6x a local wedding. The $1,680 flying average assumes domestic airfare; resort destinations add multi-night minimums at peak-season rates. The mitigating factors: couples usually negotiate a group room rate (often 10-20% off), the trip doubles as a vacation, and etiquette relaxes the gift expectation — a $75-$100 thoughtful gift is entirely acceptable when you've spent $2,000 getting there. How can guests cut the bill without looking cheap?

  1. Split the room. Two guests sharing a $160 room each pay $80; an Airbnb split four ways can drop lodging under $50 a head. This is the single biggest lever.
  2. Book flights 1-3 months out and fly the morning of the wedding when you can, skipping a hotel night.
  3. Rent, don't buy, formalwear — $75-$200 for a suit or tux rental including fittings, versus $300-$800 to buy.
  4. Shop the registry early. Early buyers find the $40-$75 items still available; the last shoppers face the $200 place settings.
  5. Choose which pre-wedding events you attend. Skipping the bach weekend saves far more than shrinking the gift, and the couple notices the gift.
  6. Carpool to out-of-town weddings — three guests splitting a 3-hour drive pay $20-$30 each in fuel.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the average wedding guest spend?

The Knot's Guest Study puts the average at $610 per wedding. USA Today's 2025 analysis splits it further: about $270 for a local wedding, $840 when you drive out of town, and $1,680 when the wedding requires flights. Attending three weddings in a year can easily top $2,000.

What is the biggest cost for a wedding guest?

Travel and lodging, by far. Once a wedding is out of town, the hotel and getting there routinely run 2-3x the gift. A flying guest faces airfare plus 2-3 hotel nights at $120-$200 each before attire or the gift enter the picture.

How much should I spend on a wedding gift as a guest?

Most guests give $75-$150, with the average cash gift near $140. Etiquette guides suggest covering your plate — $50-$150 at most receptions — as a floor. Close friends and family commonly give $150-$300.

How much does being a bridesmaid or groomsman cost?

Plan on $500-$1,300 beyond a regular guest's costs: $150-$400 for role attire, $100-$200 for professional hair and makeup, a share of the bachelorette or bachelor weekend (about $1,300 per traveling attendee on average), and often a contribution to the shower.

How can I spend less on attending a wedding?

Split a room or Airbnb with other guests, book flights 1-3 months out, rent formalwear instead of buying, and buy from the registry early before only the pricey items remain. If the year stacks up several weddings, it's acceptable to skip optional pre-wedding events rather than trim the gift to nothing.
