How to Print a PDF at 24 x 36 (Arch D)

📄 6 min readUpdated November 5, 2026

If your PDF's already 1728 × 2592 points, print it at 100% and you're done. If it isn't, Fit will get you there: a Letter page enlarges to 282.4% and an 18 × 24 page to 133.3%. The catch is that any resize breaks a drawing's written scale. Here are the numbers, plus how Arch D differs from ANSI D.

What are the architectural paper sizes?

Print shops in the US use two engineering series, and they're easy to mix up. Architectural (Arch) sizes run in whole-number steps up to 24 × 36. ANSI sizes grow out of Letter. Both have a sheet called "D", and those aren't the same.

Is Arch D the same as ANSI D?

No. ANSI D is 22 × 34, two inches smaller each way. Say "D size" to a shop and you can get either, so give them the inches. Architects usually mean 24 × 36, while engineering drawings often use 22 × 34, though the habit varies from firm to firm.

The two don't fit cleanly. Sending ANSI D to Arch D at Fit gives 105.9%, with 0.71 inches left over, and our one-inch square measured 1.059 inches. Going the other way gives 91.7%. In both cases the scale written on the drawing has stopped being true.

What happens to a drawing's scale when you resize it?

The written scale doesn't change, but the ruler truth does. Say a plan drawn at 1/4 in = 1 ft on Arch D goes to a smaller sheet:

Half size is the one that works, and it's why 12 × 18 sets are popular. Arch E to Arch C and ANSI D to tabloid also come out at exactly 50%. Anything else should be marked "not to scale", since someone will measure it. A bar scale drawn on the sheet shrinks along with the drawing, so it stays correct where a written scale doesn't.

If you're printing true size, choose Actual Size or 100% and turn off Fit. Fit can shrink a full-size sheet by a few percent to clear the printer's margins, and that's enough to throw off a measurement. After it prints, check a known dimension with a ruler.

How do you get a PDF ready for a print shop or plotter?

  • Isolate the pages. Use Extract PDF Pages if you only need a few sheets from a set.
  • Fix the orientation. Plans are often landscape, and a page saved sideways is worth correcting with Rotate PDF first.
  • Check the images. As a rule of thumb, 150 PPI is fine at viewing distance, which is 3,600 × 5,400 pixels at this size. 300 PPI is 7,200 × 10,800. Vector linework stays sharp at any scale.
  • Ask about margins. Plotters often can't print to the edge of the sheet, and roll widths vary, so ask before you add a border.
  • Ask about bleed. A poster with color to the edge usually needs extra, commonly 1/8 inch a side, or 24.25 × 36.25 in total.
  • Watch the file size. If it's too big to email, Compress PDF can help, but it can soften images, so check the result.

We haven't confirmed pricing or stock at any particular shop. Ask for a quote on bond paper if it's a plan, and on poster paper if it isn't, since those are often priced differently.

Can you print 24 x 36 at home?

Not on one sheet, unless you've got a wide-format printer. You can tile it. Counting a quarter-inch printer margin and no overlap, 24 × 36 takes 12 Letter sheets, 15 A4 sheets, or 8 sheets of 11 × 17. You'll want some overlap for trimming, which can add more. Our guide on printing a poster across several pages walks through it, and tiling works best for layout checks. It won't be accurate enough to measure from once it's taped together.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size is Arch D paper?

Arch D is 24 × 36 inches, or about 610 × 914 mm. In a PDF, that's 1728 × 2592 points. It isn't the same as ANSI D, which is 22 × 34 inches.

How much do I enlarge a Letter PDF to get 24 x 36?

Use 282.4%. That fills the 24-inch width and leaves 4.94 inches unused along the long side, because Letter's shape is wider than 2:3. Text at 12 pt becomes about 33.9 pt.

Can I shrink a 24 x 36 PDF to 11 x 17?

Yes, at 45.8%, with half an inch left over. It won't be a clean scale, though: 1/4 in = 1 ft becomes about 0.115 in. For an exact half size, print on 12 × 18 at 50%.

Should I choose Fit or Actual Size?

Use Actual Size or 100% if the PDF is already 24 × 36 and anyone will measure it. Use Fit when you're changing paper size and exact scale doesn't matter, then mark the print not to scale.

Is 24 x 36 a good poster size?

Yes, it's a common poster size as well as a plan size. Ask the shop for poster stock and tell them if there's color to the edge, since you may need about 1/8 inch of bleed on each side.
