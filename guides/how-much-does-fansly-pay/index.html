How Much Does Fansly Pay?

⏱️ 6 min read

Fansly doesn't pay an hourly rate or a per-view rate the way ad-supported platforms do. Money arrives through subscriptions, tips, and pay-per-view messages, and the platform's cut is straightforward: you keep 80%, Fansly keeps 20%. That split is the whole headline. What actually decides your income is subscriber count, tier pricing, and how well your messaging converts — so here's the published payout math, the timing rules, and what realistic accounts earn at real sizes in 2026.

What percentage does Fansly take?

Fansly keeps 20% of everything a creator earns and pays out the remaining 80%. The cut covers payment processing, hosting, and platform operations, and it applies uniformly — subscriptions, tips, and PPV messages all pass through the same 80/20 door. That's the same headline split OnlyFans uses, which surprises people who assume one of the two must be cheaper to run on. The differences between the platforms are structural, not arithmetic, and we'll get to them below.

One number to hold onto: for every $100 fans spend on your page, $80 reaches your payout account before taxes. If a price looks tempting at full retail, run it at 80% first — a $9.99 tier is really a $7.99 tier in your pocket, and the Fansly earnings calculator applies the split for you across blended tiers, tips, and PPV.

When does Fansly pay out?

Payout mechanics shape cash flow more than most creators expect. Every transaction sits in pending status for exactly seven days before it becomes available. Once you request a payout, Fansly processes on a regular cycle — historically Tuesdays and Fridays for standard methods, with international transfers and crypto handled every three days — and delivery takes another one to three business days after that. End to end, plan on roughly two weeks between a fan paying and money landing in your account, and plan your first month's income as arriving late.

How do you increase your Fansly income?

Four levers, in order of impact:

  • Tier design. A cheap entry tier with genuinely decent content, a mainstream middle tier carrying most subscribers, and a premium tier anchoring the middle. Tiers that differ only in price teach fans not to upgrade — differentiate with structure, access, and customs priority.
  • PPV and tips attach rate. Engaged audiences push tips and PPV well past 30% of gross. If nearly all your income is subscriptions, your messaging channel is underused.
  • The discovery feed. Free-tier accounts with tip-gated content give the feed something to surface. Feed traffic converts at lower price points, but it's traffic you didn't buy.
  • Multi-platform reach. An audience that follows you to a second platform survives any single ban or policy shift. Mirror content deliberately rather than frantically.

Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage does Fansly take?

Fansly keeps 20% of creator earnings and pays out 80%, the same split as OnlyFans. The 20% covers payment processing, hosting, and platform operations, and it applies to subscriptions, tips, and pay-per-view messages alike.

What is the minimum payout on Fansly?

It depends on the payout method: $20 for Paxum, Cosmopayment, and US bank deposit, $50 for SEPA transfers in euros, and $100 for international wire and crypto. Earnings under the minimum roll over until you cross the threshold.

How long do Fansly payouts take?

Every transaction sits in pending status for seven days first. Once funds clear and a payout is requested, Fansly processes on a regular cycle (historically Tuesdays and Fridays, with international and crypto payouts every three days), and delivery then takes one to three business days. Plan on roughly two weeks between a fan paying and cash in your account.

How much can you make on Fansly in a month?

At the 80% split, 100 subscribers at $9.99 plus $400 a month in tips and PPV nets about $1,119. A 500-subscriber account at a $15 blend nets $6,000. Most new accounts earn far less — creator-reported figures of tens of dollars a month are common in year one, because income tracks audience size and marketing effort, not time on the platform.

Does Fansly pay more than OnlyFans?

The headline split is identical at 80/20. The real differences are structural: Fansly supports multiple subscription tiers plus free-tier accounts with tip-gated content, and it runs an on-site discovery feed, while OnlyFans uses a single subscription price and relies almost entirely on off-platform traffic. Many established creators run both.
