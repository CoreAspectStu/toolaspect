How Much Do Gutters Cost?

⏱️ 6 min read

Gutters run $6 to $15 per linear foot installed for seamless aluminum — the material more than 80% of homeowners pick — and a typical house needs 150 to 200 linear feet plus 4 to 6 downspouts, which lands most full installs between $1,000 and $3,600. Vinyl is cheaper, copper is a different tax bracket, and guards can quietly double the bill. Here's how the pieces price out and how to count what your roof actually needs.

Aluminum dominates for boring reasons: it doesn't rust, it's light enough for one-person installation, and it takes paint. Steel earns its premium where hail or ladders will hit it. Copper is a 50-plus-year statement — you're buying curb appeal for a $2,200 to $6,000 bill on a 150-foot house, not better rain capture.

What does a whole house cost?

Apply those rates to real footprints. A gable roof carries gutter on the two eave sides; a hip roof drains on all four, so it needs roughly 75% more footage on the same footprint. Using the calculator's own math on a 40×30 house:

Seamless or sectional: which costs less?

Sectional gutters — 10-foot lengths joined with connectors — are the DIY route and the cheap route: a determined homeowner can hang vinyl sections from a ladder for a few hundred dollars in materials. Seamless gutters are roll-formed on a truck to your exact run lengths, so a 60-foot eave is one piece with joints only at corners and downspouts. Fewer joints mean fewer leaks, which is why seamless is what installers sell and why it costs more. The linear footage the calculator spits out is exactly what a seamless crew will quote from, waste factor included.

Run the arithmetic before saying yes at the quote table: a 180-foot house at $18 per foot for gutters ($3,240) plus micro-mesh at $15 ($2,700) is a $5,940 project — the guards cost more than half the gutters. Guards also don't eliminate maintenance; they stretch it. Fine debris still silts through, and someone still has to get up there eventually.

How many downspouts and hangers do you need?

Two rules of thumb cover it. Downspouts: one standard 2×3 or 3×4 unit per 600 square feet of roof area, measured on the sloped surface, not the footprint — a steeper roof has more actual area than the flat rectangle below it. Oversized 3×4 downspouts handle 800-1,000 square feet each and clog less, which makes them the standard upgrade. Hangers: every 24 to 36 inches per manufacturer standards, tightened to 24 inches in snow country where ice loads the run.

Downspouts are also where the water story ends or doesn't. Discharge should land 4 to 6 feet from the foundation — splash blocks minimum, buried drain pipe better — or the gutters you just paid for dump their load straight against the basement wall. That's a $30 fix at install time and an excavation problem later.

What drives the price past the quote?

Height and access mostly. A third story, steep lawns that need stabilizers, or a house boxed by landscaping adds labor time, and labor is half to two-thirds of an install quote. Complexity charges too: inside corners, wrap-around porches, and short runs on hips and valleys multiply the joints that slow a crew down. If your roof work is part of a bigger project — full re-roof, new siding — bundle the gutter bid into it; crews on site already are cheaper than crews mobilized.

The bottom line

Budget $6-$15 per linear foot installed for seamless aluminum and $1,000-$3,600 for a typical 150-200 foot house; vinyl shaves a little, steel and copper multiply it, and guards add $1-$15 per foot on top. Frequently Asked Questions

How much do gutters cost for a whole house?

Most homes need 150-200 linear feet of gutter plus 4-6 downspouts, and standard aluminum or vinyl installs land between $1,000 and $3,600 total. Premium materials push higher — a 150-foot copper job runs roughly $2,200-$6,000.

What is the cheapest gutter material?

Vinyl, at $6-$11 per linear foot installed ($2-$5 in materials). Aluminum — $6-$15 installed — costs only slightly more, resists rust, and is what over 80% of homeowners choose, so the savings on vinyl are real but thin.

How much do gutter guards cost to add?

Basic vinyl mesh screens run $1-$4 per linear foot, while professional systems run $7-$15 installed and micro-mesh tops $15 per foot. On a 180-foot house, guards can double a basic seamless-aluminum bill, so price them as their own line item, not an afterthought.

How many downspouts does a house need?

The industry rule of thumb is one standard 2x3 or 3x4 downspout per 600 square feet of roof area. Oversized 3x4 downspouts can serve 800-1,000 square feet each and clog less, which is why they are the usual upgrade on new installs.

Is it cheaper to install gutters yourself?

Sectional vinyl or aluminum gutters are a realistic DIY job — materials at $2-$5 per foot mean a 150-foot run costs a few hundred dollars. Seamless gutters cannot be DIY'd; the stock is roll-formed on a truck to your exact lengths, and that equipment is why seamless installs cost more but leak less.
