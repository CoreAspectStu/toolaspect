Does SSI Count USDA Rural Housing? How Section 515 Rent Works

📄 6 min readUpdated November 6, 2026

It doesn't count. SSA excludes USDA rural housing help from income and resources, so your 2026 check can stay at $994. What SSI does decide is your rent. In a Section 515 unit with rental assistance, you'd pay about $284 to $288 a month in 2026 and about $294 in 2027. Here's the math, from the rules we read.

Does USDA rental assistance count as income for SSI?

No. SSA's operations manual, at SI 00830.630, excludes the value of housing assistance paid under title V of the Housing Act of 1949. That's the law behind USDA's Section 515 apartments, the Section 521 rental assistance that goes with many of them, and Section 514/516 farm labor housing. The manual goes further. It tells staff to assume any help in which USDA's Rural Housing Service is involved is excluded, and to accept your word on it unless they have reason to doubt you.

So moving into a USDA rural apartment shouldn't shrink your check. It stays at $994 for 2026 and goes to $1,029 in 2027. What the move changes is your rent, and SSI is the income the owner uses to set it.

How does USDA figure the rent for someone on SSI?

Under 7 CFR 3560.203, the owner sets your share at the highest of four figures, and never above the unit's note rent:

  • 30% of your monthly adjusted income
  • 10% of your gross monthly income
  • the part of a public assistance payment set aside for shelter, if you get one
  • the basic rent, unless USDA rental assistance covers your household

Since April 13, 2026, USDA's rule ties annual income to HUD's definition in 24 CFR 5.609, and adjusted income was already tied to 24 CFR 5.611. That gives you HUD's deductions. A household whose head, co-head or spouse is 62 or older, or has a disability, gets a flat deduction, and medical costs count once they pass 10% of annual income. Your SSI is counted in full, as is any state supplement.

What if the unit doesn't have rental assistance?

Then the basic rent is your floor, and it's often far more than 30% of an SSI check. In our example you'd pay $640, or 62% of a 2027 check. Not every unit in a Section 515 building has RA. Ask the manager how many RA units there are and how long the waiting list is, because that one fact can be worth $346 a month in our example.

If you pay utilities yourself, the property uses a utility allowance. Shelter cost is the rent plus that allowance, and with RA your contribution is measured against that total, so the allowance comes off what you hand the owner. Your tenant certification form will show the split.

What's overage, and does it affect you?

Overage is what a tenant pays above basic rent, up to the note rent, and the owner sends it to USDA. It only happens when 30% of your adjusted income beats the basic rent. In the last row, $2,400 a month of SSDI gives a contribution of $706, so that tenant pays $66 above basic rent, and the cap is the $860 note rent. Can SSA cut your check over low rent?

It shouldn't, but here's the rule it should be setting aside. Under 20 CFR 416.1130(b), when you rent from someone, a rent at or above the presumed maximum value is a business arrangement and nothing is counted. That value is $351.33 a month for 2026 and $363 for 2027. Pay less, and SSA can count the gap as in-kind support. At a $284 rent, that gap is about $67.

The housing exclusion is meant to stop that from happening to you. So if your first check after a move comes in roughly $67 short, tell SSA your unit is a USDA Rural Development property with rental assistance, name SI 00830.630, and bring your lease and rent notice.

When does your rent change?

At least once a year, when you recertify. Under 3560.152(e), you also have to recertify when your monthly income changes by $100 or more, and you can ask for it when it drops by $50. The 2027 COLA adds $35 to SSI, so it'll normally show up at your annual review, raising rent by about $10. The owner can also raise rents across the property, but only with USDA approval. Under 3560.205, you get 20 days to comment on the request and at least 30 days' notice once it's approved.

Some things we didn't read for this guide: USDA's handbook for owners and the Section 538 guaranteed program. If that's you, ask the manager how your share is figured. Frequently Asked Questions

Does Section 515 housing count against SSI?

No. SSA excludes housing assistance paid under title V of the 1949 Housing Act, which covers Section 515 and Section 521 rental assistance. Its manual tells staff to assume any help involving USDA's Rural Housing Service is excluded.

How much rent will I pay in a USDA apartment on SSI?

With rental assistance, about 30% of your adjusted income: roughly $284 to $288 a month on $994 in 2026, and about $294 on $1,029 in 2027. Without it, you pay at least the unit's basic rent.

What is the difference between basic rent and note rent?

Basic rent covers the property's budget with USDA's interest credit applied. Note rent covers it at the loan's full interest rate. Your share can't go above note rent, and anything you pay over basic rent is overage.

Do I have to report the SSI COLA to my USDA landlord?

You must recertify when your monthly income changes by $100 or more. The 2027 SSI increase is $35, so it's normally handled at your annual recertification. Report it anyway if the owner asks.

Does a USDA rural voucher count as income for SSI?

SSA's manual says to assume any housing help involving the Rural Housing Service is excluded, and the voucher program sits under the same 1949 law. Tell SSA it's a USDA voucher and keep the paperwork.
