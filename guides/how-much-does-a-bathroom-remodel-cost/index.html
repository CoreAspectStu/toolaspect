How Much Does a Bathroom Remodel Cost in 2026?

💡 ~40K searches/mo💰 CPC: $20⏱️ 8 min read

Bathrooms are the priciest room in the house per square foot. A cosmetic refresh runs about $160 per sq ft ($6,400 for a 40 sq ft bath), a midrange gut remodel about $700 per sq ft ($28,000), and upscale spa work $900 and up. Here's what drives the difference and where budgets quietly fail.

Why is the wet area so expensive?

The tub or shower zone can consume a third of the budget by itself. A custom tile shower adds $3,000 to $8,000 over a standard insert, and every dollar of it hides behind waterproofing, slope, and labor: cement board or foam board, a sealed pan or linear drain, months-of-moisture membrane systems, then tile set and grouted on curved and vertical surfaces.

The trades stack on top. Plumber, electrician, HVAC (that exhaust fan has to vent outside), tile setter, painter, and glass installer each mobilize for a day or two in a room you can't use while they wait for each other. Labor typically claims about half of a bathroom budget, more than in any other room.

Tub-to-shower conversion: worth it?

Converting a tub to a walk-in shower runs $2,500 to $6,000, with most near $4,000 for a tiled unit with a glass panel. It's one of the most popular bathroom projects in the country, and the value math depends on one question: does another tub remain in the house?

Families with young kids (and many buyers) want at least one tub. Keep or add a tub somewhere, and converting the primary bath's tub to a shower is usually a value-neutral or value-positive move. Remove the last tub and you've narrowed your future buyer pool.

What moves a bathroom budget the most?

  • Moving fixtures, +8%. Relocating the toilet means a new drain through the floor structure; it's the single most common scope creep.
  • Custom tile showers, +$3,000–$8,000. Niche, bench, and curbless pan details add labor hours fast.
  • Double vanities, +$1,200–$3,500, plus a second sink's plumbing.
  • Heated floors, +$800–$2,000, cheap at install time, expensive to retrofit.
  • Hidden water damage. A rotten subfloor around the toilet flange or wet drywall behind tile isn't visible until demo day; keep 10% to 15% contingency.

How do you remodel a bathroom on a budget?

  1. Keep every fixture where it stands. The drain is the budget.
  2. Reglaze instead of replace. Refinishing a cast iron tub costs $300 to $1,000 versus $1,500 and up to replace and re-tile.
  3. Use large-format tile. Fewer grout lines means fewer setter hours; price your exact layout with our tile calculator before shopping.
  4. Buy the vanity off the shelf and spend the difference on the shower, where the money shows.
  5. Keep the exhaust fan location and swap the unit, not the duct run.

Done well, a refresh plus a reglaze plus lighting can read as a remodel for a quarter of the gut price. Our flooring calculator and paint calculator cover the two DIY-friendliest lines if you're doing part of the work yourself.

How long does it take, and do you need a permit?

Plan on about a week for a refresh, 3 to 6 weeks for a midrange gut, and 6 to 12 weeks for upscale work with custom glass and slabs. Permits are usually required for plumbing, electrical, and ventilation changes and typically cost $100 to $1,000; pull them properly, because unpermitted plumbing turns up in every resale inspection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I remodel a bathroom for $10,000?

Yes, with discipline. A $10,000 refresh keeps the tub, tile, and layout while swapping the vanity, toilet, lighting, mirror, hardware, and paint, and possibly regrouting or reglazing instead of replacing tile. You leave about $1,000 to $2,000 as contingency and skip moving anything plumbed.

Why do bathrooms cost more per square foot than kitchens?

Waterproofing and tile. Every wet surface needs a waterproofing system behind it, tile is pure skilled labor, and plumbing, electrical, and ventilation trades all work in sequence in the smallest room of the house. Labor typically takes about half of a bathroom budget.

What is the return on a bathroom remodel?

A midrange bathroom remodel recoups roughly 60% to 70% of cost at resale per Remodeling's Cost vs. Value Report, which beats major kitchen remodels. Refreshes and midrange work return more, percentage-wise, than upscale remodels.
