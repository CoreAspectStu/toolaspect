How to Calculate Your Mortgage Payment

💡 550K searches/mo💰 CPC: $8-15⏱️ 5 min read

Understanding your mortgage payment is the first step in homebuying. Your monthly payment includes principal, interest, taxes, and insurance (PITI).

Advertisement

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Know your loan amount (home price minus down payment)
  2. Determine your annual interest rate and divide by 12
  3. Use the formula: M = P [ r(1+r)^n ] / [ (1+r)^n - 1 ]
  4. Add property taxes and insurance for your total payment
  5. Each month, more goes to principal and less to interest (amortization)
