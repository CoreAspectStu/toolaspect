SSI and the USDA Rural Development Voucher: Does It Count?

📄 6 min readUpdated November 11, 2026

A USDA Rural Development voucher doesn't count as income or a resource for SSI, so your check stays where it is. The catch is on the rent side. Under the rules USDA has published, the voucher is a fixed dollar amount, so each rent increase comes out of your pocket. A law signed in July 2026 is meant to change that.

Does a USDA Rural Development voucher count as income for SSI?

No. We reread SSA's manual section SI 00830.630 today. It excludes from income and from resources the value of any assistance paid with respect to a dwelling unit under title V of the Housing Act of 1949. The voucher is Section 542 of that title, 42 U.S.C. 1490r. Staff are told to assume any help involving USDA's Rural Housing Service is excluded.

The money never reaches you anyway. USDA signs a housing assistance payments contract with the owner and pays the owner each month, so nothing piles up in your account. If you're near the $2,000 resource limit, the net worth calculator adds things up.

Without that exclusion, 20 CFR 416.1130(b) would have SSA compare your rent with the presumed maximum value, $351.33 for 2026 and $363 for 2027. So if your check drops after the voucher starts, tell SSA it's a USDA Rural Development voucher, name SI 00830.630, and bring the voucher letter and your lease.

Who gets a Rural Development voucher?

You can't apply off the street. It's offered to tenants already living in a USDA-financed building when it leaves the program. Our guide to SSI in USDA Section 515 housing covers life before that day. Two sets of rules matter right now.

How is the voucher amount set, and does it change?

Under the older formula, USDA finds a comparable market rent for your unit and subtracts what you were paying on the day of the prepayment or foreclosure. That difference is your voucher. It doesn't grow if you move somewhere pricier, and the notice adds that "since inception of the program, the amount of the Voucher has not experienced an increase."

In Section 8, your share follows your income. With a fixed voucher, every rent increase lands on you. Here's an example with made-up rents: an $800 market rent on the trigger date, 4% increases after that, and a tenant who was paying 30% of a $994 SSI check. That makes the voucher $502. What does the July 2026 law change for someone on SSI?

Section 502(m) says that, notwithstanding any other law, the monthly payment is to be figured the way Section 542(a) describes: a payment standard based on the area's fair market rent, minus 30% of your monthly adjusted income. The payment can't be more than your rent, utilities included, minus 10% of your gross income.

We ran that for a 2027 check of $1,029 with an example payment standard of $850, using 30% of the full check, $308.70. Deductions would lower that a little.

Can you take the voucher somewhere else?

Yes. You can use it where you live now or at any rental in the United States and its territories, if the unit passes a USDA inspection and the owner accepts it. Tell USDA before any move.

You can't stack it. It doesn't work in Section 8 or public housing units, and it can't be combined with USDA rental assistance. Give up the voucher for a rental assistance unit and, under the notice, you can't get another. On SSI that trade can still be worth it, because rental assistance follows your income. Frequently Asked Questions

Does a USDA Rural Development voucher reduce SSI?

No. SSA excludes housing assistance paid under title V of the Housing Act of 1949 from income and resources, and the voucher is Section 542 of that title. USDA pays the owner directly.

How much is a Rural Development voucher?

Under the 2017 notice, it's the comparable market rent minus what you paid on the day of the prepayment or foreclosure. A July 2026 law points to an income-based formula instead.

Does the voucher go up when the rent goes up?

Under the 2017 notice, no. The July 2026 law requires USDA to set up annual and interim reviews that can adjust it, and gives the agency two years to write the rules.

How long do I have to accept a Rural Development voucher?

The 2017 notice gives you 10 months from the prepayment or foreclosure to return the request form and citizenship declaration. Miss that and the offer is gone, with no appeal.

Can I use a Rural Development voucher with Section 8?

No. It can't be used in Section 8 or public housing units, or together with USDA rental assistance.
