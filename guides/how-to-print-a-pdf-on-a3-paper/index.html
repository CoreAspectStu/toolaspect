How to Print a PDF on A3 Paper

📄 6 min readUpdated November 3, 2026

Choose A3 as the paper size, then pick Actual size for an A3 file or Fit for anything smaller. An A4 page enlarges to exactly 141.4% and fills the sheet, while a Letter page stops at 137.6% and leaves a blank band. Here's where the settings live and what scaling does to your text.

Is your PDF actually A3?

Check first, because the fix depends on the answer. Open the document properties (Ctrl+D in Acrobat, Tools > Show Inspector in Preview) and read the page size. Page sizes are stored in points, 72 to the inch, so viewers may show either unit:

A3 is exactly twice the area of A4. That's the whole logic of the A series: fold a sheet in half across its long side and you get the next size down, with the same shape. It's why A sizes scale so cleanly into each other and why US sizes don't.

How do you print a PDF on A3 paper?

  1. Load A3 paper. Many printers take it only from a bypass tray or a specific cassette.
  2. In the print dialog, set the paper size to A3. It's often hidden behind "More settings" or Page Setup.
  3. Pick Actual size for a true A3 file, or Fit to enlarge something smaller.
  4. Match the orientation to the page, then check the preview before you commit a sheet.

Here's where the setting usually lives. This list comes from each app's menus, not from a printing test on every version, so labels may differ:

If A3 isn't listed, your driver doesn't offer it, and no PDF app can add it. Most home printers stop at A4 or Legal. Office copiers and wide-format inkjets usually handle it. Otherwise, a print shop will run it from a single PDF.

If an exact scale matters, as it does for a drawing, set 100% and accept the clipped edge, or use a printer rated for borderless output, and then measure a known dimension on the sheet.

Will an A4 PDF look blurry on A3?

Text and vector graphics won't. They're stored as shapes and redraw sharply at any size. Scanned pages and photos are made of pixels, so enlarging spreads them out by the same 141.4%:

The "what to expect" notes are a rule of thumb rather than a measurement, and viewing distance changes how much it matters. A scanned form at 150 ppi will look soft. If that's what you've got, rescan it if you can.

Why doesn't a US-size PDF fill A3?

Letter is a different shape: 1.29 times as tall as it is wide, against 1.41 for A3. Fit stops at the width, so Letter on A3 leaves about 36 mm blank across the page. Legal goes the other way and leaves 42 mm at the sides. To fill the sheet you'd have to cut something off, and our PDF crop tool lets you choose what goes.

A3 and 11 × 17 trip people up because they look alike but aren't. A3 is wider and shorter, so you lose a little either way. If a US print shop only stocks Tabloid, A3 fits at 94.1%, or have them trim. Our 11 × 17 guide covers that size in more depth.

Can you print an A3 PDF without an A3 printer?

Yes, in three ways, and only the first keeps it all on one sheet:

  • Shrink it. A3 on A4 is 70.7%, which turns 12 pt into 8.5 pt. That's readable for a form and tight for a dense plan.
  • Tile it. Poster or Tile mode splits the page across several sheets that you trim and tape. A3 is exactly two A4 sheets, plus some overlap because of the margins. See printing a poster on multiple pages.
  • Print part of it. Crop to the area you need, or use Extract PDF Pages to send only the big sheets to a shop.

The reverse trick is handy too. Two A4 pages fit side by side on landscape A3 at exactly 100%, so "2 pages per sheet" gives you a true-size spread, which is the basis of booklet printing. Two Letter pages come out at 97.3%.

If pages print sideways, the file's rotation may be wrong rather than your settings. Rotate PDF fixes it permanently. And if one file has a mix of sizes, this guide explains why and what to do about it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I print A4 to A3?

Set the paper size to A3 in the print dialog and choose Fit. A4 enlarges to 141.4% and fills the whole sheet because the two sizes have the same shape, so 12 pt text becomes 17 pt. Printer margins may bring that down to about 137%.

Why is A3 not showing in my print options?

Your printer driver doesn't offer it, usually because the printer can't feed paper that large. PDF apps only list the sizes the driver reports. Check that the right printer is selected, since a copier and a home inkjet on the same network list different sizes.

Is A3 bigger than 11x17?

Neither is simply bigger. A3 is 297 by 420 mm and 11 by 17 is 279.4 by 431.8 mm, so A3 is 17.6 mm wider and 11.8 mm shorter. A3 printed on 11 by 17 scales to 94.1%, and 11 by 17 printed on A3 scales to 97.3%.

What scale should I use to print A3 on A4?

70.7%, or just choose Fit. A3 and A4 have the same shape, so it fills the page apart from the printer margins. Text at 12 pt becomes about 8.5 pt, so check small labels on a test page.

How many A4 pages fit on A3?

Two, side by side on a landscape A3 sheet, at exactly 100%. A3 is 420 mm long and each A4 page is 210 mm wide. Some printer margin still applies, so anything right at the edge of a page may be clipped.
