How Much Does a Honeymoon Actually Cost?

💍 Post-wedding finances⏱️ 8 min read🔢 Verified line-item math

Every wedding budget spreadsheet ends with the same line nobody has researched: "honeymoon, TBD." Industry surveys commonly put the average US honeymoon around $5,000 for two, but that average is doing a lot of quiet work, averaging together $1,800 road trips and $18,000 overwater bungalows. Here's what each honeymoon tier really costs, a complete mid-tier Caribbean build you can check line by line, and the registry fee trap that shrinks the "gift money" line before you ever see it.

Ranges assume airfare is in the total and reflect published travel-industry pricing bands, not any single quote. The honest takeaway: the destination name matters less than how far you fly and what you sleep in. A $600 flight difference for two is a full extra night at a nice Caribbean resort.

The buffer: why 10% is the floor

Tips at all-inclusives, checked bags, resort fees, one rain-day spa visit, the taxi you take because the shuttle never shows. Individually small, collectively the reason couples come home to a credit-card surprise. Ten percent of the subtotal is the standard cushion: $403 on the build above. Go 15% when you're headed somewhere with aggressive excursion selling, or when the itinerary has multiple flights, since each connection is another chance for a rebooking fee.

The registry line is smaller than you think

Honeymoon registries are legitimately useful, and guests genuinely like funding a snorkel day more than buying a third blender. But the fund total isn't the payout. Platform fees run from effectively 0% (offline gifts, or platforms with free payout rails) to 9.65% at the top of the market. On a $5,000 fund, that spread is $482.50: you either receive the full $5,000 or $4,517.50 depending on which platform and payment method you chose. Four ways couples blow the budget

  • Booking the flights last. Airfare is the least flexible line; a 3-month-late booking can add $200 a seat, which erases the buffer entirely.
  • Counting the registry at face value. Budget the net after fees, or you've pre-spent $200–$480 that isn't coming.
  • Ignoring travel days. Day 1 and the last day are half-days with full food costs. Count nights + 1 days of meals.
  • Upgrading at check-in. The $150-a-night ocean-view upsell at the desk feels small and costs $1,050 over a week. Decide view tiers before you fly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the average honeymoon cost?

Industry surveys commonly put the average US honeymoon around $5,000 for roughly 7 nights for two. That average hides a huge spread: road-trip honeymoons come in under $2,000, Mexico and the Caribbean run $3,500 to $6,500, and South Pacific trips pass $10,000. Flights and hotel tier drive more of the total than the destination itself.

What is a realistic honeymoon budget for 7 nights in the Caribbean?

About $4,400 for two at mid-tier resorts: $850 of airfare ($425 each), $2,030 of lodging ($290 × 7 nights), $600 of food ($75 × 8 days), $420 of activities, $130 of ground transport, and a 10% buffer of $403. That lands at $4,433 total, or $2,216.50 per person.

When should we book our honeymoon?

Flights 3 to 6 months out for long-haul routes and 1 to 3 months for domestic and Caribbean hops, which is when fares sit near their lows. Resorts and packages can be booked earlier, especially for peak season. Booking the room refundable and the flights when the fare looks fair is the pragmatic split.

Do honeymoon registries actually save the trip budget?

They can offset a real chunk, but not at face value. Registry platforms charge fees ranging from 0% to about 9.65% per gift, so a $5,000 fund pays out anywhere from $4,517.50 to the full $5,000 depending on the platform and payment method. Compare the fee schedules before you pick one.
