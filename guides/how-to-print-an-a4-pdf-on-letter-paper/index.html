How to Print an A4 PDF on Letter Paper (Without Cutting It Off)

📄 6 min readUpdated October 21, 2026

If the top or bottom of a PDF goes missing when you print it, the file is almost certainly A4 and your printer is loaded with US Letter. The fix takes one setting, but it's worth knowing what it does to your text and when printing at actual size is the better call.

Why does an A4 PDF get cut off on Letter paper?

Because the two sizes don't nest. A4 is 6 mm narrower than Letter but 17.6 mm taller. Print an A4 page at actual size on Letter and the width fits with room to spare, but the extra height has nowhere to go. Centered, that's 8.8 mm lost off the top and 8.8 mm off the bottom. Then your printer can't reach the last few millimeters of the sheet anyway, usually about a quarter inch (6.35 mm), so in practice anything within roughly 15 mm of the A4 page's top or bottom edge can disappear.

Going the other way, Letter on A4, the problem flips: it's the sides that get clipped, by about 2.9 mm each.

What's the quickest fix?

Turn on scaling in the print dialog. Nearly every PDF viewer has it, under slightly different names:

  • Adobe Acrobat and Reader: Page Sizing & Handling, then Fit or Shrink oversized pages.
  • Chrome and Edge: More settings, then Scale, then Fit to printable area.
  • Mac Preview: Scale to Fit, with Print Entire Image selected.

Also check that the paper size in the dialog says Letter. If it says A4, some printers stop and ask for A4 paper, or print the page as if the sheet were taller. In Acrobat, untick "Choose paper source by PDF page size" if the printer keeps asking for paper you don't have.

How much smaller does scaling make the text?

Less than people expect. Here's what each combination does:

A4 on Letter, Fit or Shrink oversized pages94.1% (about 89.8% with 0.25 in printer margins)Nothing lost; 11 pt text prints at about 10 pt

Letter on A4, Fit97.3% (about 91.4% with margins)Nothing lost; extra white space at top and bottom

Shrinking A4 to fit Letter costs about 6% on paper math, or 10% once the printer's own margins are taken into account. Body text at 11 pt comes out at roughly 10 pt, which most people won't notice. Legal on Letter is the one that really hurts.

When is actual size the better choice?

When the document has normal margins. A typical A4 document with 25 mm margins has a text area of about 160 × 247 mm, which fits on Letter at 100% with about 16 mm to spare top and bottom. Print it at actual size and the text stays its real size, and you lose nothing but white space. Forms are the other case: a form designed to be filled in by hand, or measured, should print at 100% so the boxes stay the size they were meant to be.

It goes wrong with headers, footers and page numbers set close to the edge, or with designs that bleed to the edge, like flyers and certificates. Check page 1 in the print preview before you print fifty copies.

Can you make the page itself fit better?

Yes, two ways. If the PDF has wide empty margins, crop them off first. Our Crop PDF tool trims the white space around the content on every page, in your browser. A cropped page scaled to fit prints larger than a full A4 page scaled to fit, because none of the shrinking is spent on blank paper. The cropping guide explains the page boxes it changes.

If you made the file, re-export it at the right size. The Word to PDF converter and the HTML to PDF converter both let you pick Letter or A4 before you convert. Reflowing the content onto the right paper always beats squeezing it afterwards.

How common is this problem?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I print an A4 PDF on Letter paper?

Open the print dialog, set the paper size to Letter, and choose Fit, Shrink oversized pages or Fit to printable area. The page shrinks to about 94% so nothing is cut off. If the document has normal margins, Actual size also works and keeps the text full size.

Why is the bottom of my PDF cut off when I print?

The PDF is probably A4 and you're printing on Letter at actual size. A4 is 17.6 mm taller, so the top and bottom get cut off. Turn on Fit or Shrink oversized pages in the print settings.

Does shrinking A4 to Letter make text hard to read?

Rarely. Fitting A4 on Letter scales the page to about 90 to 94%, so 11 pt text prints at roughly 10 pt. Shrinking Legal to Letter is a bigger drop, to around 75 to 79%.

What is the difference between A4 and Letter?

A4 is 210 × 297 mm and US Letter is 8.5 × 11 inches, or 215.9 × 279.4 mm. A4 is about 6 mm narrower and 17.6 mm taller. A4 is the standard almost everywhere outside the US, Canada and parts of Latin America.

Can I change a PDF from A4 to Letter?

You can rescale it, but the cleanest way is to re-export the original document at Letter size so the text reflows. For a PDF you can't re-export, print with Fit, or crop the margins first so the scaled page loses less.
