How to Unscramble Words (Tricks and Examples)

⏱️ 6 min readUpdated September 26, 2026

To unscramble a word, stop staring at the whole jumble. Split the letters into vowels and consonants, pull out a common ending like -ING or -ED, and build a short word from what's left. Seven different letters can be arranged 5,040 ways, so random guessing is hopeless. A few patterns cut that down to a handful of sensible tries.

How do you unscramble words by hand?

Seeing the answer in one go works for four or five letters. At seven, it falls apart. Break the problem into pieces you already know how to solve:

  1. Write the vowels on one line and the consonants on another, so you can see what shape the word might take.
  2. Look for an ending. If you have I, N and G, set them aside. Same for E and D, E and R, or a spare S.
  3. Make a short word from the leftovers, then stick the ending back on.
  4. If nothing clicks, try a different ending or a prefix like RE- or UN-.

It helps to change how the letters look, too. Write them in a circle instead of a row so your eye isn't locked into the scrambled order. Scrabble players shuffle their racks for the same reason, and a fresh layout often shows a pair like TH or ST that was hiding in plain sight.

What does a worked example look like?

Take the jumble RGNIDAE. The vowels are A, E and I, and the consonants are R, G, N and D. An I, an N and a G together is a strong hint for -ING.

Pull those out and you're left with R, D, A and E, which spell READ, DARE and DEAR. Only one keeps all seven letters when you add -ING: READING. Now try the other obvious ending. Pull out E and D, which leaves R, G, N, I and A. That spells GRAIN, so GRAIN + ED gives GRAINED. Two endings, two answers from the same seven letters.

We checked this jumble against the ENABLE word list (172,823 words, a standard list for word-game software). The letters make exactly four seven-letter words: READING, GRAINED, GRADINE and DERAIGN. They also give 18 six-letter words, including GARDEN, DANGER, GAINED and REGAIN, and 157 words in total from two to seven letters.

How many ways can letters be arranged?

For letters that are all different, the number of orders is the factorial of the letter count. Four letters give 4 x 3 x 2 x 1 = 24. Each extra letter multiplies the total again, which is why jumbles get hard so fast.

Why do repeated letters matter?

Swapping two identical letters doesn't make a new word, so repeats shrink the count. Divide the factorial by the factorial of each repeat. LETTER has two Es and two Ts, so it has 720 / (2 x 2) = 180 distinct arrangements, not 720.

A spare S is the easiest win: solve the word without it, then add it back as a plural. After that, look for -ING and -ED, then TH, CH, SH and ST. If there's a Q, put a U right after it. Of the 2,527 ENABLE words with a Q, 2,498 contain QU, almost 99%.

Position matters as well. In ENABLE, S, C and P are the most common first letters, and S, E, D and Y the most common last ones. X and Z rarely start or end a word, so try them in the middle.

What are the best tricks for Scrabble, Words With Friends and Wordle?

A standard English Scrabble set has 100 tiles, including 2 blanks, and you hold seven at a time. Play all seven in one turn and you've scored a bingo, worth a 50-point bonus. That's why strong players memorize seven-letter anagram groups. AEINRST is the famous one, making RETAINS, RETINAS, NASTIER, STAINER and RETSINA. ACEILRT gives ARTICLE and RECITAL.

Short words matter just as much. QAT, QAID and SUQ let you dump a Q without a U, and QI is accepted in the official Scrabble dictionaries. A two-letter play that lands a 10-point tile on a premium square often beats a long word made of one-point letters.

Wordle-style puzzles don't hand you the letters, but once you've found three or four, the same instincts apply. Place the vowels, try endings like -ER or -LY in the last slots, and remember that letters can repeat.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you unscramble words quickly?

Split the letters into vowels and consonants, then look for a common ending like -ING, -ED or -ER and set those letters aside. Build a short word from what's left and bolt the ending back on. For RGNIDAE, pulling out ING leaves R, D, A, E, which spells READ, so the answer is READING.

How many ways can 7 letters be arranged?

If all seven letters are different, there are 5,040 arrangements (7 x 6 x 5 x 4 x 3 x 2 x 1). Repeated letters cut that down: LETTER has only 180 distinct orders.

How many words can you make from the letters in READING?

Checked against the ENABLE word list, the letters of READING make 157 words of two to seven letters. Four use all seven letters: READING, GRAINED, GRADINE and DERAIGN.

Are there English words with Q but no U?

Yes, but not many. The ENABLE list has 2,527 words containing Q, and only 29 of them lack a U, such as QAT, QAID, FAQIR, TRANQ and SUQ. QI is also accepted in the official Scrabble dictionaries, although it isn't in ENABLE.

What is a bingo in Scrabble?

A bingo is playing all seven tiles from your rack in a single turn. It earns a 50-point bonus on top of the word's score.
