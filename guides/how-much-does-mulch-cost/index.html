How Much Does Mulch Cost?

⏱️ 6 min read

Bulk mulch runs $30 to $50 per cubic yard for basic hardwood, $45 to $80 for cedar or cypress, and $3.25 to $6.50 per bag at retail — where it takes 13.5 of those bags to equal one yard. Delivered and spread by a crew, expect $77 to $94 per yard. The type you pick, the depth you spread it, and the bulk-versus-bag decision move the bill more than the supplier does. Here's the pricing and the math to check any quote against.

Wood dominates for a reason: it feeds the soil as it breaks down, holds moisture, and moderates soil temperature while doing it. Stone and rubber never need replacing, but they don't do anything for the dirt under them, and they cook roots in full sun. Pine straw, sold by the bale at $4 to $7 mostly in the Southeast, is its own category — cheap per bale, thin coverage, annual replenishment.

How much do mulch bags cost versus bulk?

The standard retail bag is 2 cubic feet. One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, so a yard equals 13.5 bags — the single most useful number in mulch shopping:

Buying methodPer-yard equivalentNotes
Bulk, picked up$30-$50Shoveled into your truck or trailer by the scoop
Bulk, delivered$30-$80 + deliveryTypical delivered/dumped cost lands around $72-$87
Bags at $3.25-$6.50$44-$8813.5 bags per yard; no truck needed, no shovel enjoyed

The crossover sits around one to two yards. Below that, bags are perfectly sensible — a car can carry them and the leftovers store clean. Past it, you're paying a 50%-plus premium and hauling 40-pound bags by the dozen. A 10-yard spring refresh at bag prices would mean 135 bags; the same order arrives in one dump truck.

How much does delivery or installation cost?

Delivery on a bulk order typically runs $30 to $80 depending on distance and load, and suppliers often fold it in above a volume threshold — ask rather than assume. If you'd rather not touch a rake at all, professional mulching runs $77 to $94 per cubic yard installed, including material, delivery, and spreading. Against a DIY bulk buy at $30-$50 plus your Saturday, the labor premium is roughly $30-$45 per yard; whether that's worth it depends on how you feel about wheelbarrows.

Depth is a choice, not a constant. One inch refreshes color on an existing layer. Two inches tops up a bed that's thinning. Three inches is the standard starting depth for a new bed, and four is the weed-suppression setting — beyond four or five you start suffocating roots, which is the opposite of the point. The spread from 2 to 3 inches on 1,000 square feet is 3.3 extra yards and $130; decide deliberately.

Which mulch should you choose?

Shredded hardwood is the workhorse: it mats down on slopes, moderates soil temperature, and breaks down into organic matter that lightens clay and feeds worms. Dyed versions cost a bit more and keep their color longer — fine around foundations and formal beds. Cedar and cypress last longer before fading to gray, which is why the premium sometimes pays for itself on beds you don't want to refresh annually. Around vegetables and herbs, plain undyed mulch or straw is the safer call. Two application rules regardless of type: pull mulch back 3-6 inches from trunks, crowns, and stems (volcano mulching kills trees slowly), and water it in after spreading so it settles instead of blowing.

The bottom line

Budget $30-$50 per cubic yard bulk for standard hardwood, $45-$80 for cedar or cypress, $77-$94 installed by a crew, and remember 13.5 bags make a yard at $3.25-$6.50 each. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a yard of mulch cost?

Basic hardwood mulch runs $30-$50 per cubic yard in bulk, dyed varieties run about $5-$10 more, and premium cedar or cypress runs $45-$80. The full bulk market spans $30-$135 per yard including specialty products.

How many bags of mulch equal a cubic yard?

Thirteen and a half 2-cubic-foot bags equal one cubic yard (27 cubic feet). At $3.25-$6.50 per bag, a yard of bagged mulch costs $44-$88, versus $30-$50 for the same yard dumped off a truck.

How much does mulch delivery or installation cost?

Delivery on a bulk order typically runs $30-$80 depending on distance and load size, and many suppliers fold it in on bigger orders. Professional spreading runs $77-$94 per cubic yard installed including material, delivery, and labor.

How deep should mulch be spread?

Three inches is the standard for a new bed, 1-2 inches refreshes an existing layer, and anything past 4-5 inches can smother roots. Keep mulch 3-6 inches back from trunks and plant crowns, whatever the depth.

Is bulk mulch cheaper than bags?

Almost always, once the order passes a couple of yards. A yard costs $30-$50 bulk versus $44-$88 bagged, and a 10-yard refresh at bag prices would mean hauling 135 bags. Under about half a yard, bags win on convenience.
