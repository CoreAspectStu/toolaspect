How to Print a PDF Poster on Multiple Pages

📄 6 min readUpdated October 31, 2026

To print a big PDF across several sheets of paper, you need a tiling or "poster" option, and the free Acrobat Reader is the easiest place to get one. Below are the exact steps, how many sheets common poster sizes take on Letter and A4, and how to do it without Acrobat.

Which apps can print a PDF as a poster?

Fewer than you'd think. Printing one big page across several sheets is called tiling, and most PDF viewers can't do it. Here's where to look:

AppWhere it isOverlap and cut marks?

The free Acrobat Reader is the easiest route on both Windows and Mac. If your browser opens PDFs, download the file and open it in Reader instead.

How do I tile a PDF in Acrobat Reader?

  1. Open the PDF and choose Print.
  2. Under Page Sizing & Handling, click Poster.
  3. Set Tile Scale. At 100%, a 24 × 36 page prints at 24 × 36. To enlarge a Letter-size page, raise it: 200% doubles the width and height.
  4. Set Overlap to about 0.25 in. It gives you a strip to glue or tape over, and it covers the bit your printer can't reach.
  5. Tick Cut marks and Labels. Labels print the row and column on each sheet, which saves a lot of guessing later.
  6. Check the preview on the right. It shows the grid and the number of sheets before you print anything.

"Tile only large pages" is for mixed documents. With it ticked, normal pages print as normal and only oversized ones get split.

How many sheets will my poster take?

That depends on the poster size, the paper, and how much each sheet loses to margins and overlap. We worked it out for common sizes at 100% scale, with a 0.25 in unprintable margin on every side (typical for home printers) and 0.25 in overlap, picking whichever paper direction uses fewer sheets:

Poster sizeLetter sheetsA4 sheets
11 × 17 in (tabloid)4 (2 × 2, portrait)3 (1 × 3, landscape)
18 × 24 in8 (2 × 4, landscape)8 (2 × 4, landscape)
24 × 36 in15 (3 × 5, landscape)15 (3 × 5, landscape)
36 × 48 in25 (5 × 5, portrait)25 (5 × 5, portrait)
A2 (420 × 594 mm)6 (2 × 3, landscape)8 (2 × 4, landscape)
A1 (594 × 841 mm)12 (3 × 4, portrait)15 (3 × 5, landscape)

The reason a 24 × 36 takes 15 sheets and not 12 is the margins. A Letter sheet is 8.5 × 11, but only about 8 × 10.5 of it prints, and each overlap eats another quarter inch. Lose those and three sheets across no longer reach 24 inches on the short side.

Enlarging a regular page grows fast, because doubling the scale quadruples the area:

Remember that enlarging doesn't add detail. A PDF made from a photo or screenshot will get soft at 300% or more. Vector PDFs, like most made from Word, InDesign or Illustrator, stay sharp at any size.

Can I tile a PDF without Acrobat?

Yes, with a bit more work. On Windows, check your printer's own settings first. Many HP, Canon, Epson and Brother drivers have a poster mode, usually a fixed 2 × 2 up to 4 × 4 grid.

On Linux, or anywhere with Python, pdfposter splits a PDF into page-sized tiles that any app can print. We ran pdfposter -mletter -p24x36in poster.pdf out.pdf on a 24 × 36 file. It gave a 12-page PDF, 3 columns by 4 rows of Letter pages. The catch is that it fills each page edge to edge with no overlap, so your printer's margin trims a strip off every tile. To avoid that, tell it the paper is a little smaller than it is (for example -m 8x10.5in) and print at 100% with no "fit to page."

The last option is a print shop. A single 24 × 36 poster print is often cheaper than the ink for 15 sheets, and there are no seams.

How do I print just one page of a long PDF as a poster?

Pull that page out first. Use our extract PDF pages tool to save it as its own file, then tile that. If the page has wide white margins, trim them with crop PDF so you're not spending sheets on blank paper. Starting from a photo? Turn it into a PDF with JPG to PDF and tile that in Reader.

How do I put the sheets together?

Lay them out using the labels. Trim the overlap off one edge of each joining pair, the right edge on columns and the bottom edge on rows, using the cut marks as your guide. Then lay each trimmed edge over the overlap of the next sheet. A craft knife and a metal ruler make cleaner cuts than scissors. Glue stick wrinkles thin paper less than tape on the front does. Put tape on the back if the poster is going on a wall.

Turn off any "fit to page" or "shrink oversized pages" option before you print. It quietly scales each tile down, and then the pieces won't line up. For other print settings, see printing a PDF without margins and the reverse trick, printing several pages on one sheet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I print a PDF poster on multiple pages in Chrome?

No. The PDF viewers in Chrome, Edge and Firefox don't have a tiling or poster option. Download the PDF and open it in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader, which has a Poster setting in its print dialog.

How many sheets of paper for a 24 x 36 poster?

About 15 Letter or A4 sheets in a 3 by 5 grid, assuming a 0.25 inch printer margin and 0.25 inch overlap. Without margins or overlap the math says 12, but then the printer trims the edges of every tile.

What overlap should I use when tiling a poster?

Around 0.25 inch works for most home printers. It's enough to cover the unprintable edge and gives you a strip to glue. Go up to 0.5 inch if your printer has wide margins or you're joining with tape.

Does Mac Preview have a poster print option?

No. Preview can print several pages on one sheet, but it can't split one page across several sheets. Use Acrobat Reader for Mac, or a printer driver that offers poster printing.

Why are my tiled poster pieces not lining up?

Usually a scaling option like "fit to page" or "shrink oversized pages" is on, so each tile prints slightly smaller. Print at 100% or actual size, and use the overlap and cut marks to trim before joining.
