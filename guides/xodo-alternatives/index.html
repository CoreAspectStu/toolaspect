Is there anything free in Xodo still worth using?

Yes, the desktop reader. Xodo PDF Reader is free on Windows, Mac and Linux, and it handles the things most people open a PDF app for: reading, highlighting, adding comments, filling in forms and signing. Linux users in particular don't get many polished free readers, so it's a good one to keep. Where it stops is editing existing text and converting to Word or Excel, which need the paid PDF Studio.

The daily action limit is what pushes most people to look elsewhere. It applies to the web tools, where one compress or one merge uses up the day.

Is it safe to upload PDFs to Xodo?

For everyday files it's fine. Xodo's compress page says uploads are permanently deleted after 1 hour, which is in line with the bigger PDF sites. What Xodo doesn't publish is where its servers are, which matters if you work under GDPR or a client contract that names a region. For sensitive documents, the simplest fix is a tool that never uploads in the first place. Our guide on uploading PDFs safely compares retention policies across services.

Should you pay for Xodo?

It makes sense if you want PDF Studio's full editor on a Mac or Linux machine, where good paid editors are fewer, and the $240 perpetual licence avoids yet another subscription. For web tools alone, $95.88 a year is a lot to pay for merge and compress when those jobs are free elsewhere. And if your only problem is the daily limit, try the free reader first. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Xodo free?

Some of it. The Xodo PDF Reader for Windows, Mac and Linux is free for viewing, annotating, filling forms and basic signing. The web tools allow 1 free action a day, and the mobile app's free plan adds a watermark to documents you edit. Xodo Web costs $7.99 a month, billed $95.88 a year.

Why does Xodo say I've reached my limit?

Free use of Xodo's web tools is limited to 1 action or download a day, and the limit resets daily. Merging, compressing or converting each counts. If you need more today, use a free browser tool with no task limit or the free Xodo PDF Reader desktop app for annotating and signing.

Does Xodo add a watermark?

Xodo says documents edited on the free mobile plan get a watermark. Its free desktop reader is for viewing, annotating, form filling and basic signing, and editing or conversion there needs the paid PDF Studio.

How long does Xodo keep uploaded files?

Xodo's compress tool page says uploaded files are permanently deleted after 1 hour. Its terms also say files tied to an account are deleted 30 days after the account is closed. Xodo doesn't state where its servers are.

Who owns Xodo?

Xodo is made by Apryse Software Inc., the company formerly known as PDFTron. Apryse also sells PDF software development kits to other companies, and Xodo PDF Studio is its desktop editor.
