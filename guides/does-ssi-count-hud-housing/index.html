Does SSI Count HUD-VASH, Section 202 or Section 811 Housing?

📄 6 min readUpdated November 5, 2026

They shouldn't count. SSA excludes federal housing help from income and resources, and it tells its staff to assume that any HUD assistance qualifies, so your 2026 check can stay at $994. SSI does count when your rent is set, at about $284 to $288 a month, and VA benefits can change both numbers. Here's how each program works.

Does HUD-VASH, Section 202 or Section 811 count as income?

Almost certainly not. SSA's operations manual, at SI 00830.630, excludes the value of housing assistance paid under a list of federal laws. It then tells staff to assume that any help involving HUD or USDA's Rural Housing Service falls under that exclusion, and to accept your statement unless they've got a reason to question it.

That assumption does the heavy lifting, because the manual doesn't list every program by name.

We didn't find an SSA example that names HUD-VASH, Section 811 or a current Section 202 rental assistance contract. HUD-VASH is authorized as a Section 8 voucher, and SSA's manual notes that Section 8 is HUD assistance, so that one's a short step. The Section 202 listing is narrower than it looks, as it cites only subsection (h). If a worker questions it, point to the assumption in part C of SI 00830.630.

Why does this matter when your rent is low? Normally, rent under the presumed maximum value, about $351.33 in 2026, leaves the gap counted as support under 20 CFR 416.1130. Many tenants in these programs pay less. The exclusion is what keeps that from cutting your check.

What about a tax credit apartment?

A low-income housing tax credit building isn't HUD help on its own, so the exclusion above doesn't cover it. You don't usually need it, though. If your required rent is at least $351.33, SSA treats it as a business arrangement, and there's nothing to count. If it's lower, ask SSA, because the gap can count. Some tax credit tenants also hold a voucher, and then the voucher is excluded as usual.

How much rent will you pay on SSI?

All three programs use HUD's total tenant payment, which on SSI almost always means 30% of adjusted income. The flat deduction for an elderly or disabled household is $550 for 2026 under HUD's newer rules, and $400 under the older ones, so ask which applies. Utility allowances then come off what you pay the landlord.

How do VA benefits change the math on HUD-VASH?

They count twice, and veterans often get caught by that. Veterans benefits are unearned income for SSI under 20 CFR 416.1121, so after the $20 exclusion, a $600 VA payment lowers SSI from $994 to $414. At about $1,014 a month, with no other income, SSI stops altogether. Our VA disability pay calculator shows what your rating pays.

On the housing side, HUD's 2024 HUD-VASH notice excludes VA service-connected disability benefits when deciding if you're income eligible. It then says those benefits are included when calculating your total tenant payment. So the exclusion gets you in the door, and your rent still counts the money. The notice names only service-connected disability benefits, so don't assume a VA pension gets the same treatment.

What could still reduce your SSI?

The subsidy is protected, but other money isn't. A relative who pays your $284 share to the landlord counts as support, which would take a $994 check to $730 after the $20 exclusion. Pay it yourself from your own account. Cash you keep is covered by the $2,000 limit as usual, and a voucher or subsidy won't change that.

Meals are less of a worry than they used to be. Since September 30, 2024, SSA's in-kind rule looks at shelter only, so a meal at your building shouldn't count as support. Living with someone else raises other questions, and our guide on SSI and living with family explains them.

What should you tell SSA and the housing provider?

Tell SSA when you move, and say plainly that your housing is HUD-assisted, naming the program. Keep your lease and rent notice to hand. Tell the owner or housing agency when your income or household changes. For 2027, SSI rises to $1,029 and your rent should follow at your next review, by about $10 a month. A disability benefits calculator gives a rough figure if you also get SSDI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does HUD-VASH reduce SSI?

It shouldn't. HUD-VASH is a Section 8 voucher under the 1937 Housing Act, and SSA excludes that help from income and resources. Your VA benefits are different: those do count, and they lower your SSI.

Does Section 202 housing count against SSI?

No. SSA's manual lists section 202(h) of the 1959 Housing Act and tells staff to assume HUD-involved help is excluded. You'd still pay about $284 to $288 a month in rent on $994 of SSI in 2026.

Is Section 811 housing counted as income for SSI?

SSA's list doesn't name it, but the manual says to assume any housing help involving HUD is excluded, and to accept your statement unless there's reason to doubt it. Tell SSA it's HUD-assisted.

Does VA disability count as income for HUD-VASH?

For eligibility, no. For rent, yes. HUD's 2024 notice excludes service-connected disability benefits when testing your income, then includes them when setting your total tenant payment.

Can I keep my Section 202 subsidy if I move?

No. The rental assistance goes to the building's owner, so it stays with the unit. A tenant-based HUD-VASH voucher can move with you, within the program's rules, but a project-based one is tied to its unit.
