How to Print a PDF on Legal Paper (8.5 × 14)

📄 5 min readUpdated November 2, 2026

Load 8.5 × 14 paper, set the paper size to Legal in the print dialog, and choose Actual size. That's all there is when your printer supports it. Most problems come from a setting stuck on Letter, or from expecting Fit to do something the page shape won't allow. Here are the settings in each app, the exact scale factors, and fixes for mixed files.

Is your PDF actually legal size?

Check before you touch the printer settings. Open the file's properties (Ctrl+D in Acrobat, Tools > Show Inspector in Preview) and read the page size. Legal is the same width as Letter and 3 inches longer:

A page that measures 8.5 × 14 is legal. Scanned contracts sometimes come in at 8.49 or 8.51, which is close enough. If the file is really Letter and you want it on legal paper, skip to the table below, because you won't gain anything by scaling it.

How do you print a PDF on legal paper?

Three things have to agree: the paper in the tray, the size your printer driver thinks is loaded, and the size the PDF app sends. Most failed jobs have one of them still set to Letter.

  1. Load legal paper and slide the tray guides out to the 14-inch mark. Many home printers take it only through a rear or manual feed.
  2. Open the print dialog and set the paper size to Legal. It's often hidden under "More settings."
  3. Choose Actual size or 100%, then check the preview shows the whole page.
  4. Print one page first. Legal paper costs more than Letter, and a mismatch tends to show up on the first sheet.

What happens if you print legal on Letter paper?

At 100%, the bottom 3 inches are cut off, which is 21.4% of the page. That's usually where the signature block sits, so don't print a contract this way. Fit shrinks the whole page instead. We placed test pages with PyMuPDF to check the math, and 12 pt type measured 9.43 pt on Letter:

These figures ignore printer margins. With a quarter-inch margin all around, Legal on Letter drops to 75.0% and 12 pt type to 9.0 pt, which is readable but small. There's a second way. If the bottom of each page is blank, as it often is on a short legal form, use Crop PDF to trim those 3 inches and print the rest at full size. Why doesn't Fit make a Letter PDF fill legal paper?

Because the two sizes are the same width. Fit scales until the page hits the first edge, and a Letter page already touches both sides at 100%. You'll always get 3 inches of blank paper, split top and bottom or all at the bottom depending on the app. To use the extra space, you have to change the document, not the print settings. In Word or Excel, set the page size to Legal and export a new PDF.

What if the PDF mixes Letter and legal pages?

This is common in scanned closing packages and court exhibits. You have two options:

  • Let the app pick the tray. Acrobat's "Choose paper source by PDF page size" sends each page to the matching tray. It only helps if your printer has both sizes loaded.
  • Split the file. Use Extract PDF Pages to pull the legal pages into their own file and print the two jobs separately.

Why does my legal PDF print sideways or cut off?

Sideways output usually means the page is stored as 14 × 8.5 landscape, or has a rotation flag your printer's handling differently. Fix it for good with Rotate PDF, or set the orientation by hand in the dialog. Cut-off output usually means the driver is still set to Letter even though the app says Legal, so check the printer's own preferences. If the print comes out shrunken with wide side margins, that's Fit at work on Letter stock: the 1.82 inches of side space in the table is exactly what you're seeing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size is legal paper?

Legal paper is 8.5 by 14 inches, or 215.9 by 355.6 mm. It's the same width as US Letter and 3 inches longer. In a PDF, a legal page measures 612 by 1008 points.

Can I print a legal size document on letter paper?

Yes. Choose Fit in the print dialog. The page shrinks to 78.6%, or about 75% with printer margins, and 12 pt text prints at about 9 pt. At 100% the bottom 3 inches are cut off.

Why does my printer not show legal size?

The driver for that printer doesn't offer it, or the selected tray doesn't support it. Try the rear or manual feed if your printer has one, and check the spec sheet. A PDF app can't add a size the driver doesn't list.

Do I need a special printer for legal paper?

Often not. Plenty of home and office printers take 8.5 by 14 paper, sometimes only through a manual or rear feed. Your model's spec sheet lists the maximum paper length. The width is the same as Letter, so only the length matters.

How do I print only the legal pages from a mixed PDF?

Find the page numbers in your PDF reader, then enter them in the print dialog's page range, or use an extract tool to save them as a separate file. Print that file with Legal selected and print the rest on Letter.
