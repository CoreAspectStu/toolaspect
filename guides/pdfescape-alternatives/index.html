Why do people switch away from PDFescape?

Usually it's one specific job. You open a PDF to merge it with another, shrink it for an email, or fix a typo, and the button you need has a Premium badge on it. The free plan is generous for filling and marking up, but the jobs people search for most often, merging and compressing, aren't in it.

The 10 MB upload cap is the next snag. A scanned contract or a phone-photo PDF passes 10 MB quickly, and PDFescape's answer is the 40 MB Premium limit. Browser-based tools don't have an upload cap at all, because nothing is uploaded. The limit is your device's memory.

Then there's where the file sits. PDFescape is an online editor, so your document goes to its servers, and the free plan keeps recent files for seven days. That's fine for a menu or a flyer. For a lease, a medical form or anything with your Social Security number on it, many people would rather the file stayed put.

Finally, PDFescape's desktop app is Windows only. Mac and Chromebook users are limited to the website, which is where free alternatives tend to be stronger anyway.

How do I add text to a PDF without PDFescape?

Open PDF Add Text, load the file, click the spot where the text should go and type. You can pick Helvetica, Times or Courier, set the size and color, and stamp the same text on every page if you need to, which is handy for dates or "APPROVED" stamps. The download keeps the original file intact with your text added on top.

That's the right tool for a flat form, the kind printed to PDF with blank lines and no clickable fields. For a fillable form, you don't need a tool at all. Open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, click into the fields, type, and save or print to PDF. Mac Preview and the free Adobe Reader do the same. Our guide to adding text to a PDF walks through both cases.

Can I edit existing text in a PDF for free?

This is the one job PDFescape keeps behind Premium, and it's hard for everyone. A PDF doesn't store paragraphs. It stores bits of text pinned to exact positions on the page, so changing a word without shifting everything around takes real work.

Sejda handles it best among free options, within its three tasks an hour. LibreOffice Draw opens a PDF as editable shapes and copes with simple pages. If the original is a Word file, editing that and exporting a new PDF beats any PDF editor. And if all you need is to add a correction next to the old text, PDF Add Text or a comment in PDF Annotate is often enough.

Is it safe to white out text in a PDF?

Only if you use a real redaction tool. PDFescape's free whiteout and most "edit PDF" sites draw a shape on top of the text. It looks hidden, but the words are still in the file, and anyone can copy them out or remove the box.

Redact PDF works differently. It turns each page into an image with your boxes burned in, then builds a fresh PDF from those images. The covered text is gone for good, along with the old metadata and comments. The trade-off is that the rest of the page isn't selectable text anymore. Our redaction guide explains how to check the result before you send it.

For forms, start with the browser you already have, then reach for PDF Add Text if the form isn't fillable. For markup and signatures, PDF Annotate and Sign PDF cover it without an upload. PDFescape Premium is cheap and still worth it if you edit PDFs every week and want everything in one place. For occasional jobs, free tools are enough. Our Smallpdf alternatives and free Adobe Acrobat alternatives guides cover the other big names.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDFescape free?

The online editor has a free plan. It lets you annotate, add text, fill and save forms, create basic forms and password-protect files, with a 10 MB upload limit and storage for 10 recent files kept for 7 days. Merging, compressing, editing existing text, page numbers and watermarks need Premium, and redaction and digital signatures need Ultimate.

How much does PDFescape Premium cost?

When we checked PDFescape's pricing page in October 2026, Premium was $2.99 a month billed yearly or $5.99 month to month, and Ultimate was $5.99 a month billed yearly or $8.99 month to month. Both came with a 15-day money-back guarantee.

How do I fill in a PDF form for free without PDFescape?

If the form has fillable fields, open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Mac Preview, type into the fields and save. No extra tool needed. If it's a flat form with no fields, use a tool that places text where you click, such as ToolAspect's PDF Add Text, which works in your browser without uploading.

Can I white out text in a PDF for free?

You can cover it, but be careful. A white or black box drawn on top leaves the original text in the file, where copy and paste can still find it. To actually remove it, use a real redaction tool. ToolAspect's Redact PDF turns each page into an image with your boxes burned in, so the covered words are gone.

Does PDFescape upload my files?

Yes. The online editor works on PDFescape's servers, and its free plan keeps recent files for 7 days. If you'd rather a document never left your device, use a browser-based tool that processes the file locally, or a desktop app such as Preview or LibreOffice.
