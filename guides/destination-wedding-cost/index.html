What Does a Destination Wedding Actually Cost?

💡 12-20K searches/mo💰 CPC: $3-8⏱️ 9 min read

The couple signs for $9,000–$25,000 at most Caribbean and Mexico all-inclusives — roughly a third of the ~$33,000–$36,000 average US wedding. The catch hiding in plain sight: every guest buys their own $1,340 vacation, so a 30-guest wedding's true total clears $50,000. Here's both ledgers, package tier by tier, plus the legal paperwork by country that decides whether your marriage is real when you land.

A realistic mid-range Jamaica plan — standard $4,600 package for 30 guests, $325 legal, $1,000 flights, five nights at $350, resort photographer, $800 welcome party, $8 bags — totals $9,915. Same plan at the basic tier for 20 guests: $6,185. Premium tier, 50 guests, flown-in photographer: $22,975.

Package tiers decoded

  • Basic / "complimentary" ($0–$1,500): ceremony site, basic florals, champagne toast, cake, an hour of music. Often requires a room-night minimum or suite category. Extra guests $25–$50 each past 10.
  • Standard ($3,000–$6,000): adds a coordinator, upgraded decor, reception or private-dinner credit, audio, and photographer hours. Includes ~30 guests; overages $50–$100.
  • Premium ($10,000–$15,000): private reception, full floral design, videography, spa credits, ~40 guests included, overages $100–$130.

Published 2025 examples from Jamaica resorts run $4,599 for 30 guests and $13,199 for 40, with $25–$130 per extra guest. The "free wedding" in the brochure is real but skeletal — a la carte additions are how it becomes a paid tier in disguise.

The classic move for Mexico and the DR: legally marry at the courthouse at home first, then hold a symbolic ceremony abroad. Nobody at the resort can tell the difference, and you skip apostilles, translations, and a judge fee. Rules change and resort wedding offices know the current drill — confirm before booking (and none of this is legal advice).

The ledger nobody shows you: guests

Each guest pays airfare plus three to four group-rate nights, typically $1,100–$1,700. At the worked example's $1,340, your 30 guests collectively spend $40,200 on your wedding — more than four times your own bill. Two implications worth acting on:

  1. Expect 40–60% attendance, not the 75%+ of a local wedding. Send save-the-dates 8–12 months out.
  2. Book the room block through the resort's group desk or a travel agent. Guests get group rates, you get credit toward room-night minimums (which can comp your package), and one point of contact handles flight changes for everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a realistic budget for a destination wedding?

$9,000-$25,000 for the couple covers most Caribbean and Mexico all-inclusive weddings: a $4,600 standard package for 30 guests, $325-$800 in legal fees, $1,000 in flights, five room nights, a photographer, and a welcome event lands near $9,915. Budget-tier couples do it for $5,000-$7,000 with basic packages; premium packages with flown-in vendors pass $20,000.

Why is the true total so much higher than what the couple pays?

Guests pay their own way: about $1,340 each for airfare and three group-rate nights at a typical Jamaica wedding. Multiply by 30 guests and the room-and-airfare bill the couple never sees is $40,200 — a $50,115 true total against the couple's $9,915. That's not a scam; it's the trade: the couple buys a smaller bill, guests buy a vacation.

Which destination has the easiest marriage requirements?

Jamaica: 24 hours on the island, a ~$75 license arranged through your planner, certified birth certificates, no blood test, no apostille — about $325 all-in with the officiant. Las Vegas is even faster (~$195 total). Mexico and the Dominican Republic are the paperwork-heavy ones: apostilled, translated documents and $600-$1,000 in fees, which is why many couples marry legally at home and hold a symbolic ceremony at the resort.

Do all-inclusive resorts really give free wedding packages?

Some do — a basic 'complimentary' ceremony for couples who book a minimum number of room nights (often 5-10 across the group) or a higher suite category. The free tier covers a ceremony site, basic florals, and champagne; anything you actually pictured — private reception, real decor, photography — is priced a la carte, which is how 'free' becomes $4,600.

What percentage of invited guests attend a destination wedding?

Plan on 40-60% of your invite list, versus 70-85% for a local wedding. Invitations should go out 8-12 months ahead, and the room block should be booked with a travel agent or the resort's group desk so guests get group rates and you get credit toward room-night minimums.
