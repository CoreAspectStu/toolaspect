How Much Does a Marriage License Cost in 2026?

💍 ~20K+ searches/mo💰 CPC: $1⏱️ 8 min read

A marriage license runs $18 to $169 depending on the state, and most couples pay $40–$90 at the clerk's counter. Indiana ($18–$30) is the floor; Washington ($169 after its July 2025 jump from $69) is the ceiling, with Minnesota at $125 and Los Angeles County's approved $176 fee (delayed; currently $91) set to push past it. The license is the cheapest line in your wedding budget, but it comes with the details couples actually trip on: county-set fees, waiting periods, expiration windows, and certified copies for the name change. What a courthouse wedding actually totals

The ceremony line is smaller than couples expect. Most counties charge $25–$100 for a civil ceremony with a judge or justice of the peace; some run them free during business hours, and Las Vegas, the volume leader, has officiants starting around $50–$75. Add certified copies at $5–$25 each, you want 2–4 for a name change, and the all-in math looks like this for a Texas couple: $82 license + $25–$100 ceremony + $10–$50 for two copies = $117–$232, with the counseling certificate dragging it down to $57–$172.

What the fee never covers: witnesses in states that require them (usually one or two, and clerk offices don't supply them), photos beyond what your phones take, and the marriage certificate copies themselves, those are ordered separately after the signed license goes back to the clerk.

The details that actually bite

  • Waiting periods: New York holds licenses 24 hours before the ceremony; Washington, 3 days. Book the ceremony at least a week after the application.
  • Expiration: 30–90 days depending on the state. An unused license expires worthless and you repay from scratch.
  • Where you apply: the license comes from the state, usually the county, where the ceremony happens. Elopement road trips pay the destination state's fee.
  • ID requirements: government-issued photo ID almost everywhere; some states want birth certificates or Social Security numbers; divorced applicants may need the decree date.
  • Card convenience fees: offices that take cards often add 2–3%. On Washington's new $169 fee that's another $3–$5.

One budgeting note: the license is a rounding error next to the wedding itself. Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a marriage license in 2026?

$18 to $169 depending on the state, with most couples paying $40-$90. Indiana ($18-$30) and Michigan ($20-$30) are cheapest; Washington ($169 after its July 2025 increase), Minnesota ($125), and Los Angeles County (at $91, with an approved increase to $176 still pending) are the expensive end. Because counties set fees in most states, two couples in the same state can pay different amounts.

Can a premarital counseling course really save money?

In the states that offer the discount, yes, and the course usually costs less than it saves. Oklahoma drops $50 to $5, Texas $82 to $22 (8-hour course), Georgia about $56 to $16, Florida $93.50 to $61 (4-hour course), Tennessee $95 to $35, and Minnesota $125 to $50, the biggest dollar discount in the country. Fifteen-plus states have some version of the discount.

What does a courthouse wedding cost all-in?

License plus ceremony plus copies. The civil ceremony itself runs $25-$100 in most counties, some do it free, Las Vegas officiants start around $50-$75. Certified copies for the name change run $5-$25 each and most couples want 2-4. A Texas example: $82 license + $25-$100 ceremony + $10-$50 for two copies = $117-$232 all-in.

Why do marriage license fees vary so much between states?

The fee funds the county clerk's vital-records work: processing, filing, verification, and producing the document. States with county-set fees show wide internal ranges, Pennsylvania runs $50-$90 and Maryland $35-$85 by county, and urban counties with higher operating costs tend to sit at the top. A few states raised fees sharply in 2025, Washington jumped from $69 to $169, which is why stale articles underquote.
