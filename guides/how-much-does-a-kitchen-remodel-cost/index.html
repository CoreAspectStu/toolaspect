How Much Does a Kitchen Remodel Cost in 2026?

💡 ~33K searches/mo💰 CPC: $22⏱️ 9 min read

Kitchen remodels come in three tiers: a facelift near $135 per square foot, a midrange gut remodel near $420, and upscale custom work at $825 and up. For a typical 150 sq ft kitchen that's roughly $20,000, $63,000, or $124,000. The tier you pick matters more than the size you have.

Where does the money actually go?

Kitchen budgets follow a fairly stable allocation, often taught off NKBA-style guidelines. On a $63,000 midrange remodel of a 150 sq ft kitchen:

  • Cabinets & hardware, 29% ($18,270). The single biggest line and the biggest swing: stock boxes cost about a third of custom.
  • Installation & labor, 17% ($10,710). Carpenters, trades, demo, haul-away, punch list.
  • Appliances & ventilation, 14% ($8,820). Mid-range suite of range, fridge, dishwasher, and a vented hood.
  • Countertops, 10% ($6,300). Quartz at $60–$100 per sq ft installed is today's default.
  • Flooring, 7% ($4,410). LVP or tile across the finished area.
  • Everything else, 23%. Plumbing, electrical and lighting, walls and ceilings, design fees, doors and windows.

When a bid comes in wildly different from these shares, ask why. A quote that hides the cabinet line or folds labor into "allowances" is where budgets quietly blow up.

What pushes a budget past $100,000?

Four scope changes do most of the damage, and none of them are finishes:

  • Moving plumbing or gas (about +8%). Every drain or supply line you relocate touches framing, subfloor, and permits.
  • Removing a wall ($3,000 – $9,000). A structural header, posts, possible beam engineering, and patching ceiling, floor, and roof lines where they used to be.
  • Custom cabinetry. Custom boxes run two to three times stock; semi-custom splits the difference and is where most midrange kitchens land.
  • Professional appliances. A pro range alone can cost what an entire appliance suite does in midrange.

Keep the wet wall where it is and skip the wall removal, and $80,000 of scope can land closer to $70,000.

How can you save 20% or more?

  1. Keep the layout. Plumbing relocation is the most expensive line per foot of movement in the room.
  2. Reface instead of replace when boxes are sound. New doors, drawer fronts, and hardware deliver 70% of the visual change for a third of the cost.
  3. Buy appliances on promo. Holiday appliance sales and package deals routinely save 15% to 25% off a suite.
  4. Do your own demo and paint if the general contractor allows it, with the trades left licensed.
  5. Shop the slab yards for countertop remnants, especially for islands under 40 sq ft; price a full slab with our countertop calculator first so you know the baseline.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A facelift runs 2 to 4 weeks. A midrange gut remodel runs 6 to 12 weeks from demolition to final punch list, and upscale projects with custom cabinets run 4 to 6 months; custom cabinetry alone has 8 to 12 week lead times. Budget for six to ten weeks of paper plates either way, and confirm the cabinet delivery date before demo day, because the kitchen can't close up without it.

Does a kitchen remodel pay off at resale?

Small projects do; big ones less so. Minor kitchen remodels recoup roughly 95% of cost in the Cost vs. Value Report, among the highest of any project in the study. Major midrange remodels recoup closer to 40% to 50%, and upscale remodels trail that. You remodel a kitchen because you'll live with it, not to flip a profit. If resale is the goal, the data points at paint, hardware, counters, and a good cleaning, not a gut.

One more planning note: kitchens and baths share trades and lead times, so combining them in one contract sometimes shaves 5% to 10% off the combined price. Price the second project with our bathroom remodel cost calculator before you sign anything.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a $30,000 kitchen remodel include?

At $30,000 you're doing a strong facelift or a modest midrange partial: refaced or stock cabinets, quartz or laminate counters, a new sink and faucet, lighting, paint, and flooring, keeping the layout and most appliances. It fits a small kitchen or a bigger one done in stages.

Why do kitchen remodels cost so much?

Cabinets run about 29% of the budget and are built to order, licensed trades (plumber, electrician, HVAC) touch every project, and kitchens pack more finish per square foot than any other room: counters, backsplash, flooring, two or more appliances, and ventilation in 150 square feet.

Is it worth remodeling a kitchen before selling?

A light refresh, yes: minor kitchen remodels recoup roughly 95% of cost in Remodeling's Cost vs. Value Report, among the best of any project. A $80,000+ gut remodel before selling usually loses money, since major midrange remodels recoup closer to 40% to 50%. Clean, paint, and fix; skip the gut.
