How Much Does It Cost to Change Your Name After Marriage?

⏱️ 7 min read

Doing it yourself, a marriage name change typically runs $150 to $300. The anchor step — the Social Security card — is free, always. The DMV charges $0 to $45 by state, a passport name change runs $0 to $165 depending on when it was issued, and the certified marriage-certificate copies that power everything cost $5 to $25 each. Online kits add $50 to $100 without replacing any government fee. Here's the whole stack, in the order that works.

What's the cheapest first step — and why it's free

Social Security. Form SS-5 has never carried a fee: you file it with your marriage certificate and acceptable ID, by mail or at a field office, and the reissued card arrives in about two to four weeks. It's also the gatekeeper — most DMVs and the passport agency check that the SSA record already shows your new name before they'll touch their own documents. Florida makes the chain explicit: the SSA update needs to be done at least 48 hours before your DMV visit. Build that lag into your plans everywhere.

Are online name-change services worth it?

The kits cost $50 to $100 and do exactly one thing: they pre-fill your forms and ship instructions with the right addresses and envelopes. Not one government fee gets smaller because you paid for a kit. For a simple stack — Social Security, DMV, passport — that's an expensive autopilot for three forms. Where they earn the price is a long tail of updates: a dozen banks, professional licenses, loyalty programs, deeds, and titles. Budget the kit on top of the fees, not instead of them.

What order should you update documents in?

  1. Certified copies first. Order three or four when you finalize the license — they're the fuel for every step that follows, and several offices keep them.
  2. Social Security. Free, 2–4 weeks. Everything downstream checks it.
  3. Driver's license. Usually within 30 days of the name change; bring the certified copy and your new Social Security card.
  4. Passport. Free under DS-5504 if issued within the last year; otherwise renew at $130 before booking any international travel.
  5. Vehicle title and registration, then banks, payroll, insurance, and voters registration — all free.

What happens if you wait?

Nothing catastrophic, but the friction compounds: mismatched documents slow airport checks and notarizations, tax returns that don't match SSA records get held, and the certified copies you'll need later take weeks by mail. Done inside a month, the whole stack costs one afternoon. One boundary: this covers the standard marriage path — taking, hyphenating, or merging a surname needs only the certificate. A whole new name goes through a court petition at $150 to $500 in filing fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to change your last name after marriage?

Usually $150 to $300 doing it yourself. The Social Security card is free, the DMV charges $0 to $45 depending on your state, a passport name change is $0 to $165 depending on when your current passport was issued, and certified copies of your marriage certificate run $5 to $25 each — plan on two to four. A typical Florida stack totals about $230.

Is it free to change your name with Social Security after marriage?

Yes. Form SS-5 has never carried a fee. You file it with your marriage certificate and ID, by mail or at a field office, and the new card arrives in about two to four weeks. Do it first — the DMV and passport agencies check that your Social Security record already shows the new name.

How much does it cost to change your name on your passport?

Nothing if your current passport was issued within the last year — file Form DS-5504 for free. Otherwise renew by mail with Form DS-82 at $130, or file Form DS-11 in person at $130 plus the $35 execution fee if you are not eligible to renew by mail. Budget about $15 more for a passport photo.

How many certified copies of the marriage certificate should you order?

Two to four, at $5 to $25 each. Social Security, the DMV, passport agencies, insurers, and banks each want to see a certified copy, and several keep them. Ordering extras weeks later by mail is slow, so buy them when you get the license — before you need them.

Are online name-change kits worth the money?

They cost $50 to $100 and pre-fill your paperwork, but they do not replace a single government fee. For a simple Social Security plus DMV plus passport stack, doing it yourself is a few forms and one afternoon. Kits earn their price when you are also updating a dozen banks, professional licenses, loyalty programs, and titles.
