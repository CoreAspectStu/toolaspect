How Much Gravel Do I Need? Tons, Yards and Depth

🧮 7 min readUpdated September 30, 2026

To work out how much gravel you need, multiply length × width × depth in feet, divide by 27 for cubic yards, then multiply by about 1.4 to get tons of pea gravel or #57 stone. A 20 × 10 ft area at 3 in deep takes 1.85 yd³, or about 2.6 tons. Order 5 to 10% extra for compaction.

How do you calculate how much gravel you need?

You need three measurements: the length and width of the area in feet, and the depth you want in inches. Then:

  1. Cubic feet = length × width × (depth ÷ 12). A 20 × 10 ft area at 3 in deep is 20 × 10 × 0.25 = 50 cu ft.
  2. Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. 50 ÷ 27 = 1.85 yd³.
  3. Tons = cubic yards × density. Pea gravel and #57 stone weigh about 1.4 tons per yard, so 1.85 × 1.4 = 2.59 tons.

That's exactly what our gravel calculator does, and the example above is its default. The third step is the one people skip, and it matters because of how gravel is sold. Landscape yards often price by the cubic yard. Quarries and many stone suppliers price by the ton. If you only know yards and the quote is in tons, you can't compare.

For odd shapes, split the area into rectangles and add them up. A circle is π × radius², so a 10 ft round fire-pit area is 3.1416 × 5 × 5 = 78.5 sq ft.

How deep should gravel be?

Depth depends on what the gravel has to do. Decorative gravel just needs to hide the ground. A driveway has to carry a two-ton car without rutting.

  • Decorative beds and top-dressing: 2 in is usually enough over landscape fabric.
  • Walkways and garden paths: 2 to 3 in. Go deeper with pea gravel, which rolls underfoot.
  • Patios and shed pads: about 4 in of compacted crushed stone.
  • Driveways: 4 to 6 in total for cars. That's usually a compacted base of larger angular stone with 2 to 3 in of #57 or similar on top. Heavy trucks or soft soil call for more.
  • French drains and drainage trenches: often 6 to 12 in of clean, washed stone like #57, with no fines to clog it.

The same rule applies under concrete. Most slabs sit on about 4 in of compacted gravel, and our guide on how much concrete you need covers the slab that goes on top.

Should you buy gravel in bags or in bulk?

Bagged gravel at home centers usually comes in 0.5 cu ft bags. That's 54 bags per cubic yard, so bags only make sense for small jobs: a path under half a yard, filling low spots, or topping up an existing bed.

For anything bigger, bulk delivery is cheaper per ton and far less work. A few practical points:

  • Minimum orders. Many suppliers have a minimum delivery, often 1 to 3 yards, plus a delivery fee. Under that, a trailer pickup may be cheaper.
  • Truck size. Dump trucks commonly carry 10 to 15 cubic yards, and some suppliers use smaller trucks for residential drops. If your driveway needs 17 tons, ask whether that's one load or two.
  • Where it gets dumped. The truck drops everything in one pile. Pick a spot close to the work, on a tarp or hard surface, and not on top of your septic field or a soft lawn.

Why order extra gravel?

Gravel compacts. Crushed stone with fines can lose noticeable depth once it's run over with a plate compactor or a car, and rounded stone settles into soft soil. Excavations are also rarely perfectly even. The usual allowance is 5 to 10% extra, and the gravel calculator page recommends the same.

A leftover half ton is easy to use: fill a low spot, top up a path, or keep it for next year's driveway maintenance. Being a half ton short means paying a second delivery fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many tons of gravel do I need for a driveway?

A 12 × 50 ft single driveway needs about 10.4 tons of #57 or pea gravel at 4 in deep, and about 15.6 tons at 6 in. With crushed stone at 1.5 tons per yard, those numbers are 11.1 and 16.7 tons. Add 5 to 10% for compaction.

How much does a yard of gravel weigh?

Most gravel weighs about 1.4 to 1.5 tons (2,800 to 3,000 lb) per cubic yard. Pea gravel and #57 stone are near 1.4 tons, crushed stone near 1.5, and dense limestone can reach 1.7 tons per yard. Wet stone weighs more.

How many square feet does a ton of gravel cover?

At 2 in deep, a ton of pea gravel or #57 covers about 116 sq ft. At 3 in, it's about 77 sq ft, and at 4 in about 58 sq ft. Crushed stone covers slightly less per ton because it's denser.

How many bags of gravel equal a yard?

Most bagged gravel comes in 0.5 cu ft bags, so it takes 54 bags to make one cubic yard (27 cu ft). That's why bags only make sense for small jobs. Anything over about half a yard is usually cheaper as bulk delivery.

Should I buy gravel by the ton or by the yard?

Buy it however your supplier prices it, but convert first so you can compare quotes. Multiply cubic yards by the stone's density (about 1.4 for pea gravel) to get tons, or divide tons by density to get yards.
