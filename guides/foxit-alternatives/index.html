Foxit Alternatives, Compared (Free and No Upload)

📄 6 min readUpdated October 12, 2026

Foxit is two very different products under one name. The Foxit PDF Reader is free, and it's genuinely good at reading, commenting, filling forms and signing. Foxit PDF Editor is the paid one, and it starts at $129.99 a year. Most people searching for an alternative hit the Editor's price when all they wanted was to merge a few files or squeeze a PDF under an email limit. For those jobs you don't need either. Should you keep the free Foxit PDF Reader?

Yes, for most people. It's free for personal and business use, it runs on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, and it fills both standard AcroForm fields and XFA forms. That last point matters more than it sounds. XFA forms are the ones that show a "Please wait..." message in Chrome, and plenty of government and bank forms still use them. Our guide on filling out PDF forms explains how to spot one.

Where the Reader stops is editing. Changing a paragraph, swapping a logo or converting to Word all push you toward the paid Editor, and the Reader's download page advertises that 14-day trial right next to the free download.

Is it safe to use Foxit's online PDF tools?

For ordinary files, it's reasonable. Foxit's merge and compress pages don't ask for an account, and both say files are deleted automatically after processing. What they don't say is where the servers are or how long "after processing" lasts in practice. If you work under GDPR or a client contract that names a region, that gap is worth noticing.

There's also a small inconsistency: the merge page's title says files can be up to 10 MB, while the upload box says 20 MB. Either way, a scanned contract can blow past that quickly. Browser-based tools sidestep both problems, because there's no upload and no server-side limit. For a wider look at retention policies, see is it safe to upload PDFs online.

So which should you pick?

Keep the free Foxit PDF Reader for reading, forms and quick signatures. Use a browser tool for one-off merges, splits and compression, especially with private documents. Only pay for Foxit PDF Editor if you edit existing text often, and if you do, run the numbers: by the second year, the $209.99 perpetual licence beats the subscription. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Foxit free?

Foxit PDF Reader is free for personal and business use on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, and it reads, annotates, fills forms and signs. Foxit's online merge and compress tools are also free with no account. Foxit PDF Editor, which edits existing text and converts files, is paid and starts at $129.99 a year.

How much is Foxit PDF Editor?

As of October 2026, Foxit PDF Editor costs $129.99 per user a year and PDF Editor+ costs $159.99 per user a year. A perpetual licence for PDF Editor 14 is $209.99 once. The optional AI Assistant add-on is $49.99 a year.

Does the Foxit free trial need a credit card?

No. Foxit's pricing FAQ says every plan starts with a 14-day free trial and no credit card is required, so the trial can't roll into a paid plan by itself.

Can Foxit PDF Reader edit text?

No. The free Reader handles reading, comments, highlights, form filling and signatures. Editing existing text, images or layout needs the paid Foxit PDF Editor. For simple documents, LibreOffice Draw can edit PDF text for free.

Does Foxit keep files uploaded to its online tools?

Foxit's merge and compress pages say files are deleted automatically from its servers after processing. They don't give a retention time or say where the servers are, so use a browser-based tool that doesn't upload for sensitive documents.
