How to Print a PDF on ANSI E Paper (34 x 44)

📄 6 min readUpdated November 12, 2026

ANSI E is 34 × 44 inches, which is 2448 × 3168 points in a PDF. If your file's already that size, print it at 100%. A Letter page fits it at exactly 400%. The catch is the half-size set: ANSI D isn't half scale. An E sheet only fits D at 64.7%, and the true 50% print is ANSI C.

What size is ANSI E paper?

It's the largest of the five lettered US engineering sheets. Each size is two of the one below it, side by side.

Why doesn't every ANSI size scale cleanly to the next?

Look at the Shape column. Doubling a 17:22 sheet gives an 11:17 sheet, and doubling that gives 17:22 again. So the series flips between two shapes, which the ISO A sizes never do. Jump two sizes and the scale is exactly 200% or 50% with nothing left over. Move one size and you get a blank strip.

We built a test page at each size with 12 pt text and a one-inch square, fit it to a 34 × 44 page with PyMuPDF 1.27.2, and read both back. Every reading matched the math.

Lay out on Letter and everything prints four times as big. Want 24 pt on the finished sheet? Set 6 pt. An ANSI D drawing sent to E is the awkward one, with 5.53 in of the width left empty. One step down, a Letter page on ANSI D reaches 258.8% and leaves 5.53 in of the length blank, while Tabloid fills D at exactly 200%.

Is ANSI D a half-size print of ANSI E?

No. D is half the paper of E, but it's the other shape. At Fit, our E page landed on D at 64.7%. It printed 22 × 28.47 inches and the one-inch square measured 0.647 inches, so 12 pt text drops to 7.8 pt. The true half-size sheet for E is ANSI C, and for D it's Tabloid.

At exactly 50%, 1/4 in = 1 ft becomes 1/8 in = 1 ft, which a standard architect's scale has. At 64.7% it's about 0.162 inches to the foot, and no scale ruler reads that. An E set on D is fine for reading and red-lining, but mark it "not to scale". D-size originals are luckier: D on Tabloid is a clean 50%.

Does an ANSI E file fit Arch E or A0?

It fits both, with a strip left over. The other way means shrinking.

Which roll width takes a 34-inch sheet?

A 36-inch roll is the usual answer. It's 2 inches wider than the sheet, so the shop prints and trims. We read one spec sheet today: HP lists the DesignJet T650 36-in with rolls from 11 to 36 inches and 0.2 inches of non-printable area per edge. That leaves 35.6 printable inches. We haven't checked other models.

As a rule of thumb, shops also stock 24, 42 and 44 inch rolls. A 24-inch roll is too narrow for E but takes ANSI D, which is 22 inches wide. If a page was saved sideways, turn it with Rotate PDF before you send it.

Can you tile an ANSI E PDF onto Letter or Tabloid?

Yes. E is exactly 16 Letter sheets or 8 Tabloid sheets, but only edge to edge. We split a 34 × 44 test page into tiles at 100%, with a quarter-inch margin on every edge and no overlap, and counted the pages.

What should you tell the print shop?

Give inches, not letters. "E size" can mean 34 × 44 or 36 × 48, and "D size" can mean 22 × 34 or 24 × 36. We haven't checked any shop's stock or prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size is ANSI E paper?

ANSI E is 34 × 44 inches, about 864 × 1118 mm. In a PDF that's 2448 × 3168 points. It equals two ANSI D sheets or 16 Letter sheets.

Is ANSI E the same as 36 x 48?

No. 36 × 48 inches is Arch E. An ANSI E file fits on it at 105.9% with 1.41 in of the length left blank, and an Arch E file has to shrink to 91.7% to fit on ANSI E.

Is ANSI D half the size of ANSI E?

It's half the area, but not half scale. An E drawing fits on D at 64.7% and leaves 5.53 in of the length blank. The true 50% print of ANSI E is ANSI C, 17 × 22 inches.

What scale prints a Letter PDF on ANSI E?

Exactly 400%. Letter and ANSI E have the same 17:22 shape, so the page fills the sheet, and 12 pt text prints at 48 pt.

How many Letter sheets make an ANSI E print?

Sixteen in a 4 by 4 grid if they print edge to edge. With a quarter-inch printer margin on every side it takes 24 sheets, in a 4 by 6 grid of landscape pages.
