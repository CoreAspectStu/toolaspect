How to Calculate BMI (Body Mass Index)

💡 1.2M searches/mo💰 CPC: $2-5⏱️ 5 min read

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a screening tool used to categorize weight relative to height. While not perfect, it provides a quick health indicator.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Measure your weight in kilograms or pounds
  2. Measure your height in meters or inches
  3. Metric: BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)^2
  4. Imperial: BMI = 703 x weight (lbs) / height (in)^2
  5. Categories: Under 18.5 underweight, 18.5-24.9 normal, 25-29.9 overweight, 30+ obese

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat. Muscular individuals may score high without being unhealthy.

What BMI is healthy?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy for most adults.

Should children use BMI?

Children use percentile-based charts, not adult categories.
