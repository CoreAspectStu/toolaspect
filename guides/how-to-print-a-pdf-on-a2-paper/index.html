How to Print a PDF on A2 Paper

📄 6 min readUpdated November 4, 2026

If your printer offers A2, set that as the paper size and choose Fit. An A4 page enlarges to 200% and a Letter page to 194.5%. Most printers don't go that big, so you'll probably use a print shop or tile it across smaller sheets. Here are the numbers for each route, and what to check first.

How big is A2 paper?

A2 is 420 × 594 mm, or about 16.5 × 23.4 inches. It's the size up from A3, and it has the same shape as every other A-series sheet, so anything drawn for A4 or A3 fills it with no blank band. That isn't true of US sizes. People often mix up A2 and 18 × 24, but they aren't interchangeable.

In a PDF, A2 is about 1,191 × 1,684 points. To check your file, open Properties in your PDF app and read the page size. It's worth doing first, since a poster that's already on A4 needs scaling and one that's already on A2 doesn't.

How do you print a PDF on A2?

If your printer takes A2, it's the same two settings as any size. Set the paper size to A2 in the print dialog, then choose Fit or Actual Size. If A2 isn't on the menu, the printer driver doesn't offer it, and no PDF app can add it. Most home and office printers top out at A3 or 11 × 17, so for most people, this job goes to a print shop or gets tiled across smaller sheets.

Use Actual Size when the PDF is already A2. Fit is for everything else, and it's safest for desktop printers because it stays inside the margins the printer can't reach. With a typical quarter-inch margin, an A4 page lands at about 194% and not at a full 200%. For a scale drawing, that difference matters. Print at 100% from a true A2 file and measure a known dimension before you trust it.

The scale tells you whether your text survives, but it says nothing about sharpness. Vector text and line art stay crisp at any size. Photos and screenshots don't, because the same pixels now cover more paper.

Is your PDF sharp enough for A2?

Pixel count decides this. Here's how many pixels a full-page image needs to reach each density at A2:

Image sizeDensity at A2What to expect
1,190 × 1,684 px72 PPIVisibly soft up close
2,480 × 3,508 px150 PPIUsually fine for a poster seen from a step or two back
3,508 × 4,961 px212 PPIGood for most posters
4,961 × 7,016 px300 PPIWhat most shops ask for

These PPI levels are a rule of thumb, not a standard. Viewing distance changes the answer, which is why a banner seen from across a room can get away with far less. To check a file, zoom the PDF to 100% and see whether edges break up. Enlarging a low-resolution image won't add detail, so go back to the source if you can.

What should you give a print shop?

Ask the shop for its spec first, because its rules beat any general advice. These are the things most shops will want to know:

  • The exact finished size. Many US shops stock 18 × 24 and 24 × 36, and they may not carry A2. Ask before you pay, or have them trim a larger sheet.
  • Bleed, if color runs to the edge. A common request is 3 mm (or 0.125 in) per side, making an A2 file 426 × 600 mm. Printers trim that off, so keep important text well inside it.
  • Only the pages they need. Extract PDF Pages pulls the big sheet out of a long file.
  • The right orientation. If it's sideways on screen, fix it with Rotate PDF before you send it.
  • A manageable file. Some upload forms have size limits. Compress PDF can help, but compression can also lower image quality, so keep your original and check the result against the table above.

Printing an A2 file on an 18 × 24 sheet comes out at 102.6% and leaves 26 mm of white across the width. If you want it edge to edge, ask the shop to trim it, or crop your design with our PDF cropper and accept losing a little. Frames are the catch here. An A2 frame won't hold an 18 × 24 print, so choose your size before ordering either one.

Can you print A2 without an A2 printer?

Yes. Tile it across smaller sheets and tape them together. Assuming quarter-inch margins and no overlap, a full A2 page takes:

A little overlap can push those counts up, and you'll see the seams, so tiling suits planning layouts and sewing patterns better than display posters. Frequently Asked Questions

Is A2 bigger than 18 x 24?

No. A2 is 420 by 594 mm, and 18 by 24 inches is 457.2 by 609.6 mm, so A2 is 37.2 mm narrower and 15.6 mm shorter. An A2 file fits on an 18 by 24 sheet at up to 102.6%, with a little white space left.

What scale should I use to print A4 on A2?

Use 200%, or choose Fit. A4 and A2 share the same shape, so it fills the sheet. A printer that keeps a quarter-inch margin will bring that down to about 194%. Twelve-point text becomes about 24 pt at the full 200%.

Why is A2 not showing in my print options?

Your printer driver doesn't list it, almost always because the printer can't take paper that wide. PDF apps only show what the driver reports. You'll need a large-format printer, a print shop, or a tiled print on smaller sheets.

How many A4 sheets make A2?

Four, in a 2 by 2 grid, if the sheets touch edge to edge. A desktop printer can't print right to the edge, though, so a tiled A2 page takes 8 A4 sheets with quarter-inch margins, unless you scale it down to fit fewer.

What resolution do I need to print on A2?

At 300 PPI, a full-page image needs 4,961 by 7,016 pixels. At 150 PPI, it needs 2,480 by 3,508, which is often fine for a poster seen from a little way back. Ask your shop what it accepts.
