CamScanner Alternatives, Compared (Free and No Watermark)

📄 7 min readUpdated October 5, 2026

If CamScanner's watermark or subscription prompts are the problem, the quickest fix is the scanner your phone already has. On an iPhone, the Notes and Files apps scan to a clean PDF. On Android, Google Drive does it. Adobe Scan is a good free app if you want more control. And if you'd rather not upload anything, take normal photos and turn them into a PDF with our JPG to PDF tool, which runs in your browser.

Why do people look for a CamScanner alternative?

CamScanner was one of the most downloaded scanner apps in the world, and the edge detection is genuinely good. People leave for three reasons.

The first is the watermark. The free version adds a CamScanner watermark to exported documents and shows ads. Getting rid of both needs Premium, which software directories such as Capterra list at $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year. App store prices vary by country, so check yours before you subscribe.

The second is trust. In August 2019, Kaspersky found a Trojan dropper inside the free Android version. It sat in a third-party advertising library and could download extra malicious code. Google removed the app from the Play Store, and it came back after the developer stripped the module out. A year later, in June 2020, India banned CamScanner along with 58 other Chinese apps. None of that means today's app is malicious. But for scans of passports, tax forms and contracts, plenty of people would rather not take the chance.

The third is simply that you don't need it anymore. When CamScanner took off, phones didn't ship with a scanner. Now almost all of them do.

How do I scan a document on my phone without an app?

iPhone. Open Notes, start a new note, tap the camera or paperclip icon and choose Scan Documents. Hold the phone over the page and it'll capture automatically once the edges are found. You can do the same from the Files app: tap the three-dot menu and pick Scan Documents, and the PDF saves straight into a folder. That's the closest thing to CamScanner, and it costs nothing.

Android. Open Google Drive, tap the plus button and choose Scan. Drive finds the edges, lets you crop and pick a filter, then saves a PDF to your Drive. Samsung owners can also point the normal camera at a page and tap the scan prompt that pops up.

Both routes give you a proper PDF with no watermark. The only real difference is where the file ends up. Notes can stay on your phone, while Drive always uploads.

Can I make a PDF from photos without uploading them?

Yes. Sometimes you've already got the photos, or you're on a laptop with pictures someone texted you. In that case you don't need a scanner app at all.

Open JPG to PDF, add the images, pick a page size and press Create PDF. Every photo becomes a page in the order you set. JPEGs go into the PDF byte for byte, so there's no extra quality loss, and nothing is uploaded because the PDF is built in your browser.

Be honest with yourself about what it won't do. A scanner app squares up a page you shot at an angle and whitens the background. Our tool doesn't. So shoot from directly above, in even light, and crop away the table with the Image Cropper first. If your iPhone saved the photos as HEIC, convert them with HEIC to JPG before you start.

Is a phone photo sharp enough to replace a scanner?

Usually, yes. The number that matters is DPI, meaning how many pixels land on each inch of paper. Around 300 DPI is the standard for print and for text recognition. Here's what common cameras give you on a Letter or A4 page. The second and third columns assume the page fills 80% of the frame, which is what you get when you leave a little margin around it.

A 48 MP shot gives you more detail than you need and a much bigger file, so if size matters, run the finished PDF through Compress PDF.

How do I get searchable text out of a scan?

A scan is just a picture of a page. To copy or search the words, something has to read them. That's OCR.

On an iPhone, Live Text already works on scans in Notes, so you can select text right on the page. Google Drive and OneDrive run their own recognition after upload. Adobe Scan adds a text layer to the PDF, with a page cap on its free tier.

If you'd rather keep the file local, our OCR PDF runs the open-source Tesseract engine in your browser. It reads each page and gives you a text transcript to copy or download. It doesn't write a searchable layer back into the PDF, so it's for getting the words out rather than making the file searchable. For single photos, Image to Text does the same job.

Which CamScanner alternative should you pick?

For most people the answer is whatever's already on the phone: Notes or Files on an iPhone, Google Drive on Android. They're free, they don't watermark, and they're good enough for receipts, forms and contracts.

Pick Adobe Scan if you scan a lot and want stronger cleanup and OCR, and you don't mind an Adobe account. Pick OneDrive if you lived in Microsoft Lens and want the nearest replacement. And use the browser route, JPG to PDF followed by OCR PDF, when the document is sensitive enough that you'd rather it never touched anyone's server. Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free scanner app without a watermark?

Yes, and you probably have one already. The Notes and Files apps on an iPhone scan straight to PDF with no watermark, Google Drive does the same on Android, and Adobe Scan's free tier doesn't stamp your pages either. CamScanner's free version is the one known for adding a watermark.

Is CamScanner safe to use now?

In August 2019 Kaspersky found a malicious module hidden in an advertising library inside the free Android version, and Google pulled the app from the Play Store until it was fixed. The developer removed the module and the app returned. India also banned CamScanner in June 2020 along with 58 other Chinese apps. Whether that history matters to you is your call, but the free built-in scanners avoid the question.

What replaced Microsoft Lens?

Microsoft retired Lens. It was removed from the app stores on February 9, 2026, and scanning in the app stopped on March 9, 2026. Microsoft points people to the scan feature in the OneDrive app instead.

Can I turn phone photos into a PDF without uploading them?

Yes. ToolAspect's JPG to PDF builds the PDF inside your browser, so the photos never leave your phone or computer. It doesn't straighten or crop pages for you, so crop each photo first with an image cropper or take the photos straight on.

What resolution do I need for a readable scan?

Around 300 DPI is the usual target for print and text recognition. A 12-megapixel phone photo of a Letter page that fills about 80% of the frame works out to roughly 285 DPI, which is close enough. Older 2 MP cameras land near 110 DPI, which is too soft for small print.
