How Much Does Remote Start Installation Cost?

🚗 5-10K searches/mo💰 CPC: $1.50-4⏱️ 7 min read

Remote start pricing looks simpler than it is. The ad says $199, the quote says $475, and both are honest numbers for different jobs. The gap comes down to three things: which tier of remote you're buying, whether your car needs a bypass module (most do), and how much wiring your particular model hides behind the dash. Here's what each piece costs in 2025-26 and how to price your own car before you call a shop.

Compustar's own dealer survey puts the average quality system-plus-installation at $319.99 to $449.99, and that's the honest middle of the market. The OEM accessory route costs the most for the least range, but it's the only clean option for some leased cars since the dealer fob keeps working like factory.

Why does the bypass module keep showing up on quotes?

Almost every car since the late 1990s has an immobilizer chip in the key. The remote starter has to convince the car a valid key is in the ignition, and the little box that does it is the bypass or data module — $20 to $100, mandatory on most newer vehicles. This is the number-one reason a "$249 installed" ad becomes a $400 invoice. When you call a shop, ask one question: "Is the bypass included in that price?" Good shops quote it bundled; the cheapest ads don't.

Which vehicles cost more to install on?

  • Push-to-start and chip-key cars: +$20-$100 for the bypass module. Nearly everything built in the last 15 years.
  • European cars (BMW, Audi, VW, Mercedes): +$100-$300 in labor. Databus wiring is denser and diagrams are guarded; fewer shops want the job, so those that do charge for it.
  • Manual transmissions: +$50-$150 for neutral-safety and parking-brake sensors. The car won't remote-start unless it's left in neutral with the brake set.
  • Diesels: +$50-$100 for glow-plug delay programming.
  • Hybrids and EVs: only select systems support cabin climate without an engine start; call a specialist shop before buying anything.

A worked example: a 2021 Honda Accord with push-button start and a 2-way LCD system runs $399.99 + $75 = $474.99 installed. The same system on a VW Golf is $399.99 + $150 = $549.99, and big-metro shops quote $600-plus. Same remote, different dash.

Is DIY worth it?

The parts for a 2-way system plus bypass run about $249.98 ($189.99 kit + $59.99 module) versus $475 installed, so you're saving roughly $225 in labor. Whether that's a good trade depends on you: the job needs a multimeter, a wiring diagram for your exact model year, soldered or properly crimped connections, and four to eight patient hours. First-timers on push-to-start cars routinely spend a full weekend. Get it wrong and you're into no-start diagnostics, a drained battery, or a trip to the shop anyway — now paying labor plus repair.

The middle path most people land on: buy the kit online at whatever price you like, then pay a shop $100-$200 to install customer-provided hardware. Not every shop accepts customer parts, but the ones that do are often the better installers anyway.

Will it void my warranty?

No, and a dealer who says otherwise is misstating the law. Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, a warranty claim can only be denied if the aftermarket part actually caused the failure. A remote start that's properly installed with soldered connections doesn't cause failures; a hack job with scotch locks melting into a harness can. Keep the receipt and the shop's workmanship warranty (good ones offer lifetime labor). Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I pay for a remote start installed?

Plan on $319.99 to $449.99 for a quality 2-way Compustar or Viper system installed at an authorized shop, which matches Compustar's own 2025 dealer survey. Budget 1-way jobs run $199-$350, and a push-to-start car adds $20-$100 for the bypass module. Anything under $200 installed is usually a bare 1-way kit with no bypass on an older, simple vehicle.

Why do quotes for the same car vary by $200?

Three things: whether the bypass module is included in the quote, the shop's labor rate ($100-$150/hr is typical), and whether they're quoting a 1-way or 2-way remote. A $249 quote and a $475 quote for the same Accord are often the same hardware with different fine print. Ask each shop to quote the exact tier with bypass included.

Does remote start work on manual transmission cars?

Yes with a compatible system and the right safety sensors, which add $50-$150 to the install. The system checks the car is in neutral with the parking brake set before it will remote-start. Modern systems like Compustar's DAS-II handle this reliably; the myth that manuals can't remote start dates from 1990s kits.

Is the smartphone module worth the subscription?

If you park within key-fob range, no. If you park in a structure at the far end of a campus or want lock-status alerts while traveling, the $99 module plus $50-$70 a year is cheaper than a second key fob and works from anywhere. Over three years it adds about $280 to the cost of the system.
