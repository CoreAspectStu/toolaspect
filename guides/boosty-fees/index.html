How Much Does Boosty Take in Fees?

💡 12K searches/mo on Boosty💰 6 min read

Boosty is the subscription platform that undercuts the majors on price — and the discount is real, but it comes layered: a commission on everything you earn, a second fee when you withdraw, and a payout system built around Russian rails. Here's each layer with numbers, and what actually lands in your account.

What percentage does Boosty take?

Start with the headline: Boosty's commission on creator income runs roughly 10% as the service's own cut. But what creators actually see deducted is higher, because Boosty folds the payment processors' fees into the same line. Boosty's own FAQ puts the combined figure at 11.7%. Independent reviews of the platform land between 10% and 15% depending on how fans choose to pay — some payment methods cost more to process than others.

That commission applies across the board: monthly subscriptions, one-off donations, and sales of individual posts or content. There's no separate cheaper tier for one income type.

What does withdrawing cost?

The commission is only the first layer. Getting money out is the second:

  • Card withdrawal: about 1.4% plus a fixed fee of roughly 20 RUB per payout.
  • Other withdrawal methods price differently — check the payout screen in your dashboard for the exact list.
  • International creators add their own bank's receiving and currency-conversion costs on top, which Boosty doesn't control but you certainly pay.

The practical consequence: withdraw less often. A fixed fee of 20 RUB hurts weekly payouts far more than monthly ones. Two withdrawals a month instead of eight halves your fixed-fee bill without changing anything else.

What changed in February 2026?

Boosty announced a commission unification effective February 20, 2026. The stated reasons: a shifting economic environment, growing tax burden, and rising infrastructure and security costs. The platform moved to a single unified commission structure — ending the patchwork where the effective rate varied by payment method.

For a creator, the action item is simple: open your dashboard and read the rate that now applies to you. Everything in this guide uses Boosty's published FAQ figure (11.7%) and review-site ranges (10-15%); if your dashboard says 12% or 9%, believe the dashboard and plug that into the Boosty calculator.

The take-home math on a real example

A mid-size Boosty creator: 200 subscribers at $4.99, $150 in monthly donations, $80 in content sales. Gross is $1,228. The 11.7% commission takes $143.68. The 1.4% card payout takes another $15.18. Net: $1,069.14 a month, $12,830 a year, an effective total cut of 12.94%.

Run the same gross through a 20% platform with the same payout fee and you net $968.65. Boosty's cheaper commission is worth $100.50 a month at this size — $1,206 a year, enough to matter, not enough to base the entire platform decision on.

How is Boosty income taxed?

Boosty doesn't withhold anything. You're paid as an independent earner, and the tax question is between you and your jurisdiction. Russian creators commonly register under the self-employment regime, which taxes professional income at a low flat rate but requires registration and reporting; creators elsewhere declare the income under their local self-employment or business rules. Whatever applies to you, the habit that survives every jurisdiction: set aside a slice of each payout before spending it. A quarter to a third is the usual cushion for self-employed income.

The bottom line

Boosty costs roughly 11.7% all-in on what you earn plus about 1.4% and a small fixed fee to withdraw — call it 13% total friction on a card payout. That undercuts OnlyFans and Fansly by seven points and trades blows with Patreon. Verify your post-February-2026 rate on the dashboard, batch your withdrawals, price in your own bank's conversion costs, and model the full stack with the Boosty calculator before committing to the platform as your primary or your backup.

Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage does Boosty take?

About 11.7% combined, according to Boosty's own FAQ — that figure includes the service's commission and payment-system fees together. Independent reviews put the total between 10% and 15% depending on how fans pay.

Does Boosty charge to withdraw money?

Yes. Withdrawals are separate from the commission: card payouts cost about 1.4% plus a fixed fee of roughly 20 RUB. International creators also absorb their own bank's conversion and receiving costs.

Is Boosty cheaper than OnlyFans?

On commission, clearly: Boosty's 10-12% versus OnlyFans' 20%. On $1,228 of monthly gross, that gap is worth about $100 a month. But OnlyFans offers far larger native discovery, and Boosty's payout rails are built around Russian banks — cheaper cut, different friction.

Did Boosty change its fees in 2026?

Yes. Boosty announced a commission unification effective February 20, 2026, citing rising tax burden and infrastructure costs. The platform framed it as moving to a single unified rate; check your creator dashboard for the number that now applies to you.
