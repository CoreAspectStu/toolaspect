How to Print a PDF on 11 × 17 Paper

📄 6 min readUpdated November 1, 2026

Choose Tabloid (or Ledger for landscape) as the paper size, then pick Fit. A Letter page enlarges to 129.4%, and an 11 × 17 page shrinks to 64.7% on Letter. Here's where the setting lives in each app, what the scaling does to your text, and why some printers never offer the size.

Is 11 × 17 the same as tabloid, ledger and A3?

Tabloid and 11 × 17 are the same sheet. Ledger is that sheet turned sideways, which is why print dialogs list "Tabloid" for portrait and "Ledger" for landscape. A3 is the international cousin and it's close, but not the same: 17.6 mm narrower and 11.8 mm shorter, so a Tabloid file printed on A3 gets scaled to 97.3% unless you crop it.

How do I set the paper size to 11 × 17?

Every app has two separate controls, and the mistake people make is setting one and not the other. One is the paper size, which tells the printer what's in the tray. The other is the scale, which decides how big the page is drawn on that paper. Here's where both live:

If Tabloid isn't in the paper size menu, the printer driver doesn't offer it, and no setting in the PDF app will add it. Most home inkjets stop at Legal. Office copiers, A3 inkjets and any print shop will have it.

How do I enlarge a Letter PDF to fill 11 × 17?

Pick Tabloid as the paper and choose Fit (or "Fit to printable area"). The page scales to 129.4%, limited by width: 8.5 in becomes 11 in exactly, while 11 in becomes 14.24 in, leaving 2.76 in of blank paper split between the top and bottom. Twelve-point body text comes out at 15.5 pt. Nothing is cut off, but the proportions of Letter and Tabloid differ, so the fit is never edge to edge.

The other way to use the sheet is to put two Letter pages side by side at 100%. Tabloid is exactly two Letter sheets, so set the paper to Ledger (landscape) and choose 2 pages per sheet, as we show in our guide to printing multiple pages on one sheet. We checked both layouts by building the pages in PyMuPDF: a Letter page placed in half of a 17 × 11 sheet lands at precisely 100%.

How do I print an 11 × 17 PDF on Letter paper?

Fit does it, at 64.7%. The height is the limit this time: 17 in shrinks to 11 in and 11 in shrinks to 7.12 in, leaving 1.38 in of blank margin across the two sides. Twelve-point text drops to 7.8 pt, which is legible for a poster headline and painful for a spreadsheet.

If the result is too small, you have three options. Print on Legal paper instead, which only gets you to 77.3%. Tile it across four Letter sheets at full size, which our poster printing guide walks through. Or trim the file down to the part you need with the PDF cropper and print that at a larger scale.

Why does my 11 × 17 print come out small or cut off?

  • Paper set to Letter, file is Tabloid. With "Actual size" selected, the printer prints the middle of the page and drops the rest. Set the paper size, or switch to Fit.
  • Orientation mismatch. A landscape 17 × 11 page sent as portrait Tabloid shrinks to fit the short way. Choose Ledger, or turn the pages with the PDF rotator so the file and the paper agree.
  • Mixed page sizes in one file. A Letter cover followed by Tabloid drawings prints every page on whatever tray you chose. Acrobat's "Choose paper source by PDF page size" sorts this; Chrome and Preview don't. Our guide on why PDF pages come out different sizes explains how to check.
  • Printer margins. Most printers can't reach the last 0.17 to 0.25 in at each edge, and "Fit to printable area" shrinks the page a little more to respect that. Only borderless modes avoid it.

Where can I get 11 × 17 printed?

Office multifunction printers from the big copier brands nearly all take it. Among desktop machines, look for "wide format" or "A3" inkjets; many of them list 11 × 17 as a supported size, but check the spec sheet, because Tabloid is 12 mm longer than A3 and some A3 trays stop there. Copy shops and office-supply stores print Tabloid as a standard size, and they'll want a single PDF, so combine your pages with the PDF merger first and set the page size you expect in the file name so nobody scales it by mistake.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size is 11x17 paper called?

Tabloid when it's portrait and Ledger when it's landscape. Both names refer to the same 11 by 17 inch sheet (279.4 by 431.8 mm). It's exactly twice the size of Letter paper. A3 is similar but a different size: 297 by 420 mm.

Can I print 11x17 on a regular printer?

Only if the printer supports it, and most home inkjets and laser printers stop at Legal (8.5 by 14 in). If Tabloid isn't in the paper size list of your print dialog, the printer can't take it. Office copiers and A3 inkjets can.

How do I print a Letter PDF on 11x17 paper?

Set the paper size to Tabloid and choose Fit or Fit to printable area. The page enlarges to 129.4%, with 8.5 in becoming 11 in. Because the two sizes have different proportions, about 2.76 in of blank paper is left top and bottom.

How do I shrink an 11x17 PDF to Letter?

Set the paper size to Letter and choose Fit. The page prints at 64.7%, so 12-point text becomes about 7.8 points. For a bigger result, tile it across four Letter sheets using the poster option in Acrobat Reader.

Is A3 the same as 11x17?

No. A3 is 297 by 420 mm, while 11 by 17 is 279.4 by 431.8 mm. A3 is wider and shorter. A Tabloid PDF printed on A3 scales to 97.3%, and an A3 PDF printed on Tabloid scales to 94.1%.
