How Much Do Wedding Flowers Cost? The Per-Piece Rate Card

💐 14K searches/mo💰 CPC: $3.50⏱️ 7 min read

Wedding flowers average $2,800 (The Knot), full-service florists run $5,100-$7,600 (Zola), and most couples budget $2,500-$8,000. The math underneath is simple: per-piece prices times your counts — bridal bouquet $150-$350, bridesmaid bouquets $50-$200, centerpieces $75-$300 a table, arch installs $800-$3,000+, plus delivery and setup. A 12-table wedding with the venue's own altar decor lands at $2,861, right on the national average.

Where the money actually goes

Not the bouquet. Run the standard order — 12 guest tables, 4 bridesmaids, 5 boutonnieres, 3 corsages — and the centerpieces alone run $1,800, about 63% of the $2,861 total. The bouquet everyone talks about is $225 of it. That mismatch is the single most useful thing to know before a florist consultation: the pieces the photographer shoots (bouquet, arch, head table) and the pieces filling the room (guest-table centerpieces) are different budget decisions.

Labor is the other half of the story. A florist's invoice is typically 60-70% design time, conditioning stems, delivery, installation, and the after-party strike — the flowers themselves are the minority of what you pay. Perishable product carries a waste factor, and a Saturday wedding compresses a week of work into one morning delivery window. That's why the delivery-and-setup line ($100-$500) exists even on small orders, and why two venues (ceremony here, reception there) push it to the top of the band or double it.

Three ways to cut the flower bill

Reprioritize, don't shrink. Move money from guest-table centerpieces to the arch and bouquet: one statement installation photographs as more luxurious than fifteen medium arrangements at the same price, and candles-plus-greenery fill tables at $10-$60 each.

Ride the seasons. In-season domestic flowers cost a fraction of imports — peonies in June are reasonable and impossible in December. Ask the florist what's peak that month and design around it; a good one answers instantly.

Watch the replication. Every bridesmaid bouquet, boutonniere, and corsage is a real line ($10-$175 each). Halving the corsage list (mothers and grandmothers only) or letting attendants carry single stems saves hundreds on big parties.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do wedding flowers cost on average?

About $2,800 per The Knot; Zola puts full-service florists at $5,100-$7,600, and Poppy's dataset of 7,500+ real weddings averages $3,500 with most couples budgeting $2,500-$8,000. As a budget share, flowers and decor together run about 9% of the average $34,200 wedding, or roughly $3,100 including rental decor.

What does a bridal bouquet cost?

$150-$350 is the standard range ($225 typical), with luxury designs reaching $1,000. Bridesmaid bouquets run $50-$200 each, roughly half to two-thirds of the bridal price. Despite being the most-photographed piece, the bouquet is a small share of the total — centerpieces and ceremony installations are where flower budgets actually go.

How much should centerpieces cost per table?

The standard benchmark is $100-$200 per guest table. Simple greenery-and-candle designs run about $125-$130, mid-size mixed arrangements $150-$300, and lush full designs $300-$650, with $50 the DIY floor. Twelve tables at $150 each is $1,800 — typically the single largest flower line and about two-thirds of an average order.

How much does a floral arch cost?

A small altar or arch arrangement runs $200-$800; a full installation covering the structure in flowers runs $800-$3,000+, with the grandest installs inside Zola's $5,100-$7,600 full-service band. Halo and asymmetrical designs — a floral semicircle or off-center cascade behind the couple — are the popular mid-option at roughly $400-$1,200.

How far in advance should I book a wedding florist?

Eight to twelve months out for peak season (May-October), when the best florists book up; six months can work off-peak. Bring your guest count, table count, venue photos, and inspiration images to the consultation — florists quote from piece lists, so the more concrete the counts, the tighter the quote.

Is greenery cheaper than flowers for wedding decor?

Usually, yes. Greenery-heavy designs typically cost less than equivalent bloom-heavy ones, which is why the $125 greenery-and-candle centerpiece undercuts the $150-$300 mixed arrangement. Eucalyptus, ruscus, and ferns cost a fraction of roses per stem and cover more table. The exceptions are in-season local blooms in peak months — garden roses in June can beat imported greenery garlands.
