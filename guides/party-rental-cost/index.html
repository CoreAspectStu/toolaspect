How Much Do Party Rentals Cost? Tents, Tables, Chairs, and the Fees Between

🎪 10-20K searches/mo💰 CPC: $3-8⏱️ 8 min read

Renting for a backyard party runs in two layers: the tent, then everything under it. Tents go for $250-$400 (20x20) to $1,000-$2,000 (40x60) for the day. Under it: chairs $1.25-$3 each, round tables $8-$18, linens $6-$20, dance floors $1.50-$4 per square foot. A 100-guest reception stacks to $2,515 typical ($1,765-$3,980); a 30-guest cookout stays under $500.

How much does it cost to rent tables and chairs?

Furniture is the easy part of the quote. Resin chairs, the white or black stacking standard, rent for $1.25-$3 each depending on market and color. Round 60-inch tables (seats 8-10) run $8-$18, and 8-foot banquet tables for buffet lines run $8-$15. Do the multiplication before you compare quotes: 100 guests need 10 rounds and 100 chairs, roughly $320 typical in furniture.

Linens deserve their own line. Basic polyester cloths rent for $6-$20 each, with 120-inch rounds for 60-inch tables around $12 typical. Premium fabrics run $15-$40, and that per-table difference is where near-identical quotes diverge. Many yards sell a table + chair + linen bundle at $12-$20 per guest; if you're renting premium linens anyway, the bundle usually wins.

What size tent do you need?

Tent sizing follows three planning rules that every rental yard uses:

  • Seated at 60-inch rounds: 10-12 sq ft per guest
  • Standing cocktail party: 6-8 sq ft per guest
  • Dance floor: 2-4.5 sq ft per guest, plus real space for a head table and bar

Frame tents (no center poles, more layout freedom) price above pole tents at the same size, and clear-top premiums can double the number in some markets. If your yard quotes a 40x60 at $1,200 flat with setup included, that's a fair deal in most of the country.

A worked stack: the 100-guest backyard reception

Here's the full order, priced at typical rates: 40x60 tent ($1,400), 10 round tables ($120), 100 chairs ($200), 10 linens ($120), 300 sq ft dance floor at $2 per square foot ($600), local delivery ($75). Total: $2,515, with honest ranges running $1,765-$3,980 depending on market. Add catering, and you begin to see why venues charge what they charge.

The other end: 30-guest graduation cookout. A 20x20 ($325), 3 rounds ($36), 30 chairs ($60), 3 linens ($36), delivery included. Total: $457. Renting beats almost any venue the moment your crowd is small and your yard exists.

What fees show up after the quote?

Delivery and setup run $50-$150 when not included; many yards include it within 15-25 miles. Tent permits cost $50-$300 where towns require them, and the yard usually handles the paperwork if you ask early. Damage waivers add roughly 8-12% on some orders. Watch the cancellation window too: items cut inside 72 hours of delivery are often billed in full, so finalize the guest count before signing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to rent tables and chairs for 100 guests?

Chairs run $1.25-$3 each (about $2 typical), so 100 chairs cost around $200. Ten 60-inch round tables at $8-$18 each add about $120, and ten linens at $6-$20 add another $120. The furniture stack for 100 guests lands near $440 typical before the tent, floor, and delivery. Many yards bundle a table, chair, and linen at $12-$20 per guest ($1,200-$2,000 for 100), which is worth comparing when you want premium linens anyway.

What size tent do I need for my guest count?

Plan 10-12 square feet per guest seated at 60-inch rounds, 6-8 for standing cocktail style, and add 2-4.5 square feet per guest for a dance floor. A 20x20 tent (400 sq ft) seats 32-40; a 20x30 seats 48-60; a 20x40 seats 64-80; a 40x60 seats 160-200 with room to dance. For 100 seated guests with dancing, budget 1,700 sq ft total including the head table and bar, which means a 40x60.

How much does a tent rental cost for a day?

Small canopies (10x10) run $50-$150. A 20x20 rents for $250-$400, a 20x30 for $300-$500, a 20x40 for $450-$800, and a 40x60 for $1,000-$2,000 in most markets. The biggest structures climb fast: 40x80 frame tents quote $1,800-$3,400, and clear-top versions in some Connecticut-class markets reach past $3,300 for a 40x40. Most day rates cover setup and teardown within the yard's delivery radius.

Is it cheaper to rent a tent or book a venue?

For 100 guests, the full backyard rental stack runs about $2,515 typical ($1,765-$3,980): tent, tables, chairs, linens, a 300 sq ft dance floor, and local delivery. Saturday-night venue rentals commonly start at $3,000-$6,000 before catering minimums, so the yard usually wins if you have the space. The tent math sours once you add rented restrooms, generators for catering, and sidewalls or heaters in cooler months.

What fees do party rental companies add?

Delivery and setup run $50-$150 when they're not included (many yards include it within 15-25 miles). Tent permits, where towns require them, cost $50-$300. Damage waivers, where offered, add a small percentage of the order in exchange for forgiving normal wear. And last-minute changes are expensive: yards often charge full price for items canceled inside 72 hours of delivery, so lock the guest count before you sign.
