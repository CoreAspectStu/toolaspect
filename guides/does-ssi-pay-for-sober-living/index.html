Does SSI Pay for Sober Living or a Halfway House?

📄 6 min readUpdated November 3, 2026

Not directly, but you can spend your SSI on rent, and paying your own way usually protects the full $994 check. The cuts come when someone else pays, when the rent is very low, or when the place can legally confine you. Here's how each setup works, with 2026 and 2027 numbers.

Does SSI pay for sober living?

Not directly. SSA doesn't have a sober living benefit and it doesn't send money to a house. SSI is a monthly cash payment made to you, or to your representative payee, and rent is one of the things it's meant to cover. The federal rate is $994 in 2026 and rises to $1,029 in January 2027, and some states add a supplement.

So the useful question is whether moving in changes your check. Usually it doesn't, as long as you pay your own way and nobody can lock the door on you.

Will sober living rent reduce your SSI check?

Rent you pay yourself isn't income, so it can't reduce your check. The cut comes when someone else covers your shelter, or when your rent is so low that SSA counts the difference as support. SSA calls this a business arrangement test: if your required rent is at least the presumed maximum value, $351.33 in 2026 and $363 in 2027, you're not receiving support at all.

Is a halfway house treated differently from sober living?

It can be, and the difference is confinement. People use "halfway house" and "sober living" for the same building, but SSA looks at what the place can do to you:

Does going to rehab stop your SSI?

Length isn't what counts. The $0 and $30 rules only apply for a month you're there from the first day through the last, so most short stays never trigger them:

Does Medicaid pay for the room?

Usually not. Medicaid can pay for licensed treatment, such as counseling at a clinic, but a sober living house is housing and not a medical service. North Dakota's Medicaid policy spells this out: providers may charge residents for room and board, and Medicaid won't reimburse it. Rules differ by state and a few programs do help with recovery housing, so call your state Medicaid agency or your plan.

That usually leaves rent to your check. Use our rent calculator to see what your income can carry, and ask whether the fee includes food, utilities and drug testing. A house that wants you to sign over your check is a warning sign. Only an SSA-appointed payee can manage your benefit for you. Having over $2,000 on the first of a month makes you ineligible, so track it with the net worth calculator.

What should you tell Social Security?

Report the move, the new rent and anyone who helps pay it by the 10th day of the following month. Keep a signed lease or house agreement that shows the required rent and your receipts, because that's what proves a business arrangement. If you're newly applying, expect one more question. When there's medical evidence of drug or alcohol use, SSA has to decide whether you'd still be disabled if you stopped. If you would be, addiction doesn't block benefits. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use SSI to pay for sober living?

Yes. SSI is cash paid to you or your payee, and you can spend it on rent at a sober living house. SSA doesn't pay the house directly, and it doesn't have a special sober living benefit. Paying your own required rent doesn't reduce your check as long as the rent is at least $351.33 a month in 2026.

Do I lose SSI if I live in a halfway house?

Not always. You'd get nothing for a full calendar month only if the place is a public institution, or a private one acting for corrections, meaning it has authority to physically confine you. A voluntary house with no such authority follows the normal rules.

Does SSI stop during a 30-day rehab?

Usually not. The rule looks at whole calendar months, so a 30-day stay that runs from the middle of one month to the middle of the next doesn't trigger it. If you do stay a whole month in a Medicaid-paid medical facility, the limit is $30, unless you qualify for the short-stay exception.

What if my parents pay my sober living rent?

SSA counts that as in-kind support. If they pay it all, the presumed value of $351.33 applies in 2026, and your federal check falls to $662.67 if you have no other income. If they pay less than that and you can prove it, SSA counts the actual amount.

Can a sober living house take my SSI check?

It can charge you rent, but it can't take control of your benefit unless SSA has appointed it as your representative payee. If someone is pressuring you to sign over your check or hand over your card, contact Social Security at 800-772-1213.
