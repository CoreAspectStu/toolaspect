pdfFiller Alternatives: Free Ways to Fill and Sign PDFs

📄 7 min readUpdated October 9, 2026

pdfFiller is a capable online PDF editor, owned by airSlate, that a lot of people land on from a search for "fill PDF online". Then they hit the sign-up wall: you start a 30-day trial with a card, and if you forget to cancel, you've got a subscription. If all you need is to fill in one form, sign it and send it back, you don't need any of that. Your browser can fill most forms for free, and our Add Text to PDF and Sign PDF tools handle the rest without uploading anything. Here's the full picture.

Is your PDF a fillable form or a flat one?

This decides which free tool works. Open the file and click on a blank line. If a text cursor appears in a highlighted box, it's a fillable form with real fields (government and tax forms usually are). If nothing happens, it's a flat PDF, often a scan or a Word file someone exported, and you'll need to place text on top of the page instead of typing into fields.

Why does my filled form look blank when I send it?

This is the most common complaint with free form filling, and it's not your fault. Some phone apps and email previews don't display form field values, only the page underneath. The fix is to flatten the PDF before sending, which turns your answers into part of the page itself. Print-to-PDF does the same job in a pinch, though it can drop links and make the text harder to select.

Is it safe to fill forms on an upload site?

Forms tend to carry exactly the details you'd least want leaked: Social Security numbers, dates of birth, bank details, medical history. pdfFiller and other online editors process your file on their servers. That's normal for cloud software, but for a tax or medical form a tool that runs on your device is the safer choice. Our guide on whether it's safe to upload PDFs online goes through what the big services keep and for how long.

When is pdfFiller worth paying for?

If you build fillable forms from scratch, edit other people's PDFs every week, or send forms out to clients and need them back signed, a subscription can save real time. If you fill a few forms a year, it isn't. Your browser and a couple of free tools will cover it, and you won't need to remember to cancel anything. Frequently Asked Questions

Is pdfFiller free?

No. pdfFiller offers a 30-day free trial, and you need to enter a payment card to start it. After that it's a paid subscription, with Basic at $8 a month, Plus at $12 and Premium at $15 when billed yearly, according to pdfFiller's own blog in 2026. Paying monthly costs more.

How do I cancel a pdfFiller free trial?

Go to My Account, then Subscription and payment, and cancel there. pdfFiller's own guide says to cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, otherwise the trial turns into a paid subscription automatically. Keep the confirmation email.

What's the easiest free way to fill a PDF form?

Open it in your browser. Chrome, Edge and Firefox can all type into fillable PDF form fields and save the result, and Mac Preview does the same. If the form is a scan with no fields, use a tool that places text anywhere on the page, such as our Add Text to PDF tool.

Can I sign a PDF for free without pdfFiller?

Yes. Our Sign PDF tool lets you draw, type or upload a signature and place it on any page in your browser. Adobe Acrobat Reader's Fill and Sign and Mac Preview's signature tool are free too. If you need other people to sign and want an audit trail, look at a dedicated e-signature service.

How do I stop someone editing a form after I fill it?

Flatten it. Flattening turns filled-in form fields into ordinary page content, so the values can't be changed by clicking into a field. Our Flatten PDF tool does this in your browser. It isn't tamper-proof, but it stops casual edits.
