How to Print a PDF on A0 Paper

📄 6 min readUpdated November 11, 2026

A0 is 841 × 1189 mm, which is 2384 × 3370 points in a PDF. If your file's already that size, print it at 100%. If it's A4, Fit takes it to 400%, and 12 pt text comes out at 48 pt. Below are measured scales for other sizes, tile counts, pixel needs and what to ask a print shop.

How big is A0 paper?

A0 is the top of the ISO A series. Its area is one square metre, or 999,949 mm² once the sides are rounded to whole millimetres. It's close to the big US sheets and matches none of them.

Arch E is wider and longer than A0, ANSI E is wider and shorter, and B0 is the same shape with 41% more area. An A0 file fits Arch E at 102.5% with 52 mm of blank width, and it has to shrink to 94% to get onto ANSI E. Our 36 × 48 guide covers Arch E.

What scale takes A4, A3, A2 or A1 to A0?

Halve an A-series sheet across its long side and you get the next size down, in the same shape. Each step is a factor of √2 in length: 141.4% up, 70.7% down. Two steps is 200% or 50%, and four steps, A4 to A0, is 400%.

We built a test page at each size with 12 pt text and a 25.4 mm square, fit it to the target with PyMuPDF 1.27.2, and read both back. Every reading matched the math.

The figures sit a hair off the round numbers because the official sizes are rounded to whole millimetres. Four A4 widths make 840 mm, not 841. The blank strip is about 1 mm at most.

Text grows by the same factor. Design on A4 and 12 pt body text prints at 48 pt. If you want 24 pt on the finished A0, that's 6 pt on the A4 layout, and a 72 pt title is 18 pt. Going down, A0 on A3 turns 12 pt into 4.2 pt.

What happens to a drawing's scale when you shrink A0?

The scale written in the title block stops being true. In our test, the 25.4 mm square on an A0 page measured 17.94 mm after Fit to A1, so 1:100 had become 1:141.6. No scale ruler has that. Two steps is the clean one: A0 on A2 lands on 1:200 to within 0.1%.

Mark any other reduction "not to scale". A bar scale drawn on the sheet shrinks with the drawing, so it stays right. For a true-size print, choose 100% and check a known dimension with a ruler.

Will a Letter, Arch or slide PDF fill A0?

Widescreen slides are the worst case. If you're building the poster in a slide app, set the slide to 84.1 × 118.9 cm before you lay anything out, because changing it later shifts or stretches the content. Filling the sheet from the wrong shape means cutting something off. A Letter page needs 425.6% and 78 mm of the enlarged width gets trimmed. Crop PDF lets you pick what goes.

Is A0 the right size for a conference poster?

Often, but check. A0 portrait is a common request for academic posters, and some events want landscape, A1 or a US size. We haven't checked any conference's rules, so read yours before you design. If a finished page is sideways, Rotate PDF turns it, but it can't re-flow a portrait layout for a landscape board.

How many pixels does an A0 print need?

Vector text and charts stay sharp at any size. Photos and screenshots don't. Here's what a full-sheet image needs:

As a rule of thumb, 100 to 150 PPI is fine for a poster read from a metre or two away, and 300 is for close inspection. A 12-megapixel photo at 4,000 × 3,000 pixels works out to 85.4 PPI along A0's long side. JPEG compression makes real files much smaller than the last column. If yours is still too big to upload, Compress PDF can shrink it, though it can soften images, so check the result.

Can you print A0 without an A0 printer?

You can tile it. We counted sheets with a quarter-inch margin on every edge and no overlap:

In theory A0 is 16 A4 sheets. Margins push that to 21, and even two A1 prints become 4 unless they're borderless. Overlap for gluing can add a row, and the seams will show. Our multi-page poster guide has the steps, and the A2 guide covers a smaller metric sheet.

What should you tell the print shop?

  • Size. Say 841 × 1189 mm, and portrait or landscape.
  • Roll width. A 36-inch roll is 914.4 mm, which is 73.4 mm wider than A0, so A0 fits across one with trimming. Stock varies, so ask.
  • Bleed. For color to the edge, a common request is 3 mm a side, making the file 847 × 1195 mm. That's a rule of thumb. The shop's spec wins.
  • One page only. If the poster sits inside a longer file, Extract PDF Pages pulls it out.
  • Paper and price. We haven't checked any shop's stock or prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size is A0 paper in inches and points?

A0 is 841 × 1189 mm, about 33.11 × 46.81 inches. In a PDF that's 2384 × 3370 points. Its area is one square metre.

What scale do I use to print A4 on A0?

Use 400%, or choose Fit. Both sheets have the same shape, so the page fills A0. We measured 400.3%, since the sizes are rounded to whole millimetres, and 12 pt text came out at 48 pt.

Is A0 the same as 36 x 48 inches?

No. 36 × 48 inches is Arch E, which is 914 × 1219 mm. An A0 file fits on it at 102.5% and leaves about 52 mm of the width blank.

How many A4 sheets make an A0?

Sixteen, in a 4 by 4 grid, if the sheets print edge to edge. With a quarter-inch printer margin on each side, it takes 21 sheets in a 3 by 7 grid.

What resolution should an A0 poster be?

As a rule of thumb, 150 PPI is enough for a poster read from a metre or two away. That's 4,967 × 7,022 pixels for a full-sheet image. At 300 PPI it's 9,933 × 14,043.
