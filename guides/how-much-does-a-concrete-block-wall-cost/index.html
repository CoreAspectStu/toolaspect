How Much Does a Concrete Block Wall Cost?

⏱️ 6 min read

A concrete block wall runs $15 to $30 per square foot installed, with materials alone at $3 to $13. The arithmetic is refreshingly honest: 1.125 blocks per square foot, roughly $2.25 a block, one 80-lb bag of mortar per 33 blocks. What turns a $600 pallet into a $6,000 wall is everything around the blocks — footing, rebar, grout, and a mason's day rate.

Square-foot pricing is how masons quote because wall difficulty scales with height and reinforcement, not just size. The 2026 bands:

Fence-height walls also get quoted per linear foot, since height is fixed: most 6-foot block walls land between $45 and $95 per linear foot, and taller or engineered retaining walls run well past that. If a quote looks cheap, the first thing to check is whether the footing is in it (more on that below).

What does a whole wall cost by size?

Block-count math is the same everywhere: wall area minus openings, times 1.125 blocks per square foot, plus a waste factor — 5% for a straight run, 8–10% once corners and cuts pile up. Why does labor dominate the quote?

Because a block wall is three projects stacked on each other. First the footing: concrete poured below grade, wider than the wall, level within a mason's tolerance — get it wrong and every course above telegraphs it. Then the laying itself: each block gets buttered, set, leveled, and aligned to a string line, and a skilled crew averages a few hundred blocks a day. Then reinforcement: most structural walls want vertical rebar in the cores at code spacing and those cores grouted solid, which adds steel, grout, and inspection stops.

Masons run $35+ an hour, and experienced crews earn it — the difference between a straight wall and a wavy one is invisible in the materials list. Openings add lintels, corners add cut units (half blocks at openings, corner blocks at turns), and anything tall adds scaffolding. This is why the per-square-foot band is wide: two walls of identical size can carry very different difficulty.

How much does DIY block work save?

On paper, half the project or better — materials at $3–$13 per square foot against $15–$30 installed. That 30×4 garden wall is 142 blocks (about $320), 5 bags of mortar premix at $7–$10 each, a borrowed or rented mixer, and a weekend you'll feel on Monday.

The realistic advice: small and short first. A 4-foot garden wall on a slab is a genuinely doable starter project. A foundation wall is not — between the footing pour, rebar schedule, grout fills, and code inspection, DIY masonry on structural walls usually ends in a paid redo. For the footing concrete itself, the concrete calculator sizes the pour, and the rebar calculator handles the steel schedule; our rebar cost guide prices that line item.

Cinder block, concrete block, CMU — what's the difference?

Nothing that matters to your quote. "Cinder block" is the older name from when units were cast with coal-cinder aggregate; modern ones are concrete masonry units (CMUs) with sand and gravel. The terms get used interchangeably in pricing, and the standard unit everywhere is the nominal 8×8×16 — actual 7-5/8 × 7-5/8 × 15-5/8 once you subtract the 3/8-inch mortar joints. Widths of 4, 6, 10, and 12 inches exist for different wall thicknesses, but height and length stay 8×16, so the block count per square foot doesn't change with width. Only the per-block price does.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a concrete block wall cost per square foot?

$15 to $30 per square foot installed for a standard wall, with Homewyse's May 2026 basic install at $18.87 to $24.17. Materials alone run $3 to $13 per square foot, and labor-only rates run $10 to $17 when you supply the block.

How many concrete blocks are in a square foot of wall?

1.125. A nominal 8×8×16 block covers 0.889 square feet of wall face, so a 100-square-foot wall takes about 113 blocks before waste. Add 5% waste for straight runs and 8–10% for walls with corners, openings, or lots of cuts.

How much does a 6-foot block fence wall cost per linear foot?

Most 6-foot block fence walls quote out between $45 and $95 per linear foot installed, with reinforced and engineered retaining walls above that band. At $15–$30 per square foot, six feet of height works out to $90–$180 per linear foot of face before efficiencies; multi-course deals and simple layouts pull the real-world number down.

Is the footing included in a block wall quote?

Usually as a separate line, and it's not small. Footings sit below frost depth in cold climates, typically 8–12 inches thick and at least twice the wall width — a 100-foot wall can add 5–7 cubic yards of concrete plus excavation. Quotes that omit the footing look $2,000–$4,000 cheaper and aren't, so ask specifically.

How much do you save building a block wall yourself?

Half the installed price or more, since materials run $3 to $13 per square foot against $15–$30 installed. A 30×4 garden wall is 142 blocks (about $320 at $2.25 each) plus 5 bags of mortar premix at roughly $7–$10 a bag. The catch: masonry is slow, unforgiving work, and if the footing and first course aren't level, every course above shows it.
