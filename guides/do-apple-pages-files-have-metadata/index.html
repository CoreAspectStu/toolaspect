Do Apple Pages, Numbers and Keynote Files Have Metadata?

📄 6 min readUpdated November 6, 2026

Some, but not where you'd look first. The newer Pages, Numbers and Keynote files we took apart had no author field at all. Names turned up on comments instead, alongside a language and region code, an app build history and a preview picture of the first page. Here's what we found in 8 sample files, what exporting changes, and how to send a cleaner copy.

What metadata is inside a Pages, Numbers or Keynote file?

Less than you'd think in the newer format, and more than you'd think in the old one. A .pages, .numbers or .key file is really a zip package. Inside are compressed .iwa files, a Metadata folder, a Data folder of images and some preview pictures.

We didn't have a Mac to make test files, so we used public samples from three open-source parser projects: Apache Tika, numbers-parser and keynote-parser. That gave us 6 files in the current format, the newest saved by Numbers 15.4, and 2 old Pages '09 files. We unzipped each one, decompressed every .iwa file and searched it for names and other text.

The big shift is the author field. Pages '09 had one, along with title, keywords and comments boxes, and both old files had them filled in. None of the newer files had anything like it.

These are other people's test files, so treat the counts as a sample, not a rule. Both commenter names came from Numbers files; we had no new-format Pages file with comments or tracked changes.

Does a Pages file show who wrote it?

Through comments and tracked changes, yes. Apple's Pages guide says the name on your comments and edits comes from the Author field in Pages > Settings > General. In a shared iCloud document, it's the name on your Apple Account instead. Change it, and Apple says your old comments update too.

In a Numbers sample with seven comments and replies, the commenter's full name sat in one part of the package, next to an ID string. The comment text was stored somewhere else. We couldn't save a new version, so we can't say if deleting the comments also clears that name. Work on a copy and assume it might stay.

The other items are smaller leaks. The language and region code says roughly where the author's Mac was set up. In Keynote, the Data folder kept images under what look like their original file names, and a file name like "IMG_4021" or "smith-offer-final" can say more than you'd want.

Both were PDFs made by macOS, and both had a name in the PDF author field that wasn't the one in the document's author box. A Mac-made PDF can carry a name you never typed into the document.

How do you send an Apple file without the extras?

  1. Work on a duplicate. Use File > Duplicate, so your working copy keeps its history.
  2. Delete comments and settle tracked changes. Accept or reject all changes, then turn tracking off. Apple says you can't turn it off until every change is handled.
  3. Clear presenter notes and skipped slides in Keynote, or leave their boxes unticked when you export to PDF.
  4. Check your author name. If it's your full name and you'd rather it weren't, change it in Settings before you comment.
  5. Export to PDF or Office, then run the copy through our remover and open it the way the recipient will.
  6. Check photos before you add them. We couldn't test whether Pages keeps location data from a phone photo. Our EXIF viewer shows what an image holds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Pages files store the author's name?

Not in a properties field, in the 6 newer-format files we checked. Names show up on comments and tracked changes instead, taken from the Author field in Pages settings or your Apple Account name for shared files. Old Pages '09 files did have an author field.

Can I remove metadata from a .pages file?

Pages doesn't have a remove-personal-info button that we know of, and our remover doesn't accept .pages files. Delete comments and tracked changes, then export to PDF or Word and clean that copy.

Does a Pages PDF export include comments?

Only if you choose to. Apple's export page says you tick a checkbox to include comments, and Keynote has separate boxes for comments, presenter notes and skipped slides.

Do Keynote files keep image file names?

In the sample we checked, yes. The Data folder held each image under its original file name. Rename sensitive images before you add them to a slide.

Is a .numbers file safe to email?

It depends what's in it. Our samples held commenter names, a language and region code and a preview picture. Delete comments and any hidden rows or columns you don't want seen, or export to PDF or Excel and check that copy.
