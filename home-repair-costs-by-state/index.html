How to use these numbers

Each table shows a state-level installed-cost average for 2026. Real quotes vary with property size, material grade, labour rates and permits — treat these as a mid-range baseline and expect metro areas to run 10–25% above the state figure. Combining tables is the fastest renovation budget: a roof plus windows in Mississippi lands near the national low, while the identical scope in California or Hawaii runs 30%+ above average, mostly from labour rather than materials.

Method: national baseline averages from installed-cost surveys, scaled per state with published regional construction-cost and insurance-rating indices; figures rounded to the nearest dollar.

FAQ

Which state has the cheapest home repair costs?

Mississippi, Arkansas and Oklahoma consistently post the lowest averages across the trades, driven by lower labour rates and lower permitting overhead.

Why do identical jobs cost differently by state?

Labour rates, permit fees, climate-driven code requirements (for example hurricane-rated roofs in Florida) and material logistics all shift the state average.

Are these prices installed or materials-only?

Installed — they include typical labour. An itemised contractor quote should separate the two so you can compare against these baselines.

Why is car insurance not scaled like the repair tables?

Insurance pricing follows state tort law, fraud rates, weather exposure and urban density rather than construction labour — which is why Michigan and Florida top that table while construction-cheap states dominate the others.
