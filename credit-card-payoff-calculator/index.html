Credit Card Payoff Strategies

Avalanche Method: Pay off highest-interest debt first. Saves the most money mathematically.

Snowball Method: Pay off smallest balance first. Psychological wins keep you motivated.

Balance Transfer: Move debt to a 0% APR card for 12-18 months. Every dollar goes to principal.
