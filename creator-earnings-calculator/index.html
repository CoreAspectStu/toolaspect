Maximizing Creator Revenue

Top creators don't rely on subscriptions alone. PPV (pay-per-view) content, custom requests, and tips often generate 40-70% of total revenue. Focus on retention — keeping existing subscribers is 5x cheaper than acquiring new ones.

Revenue Optimization Tips

  • Bundle Pricing: 3-month and 6-month subscriptions at 10-15% discount
  • PPV Strategy: 2-3 PPV messages per week at $5-15 each
  • Custom Content: Charge $50-500+ for personalized content
  • Trial Links: Free or discounted trials convert 15-25% to paid
  • Cross-Promotion: Collab with similar-sized creators to grow faster
