How the Boosty Calculator Works

Boosty is a subscription platform in the Patreon family — monthly tiers, donations, and content sales — with a fee structure that's cheaper on the surface than its Western competitors but paid back in payout friction. The calculator models both layers so the number you see is what actually lands.

The formula

Gross = subscribers × price + tips + sales. Boosty commission = gross × commission rate. Payout fee = (gross − commission) × payout percentage + fixed fee. Net = what's left. The effective cut divides total fees by gross, which is the honest way to compare platforms — a 20% cut plus payout fees costs more than 20%.

How to use it

Enter your subscriber count, monthly price, and the tips and sales you typically pull in. The fee defaults match Boosty's published FAQ and a standard card withdrawal; if your dashboard shows a different post-February 2026 rate, just type it in. Everything recalculates as you type.

A worked example

200 subscribers at $4.99 is $998 a month, plus $150 in donations and $80 in content sales: $1,228 gross. Boosty's 11.7% combined commission takes $143.68, leaving $1,084.32. The card payout fee of 1.4% takes another $15.18, for a net of $1,069.14 a month — about $12,830 a year. The effective total cut is 12.94%, or $12.94 out of every $100 earned.

Run the same income through a 20%-cut platform and the net drops to $968.65 a month. Boosty's cheaper commission is worth $100.50 a month here — real money, though only part of the platform decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much commission does Boosty take?

Boosty's own FAQ puts the combined commission at about 11.7%, which includes both the service's cut and payment-system fees. Third-party reviews place the total between 10% and 15% depending on the payment method. A commission unification announced in February 2026 changed the structure, so check your creator dashboard for the exact rate applied to your income.

What are Boosty's payout fees?

Withdrawals carry a separate fee that depends on the method. Card payouts run about 1.4% plus a small fixed fee of roughly 20 RUB. Other methods differ, and international creators should expect additional banking or currency-conversion costs on their side.

Is Boosty free to join?

Yes. There is no signup fee and no monthly platform charge — Boosty makes its money from the commission on what creators earn, not from subscriptions to the service itself.

How does Boosty compare to OnlyFans and Fansly?

On cut alone, Boosty is cheaper: roughly 10-12% all-in versus the 20% that OnlyFans and Fansly take. On a $1,228 monthly gross that difference is worth about $100 a month. The trade-off is audience size — OnlyFans has far larger native discovery — and Boosty's payment rails are primarily built around Russian banks and cards.

Does Boosty withhold taxes?

No. Boosty pays creators as independent earners and does not withhold income tax. What you owe depends on where you are tax-resident and how your income is registered — many Russian creators use the self-employment tax regime — so set aside a portion of every payout and confirm the rules that apply to you.
