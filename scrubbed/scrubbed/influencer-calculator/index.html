How Much Should Influencers Charge?

The standard formula is $100 per 10,000 followers for Instagram posts. TikTok commands higher rates ($200-500 per 10K). YouTube integrations start at $500 per 10K. Adjust based on engagement rate (above 3% = premium), niche (finance and tech pay more), and exclusivity.

Factors That Affect Your Rate

  • Engagement Rate: Higher than 3% lets you charge 20-50% more
  • Niche: Finance, tech, and B2B pay 2-3x more than lifestyle
  • Usage Rights: Brand usage (ads, website) = 2-5x base rate
  • Exclusivity: Not working with competitors = 30-100% premium
  • Deliverables: Stories, carousels, reels bundled = package pricing
