What Is a Good Engagement Rate?

Engagement rate varies by platform and follower count. Here are average benchmarks:

  • Instagram: 1-5% (above 3% is excellent)
  • TikTok: 4-18% (highest of all platforms)
  • YouTube: 2-10% (likes + comments per view)
  • X/Twitter: 0.5-1% (engagements per impression)
  • LinkedIn: 2-6% (highest for B2B)

How to Improve Engagement

Post consistently, respond to every comment within the first hour, use trending audio on TikTok and Reels, create saveable content (carousels, lists, tutorials), and ask questions in your captions.
