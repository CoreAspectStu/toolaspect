Loan Payment Formula

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where M = monthly payment, P = principal, r = monthly rate, n = number of payments

Average Interest Rates by Loan Type (2026)

  • Auto loan (new car): 5-8%
  • Auto loan (used car): 7-12%
  • Personal loan: 8-36% (depends on credit)
  • Student loan (federal): 4.5-7.5%
  • Student loan (private): 4-15%
  • Business loan (SBA): 8-13%

Tips for Getting the Best Rate

  • Check and improve your credit score before applying
  • Compare offers from multiple lenders
  • Consider credit unions — often 1-2% lower than banks
  • Shorter terms = lower rates but higher monthly payments
  • Make a larger down payment to reduce the loan amount
