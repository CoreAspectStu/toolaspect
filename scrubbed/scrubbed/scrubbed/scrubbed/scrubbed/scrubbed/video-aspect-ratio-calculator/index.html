Video Dimensions by Platform

Using the correct aspect ratio prevents awkward cropping and black bars. Each platform has optimal dimensions that maximize engagement and visibility in feeds.

  • YouTube: 1920×1080 (16:9) — 4K (3840×2160) preferred
  • TikTok / Shorts: 1080×1920 (9:16) — vertical full screen
  • Instagram Feed: 1080×1080 (1:1) or 1080×1350 (4:5)
  • Instagram Story/Reel: 1080×1920 (9:16)
  • Pinterest: 1000×1500 (2:3) — taller pins get more clicks
