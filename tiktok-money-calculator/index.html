How TikTok Pays Creators

The TikTok Creator Rewards Program pays approximately $0.50-$1.00 per 1,000 views for eligible videos over 1 minute. Shorter videos earn less. Most TikTok income comes from brand deals ($200-$20,000 per post depending on follower count), live gifts, and affiliate marketing.
