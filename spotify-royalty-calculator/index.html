How Music Streaming Pays

Spotify pays approximately $0.003-$0.005 per stream, meaning you need about 250,000 streams to earn $1,000. Apple Music pays nearly double at $0.007/stream. Tidal pays the most at $0.012/stream. Royalties vary by listener's country and subscription type.
