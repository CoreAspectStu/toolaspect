How the Paver Calculator Works

Enter your patio or walkway dimensions (or a direct area), pick your paver size, and this calculator returns the exact number of pavers to buy including a waste factor for cuts and breakage. It also estimates the tons of crushed base rock for the specified compacted thickness and the pounds of polymeric sand needed to sweep into the joints. Use the price field to get a full material cost estimate for your project.

Formulas Used

  • Pavers per sq ft: 144 ÷ (paver length × paver width in inches)
  • Paver count: ceil(area × (1 + waste%) × pavers per sq ft)
  • Base rock: tons = area × (thickness ft ÷ 27) × 1.4 (crushed stone ≈ 1.4 tons/yd³) — plus ~20% for compaction
  • Polymeric sand: ≈ 1 lb per 4–5 sq ft of paver surface for typical 3/16" joints

Worked Example

A 20 ft × 16 ft patio = 320 sq ft with 16"×16" pavers. Each paver covers 256 sq in = 1.78 sq ft, so 320 ÷ 1.78 ≈ 180 pavers. With 10% waste: 199 pavers. At $3.50 each that's ~$697 in pavers. Base rock at 6" compacted: 320 × 0.5 ft = 160 ft³ ≈ 5.9 yd³ × 1.4 × 1.2 compaction ≈ 10 tons. Polymeric sand: about 70–80 lbs (two 40–50 lb pails).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much waste should I add for pavers? 10% for simple rectangular layouts, 15% for diagonal (45°) patterns or curved borders, and up to 20% with herringbone and lots of cuts.

How thick should the base be? 4–6 inches of compacted crusherrun for patios and walkways; 8–12 inches under driveways that carry vehicles.

How many 12×12 pavers in a square foot? Exactly one. 16×16 covers 1.78 sq ft; 4×8 holland covers 0.22 sq ft (4.5 per sq ft).

Do I need bedding sand too? Yes — about 1 inch of concrete sand under the pavers, roughly 1 yd³ per 325 sq ft. The polymeric sand in this calculator is only for the joints on top.
