Car Costs, Calculated

Owning a car is a stack of numbers that mostly arrive as surprises: a repair quote, an insurance renewal, a ticket with a surcharge hiding behind it, a relocation that needs the vehicle moved 1,200 miles. The calculators in this collection convert each surprise into a range you can check a quote against, using published national data and the same arithmetic a shop, broker, or adjuster would use.

Start with the cost of keeping the car on the road. The repair calculator prices 25 common jobs as parts plus flat-rate labor, so you can see whether a quote's hours or markup is doing the work. The insurance estimator and fuel cost calculator cover the recurring lines, and the payment calculator handles the financing that started it all.

Then the episodic costs. A color-change wrap runs $2,000 to $5,500 depending on the car and finish; shipping one cross-country runs $900 to $1,850 open. And when things go wrong, the accident and ticket calculators price settlements and surcharges before you accept a first offer. Every tool links to a longer guide when you want the reasoning, not just the number.
