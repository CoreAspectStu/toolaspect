How the Blow-In Insulation Calculator Works

Enter your attic footprint and target R-value. The calculator converts the R-value to the installed depth needed for your material (cellulose insulates at about R-3.7 per inch, loose fiberglass at R-2.9–3.1 per inch), subtracts any existing insulation, and returns the number of bags to buy using each product's coverage chart. A typical 25–30 lb bag of cellulose covers about 40–67 sq ft at R-49 depending on the brand and settling characteristics.

Formulas Used

  • Depth needed: R-value ÷ R-per-inch (cellulose 3.7, fiberglass ~2.9–3.1)
  • Net depth: depth needed − existing depth (existing depth rated at its own R/in)
  • Bags: ceil(area ÷ coverage per bag at target R) — coverage per bag from the product chart, typically 40–67 sq ft at R-49
  • Rule-of-thumb check: bags ≈ area × depth ÷ bag yield (cellulose bag ≈ 6.5 ft³ settled)

Worked Example

A 40 × 30 attic (1,200 sq ft) targeting R-49 with no existing insulation: depth = 49 ÷ 3.7 = 13.2" of cellulose. Coverage per 25 lb bag at R-49 runs about 40 sq ft for dense products and up to 67 sq ft for premium low-dust blends — assume 50 sq ft: 1,200 ÷ 50 = 24 bags. At $15/bag that's $360 of material, or roughly $0.30/sq ft.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bags of insulation do I need for 1,000 sq ft at R-49? Cellulose: roughly 17–25 bags (coverage 40–67 sq ft per bag at R-49 depending on product). Check the coverage chart printed on the bag — it's legally required to state settled coverage.

How deep is R-49 blown insulation? About 13.2" of cellulose or 15.5–17" of loose fiberglass. R-60 needs ~16" cellulose / ~21" fiberglass.

What R-value should my attic have? IRC zone 2–3: R-30–49; zone 4: R-49; zones 5–8: R-49–60. ENERGY STAR recommends R-60 in cold climates.

Does blown insulation settle? Yes — cellulose settles about 20%, which the manufacturer's coverage chart already accounts for. Blow to the chart's minimum thickness, marked on attic rulers stapled to the joists.
