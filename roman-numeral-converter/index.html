Rules

  • Subtractive: IV=4, IX=9, XL=40, XC=90, CD=400, CM=900
  • Additive: VI=6, XV=15, LX=60, DC=600
  • No symbol repeats more than 3 times (III=3, but 4=IV)
  • V, L, D are never repeated

Where Roman Numerals Are Used

Movie sequels (Rocky IV), monarch names (Elizabeth II), Super Bowl numbering, book chapters, clock faces, copyright dates, and building cornerstones.
