Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Our Promise: Your Data Stays in Your Browser

ToolAspect is built differently. Every tool on this site — from the finance calculator to the image compressor — runs entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. We do not upload, store, or transmit your data to any server. When you use a tool, your inputs and results never leave your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No accounts or sign-ups. We don't ask for your name, email, or any personal information.
  • No tool data is stored. What you type into a calculator or paste into a formatter stays on your screen and is gone when you close the tab.
  • No server-side databases. This is a static site hosted on Cloudflare Pages — there is no backend that could store your data even if it wanted to.

Cookies

ToolAspect does not set any cookies of its own. If we add features in the future that require cookies (such as saving preferences), we will update this policy and request your consent first.

Third-Party Services

We currently use or may use the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Cloudflare Pages — Our hosting provider. Cloudflare may log standard request metadata (IP address, request time, user agent) as part of their CDN infrastructure. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense (if/when enabled) — Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. See Google's Ad Privacy.

Your Rights

Because we don't collect or store personal data, there is no personal information for you to request, modify, or delete. If you believe any third-party service has data about you, please contact that service directly using the links above.

Children's Privacy

ToolAspect is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else, in compliance with COPPA and similar regulations.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and revise the content accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
