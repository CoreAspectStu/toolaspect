About ToolAspect

Who we are, how our tools are built, and why everything is free.

Who we are

ToolAspect is an independent publisher of free online calculators, converters, and document tools, run by a small team of engineers and researchers. The site launched with a handful of developer utilities and has grown into a library of more than 600 tools covering finance, health, pets, weddings, construction, legal costs, and everyday file tasks.

We are not a lender, insurer, medical provider, or lead-generation front. We don't sell your data and we don't collect it in the first place: every calculator runs entirely in your browser, and there is no backend that could store what you type.

Our methodology

Every tool starts with the question people actually type, not with a template. Before a calculator ships we: (1) implement the real formula behind the answer, from amortization math to the Mifflin-St Jeor equation; (2) verify it against two worked examples computed by hand; (3) add reference data tables — rates, thresholds, state-by-state rows — sourced from primary references such as IRS publications, CMS data, and peer-reviewed meta-analyses; and (4) publish a plain-English explanation of the formula and its limits alongside the tool.

The full process, including how we review, update, and correct tools, is documented in our editorial policy.

Why the tools are free

The marginal cost of a static page on a CDN is close to zero, so we don't need to charge you. The site is supported by unobtrusive advertising; that's the entire business model. No accounts, no premium tier, no "free trial" that asks for a card, and no watermarks on your output.

We also publish embeddable versions of many calculators so other sites can offer them free — attribution links in the embeds are how word spreads, not how we monetize you.

What we are not

Our calculators produce estimates, not professional advice. A mortgage calculator cannot know your lender's overlays; a benefits calculator cannot know your case file. Where a decision has real money or health attached, we say so on the page and point you to the authoritative source.

Contact

Found a bug, a wrong number, or a tool you wish existed? We read everything: hello@toolaspect.com. Corrections get priority — see the corrections policy for how fixes are handled and noted on the page.
