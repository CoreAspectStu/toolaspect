Häufig gestellte Fragen

Wie wird meine Endnote berechnet, wenn die Abschlussprüfung 20 % zählt?

Ihre Kursnote ergibt sich aus Ihrer bisherigen Note mal 0.80 plus Ihrem Prüfungsergebnis mal 0.20. Aus 84 % vor der Prüfung und 70 % in der Prüfung werden also 67.2 + 14 = 81.2 %. Um statt des erzielten Ergebnisses das benötigte zu finden, lösen Sie dieselbe Gleichung rückwärts: (Zielnote − bisherige Note × 0.80) ÷ 0.20.

Was, wenn mein Kurs nach Kategorien benotet statt mit einer einzigen bisherigen Note?

Compute your current grade category by category first: homework average times its weight, quiz average times its weight, and so on, skipping the final exam category. That weighted number is your current grade; the final's category weight is the final's weight. Then run the standard formula.

Meine Abschlussprüfung ersetzt mein schlechtestes Testergebnis. Wie ändert das die Rechnung?

It changes your current grade, not the formula. Recompute your test-category average as if your lowest test were replaced by your expected final score, then use that higher current grade in the formula. This policy is a gift: it raises both your current grade and your ceiling, so the score you need usually drops by several points.

Kann eine einzige Abschlussprüfung mich um eine volle Notenstufe fallen lassen?

Yes, when its weight is large. At 40% weight, a 90% student who scores 50% on the final drops to 54 + 20 = 74%, two letters. At 10% weight the same collapse only takes them to 86%. The heavier the final, the more your semester of work is a floor you can fall through — check the weight before you decide how hard to study.

Umsatzsteuersätze der Bundesstaaten

Five states levy no statewide sales tax: Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire, Oregon, and Alaska (local taxes in some Alaska and Montana cities). Most other states set a base rate between about 4% and 7%, and local governments layer county, city, and special-district taxes on top — which is why the rate you actually pay is often higher than the state figure. California has the highest state-level rate at 7.25%, but the highest combined state-plus-local averages sit elsewhere: Louisiana leads 2026 at 10.11%, followed by Tennessee at 9.61% and Washington at 9.51% (Tax Foundation). Always check local rates — city and county taxes can add 1-4 percentage points on top of the state base.

So wird die Umsatzsteuer berechnet

The arithmetic is one multiplication: tax = price × rate, total = price + tax. A $250 purchase in NYC at 8.875% adds $22.19, for a total of $272.19. What complicates real transactions is which price is taxable. Discounts and coupons usually reduce the taxable base, but mail-in rebates generally do not — tax is typically charged on the pre-rebate amount. Shipping charges are taxable in some states (notably New York and Texas, when the item itself is taxable) and exempt in others, like California, when separately stated. Trade-ins reduce the taxable price on car purchases in most states, a meaningful exception worth hundreds of dollars on a vehicle deal.

Was unterschiedlich besteuert wird

Categories carve up the base. Unprepared groceries are exempt or reduced-rate in most states, though a handful tax them fully. Restaurant meals and prepared food are almost universally taxed at full rate, and some cities add an extra meals tax. Clothing is exempt in Pennsylvania, Minnesota, and New Jersey, and exempt under $110 per item in New York. Prescription drugs are exempt nearly everywhere; over-the-counter drugs vary. Services — haircuts, repairs, legal work — are taxable in some states and not others, which is why a barber and a mechanic can face different tax rules in the same town.

Online-Käufe und Gebrauchsteuer

Since the Supreme Court's 2018 South Dakota v. Wayfair decision, states can require remote sellers to collect sales tax once the seller crosses an economic nexus threshold, commonly $100,000 in annual sales or 200 transactions into the state. Before Wayfair, online retailers without physical presence often did not collect, leaving buyers technically responsible for declaring a complementary "use tax" on their state income tax return — an obligation that predates the internet and still applies to untaxed out-of-state purchases, including those from small sellers below nexus thresholds. Marketplace facilitators like Amazon, eBay, and Etsy now collect on behalf of their third-party sellers in nearly all states that have sales tax.

Holiday-specific wrinkles also matter: many states run sales-tax holidays — most commonly a back-to-school weekend in August exempting clothing, school supplies, and sometimes computers below price caps — and during those windows the register applies the exempt rate automatically. A further caveat for comparisons: "combined average" figures weight local rates by where spending happens, so a specific address can sit above or below the state average; the only exact number is the rate for the specific jurisdiction of the sale, which state revenue department lookup tools provide.

Businesses face the mirror-image problem: collecting the right rate, on the right base, in every jurisdiction they ship into — which in states with home-rule local taxation can mean thousands of distinct rate combinations. Most states that require seller collection also require filing returns monthly or quarterly, and late filings carry penalties and interest even when the tax itself was collected. Small sellers under nexus thresholds in a given state generally need not register or collect there, but the moment they cross the threshold, registration obligations typically begin with the next sale period.

Häufig gestellte Fragen

Wie berechne ich die Umsatzsteuer?Multiplizieren Sie den Preis mit dem kombinierten Satz als Dezimalzahl — bei $100 und 8.875 % kommen $8.88 hinzu. Der kombinierte Satz umfasst staatliche, Kreis-, Stadt- und Bezugssteuern, wo sie gelten.

Welche Bundesstaaten haben keine Umsatzsteuer?Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire und Oregon haben keine landesweite Umsatzsteuer, obwohl Teile von Alaska und Montana lokale Steuern erlauben.

Werden Gutscheine und Rückvergütungen unterschiedlich besteuert?Rabatte senken meist den steuerpflichtigen Preis; Mail-in-Rebates in der Regel nicht — in den meisten Staaten zahlen Sie Steuer auf den vollen Betrag vor Rückvergütung.

Wird Versand besteuert?Je nach Bundesstaat: steuerpflichtig in Staaten wie New York und Texas, wenn die Waren steuerpflichtig sind; in Kalifornien in der Regel befreit, wenn separat ausgewiesen.

Schulde ich Steuer auf Online-Käufe, die nicht abgeführt wurde?Ja, als Gebrauchsteuer in Ihrer Einkommensteuererklärung — eine Pflicht, die weiterhin für Käufe bei Verkäufern unterhalb der Nexus-Schwellen gilt.
