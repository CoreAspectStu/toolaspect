How the Wall Framing Calculator Works

Enter wall length, height, and count, pick 16 or 24 inch on-center spacing, and the calculator returns the full framing package: studs per wall and total (including a starter corner plus your extras for openings and tee intersections), plate boards (one bottom plate and two top plates = 3 × wall length), sheathing sheets at 32 sq ft per 4×8 panel, and a lumber cost total. It is the quick takeoff you do before ordering the lumber drop.

Formulas Used

  • Studs per wall: floor(length_ft × 12 ÷ OC) + 1, plus extra studs for corners and openings
  • Plates: 3 × wall length (single bottom + double top plate), converted to 8 ft boards: ceil(3 × length ÷ 8)
  • Sheathing: sheets = ceil(wall area ÷ 32) where a 4×8 sheet = 32 sq ft
  • Wall area: length × height × number of walls

Worked Example

A 12 ft wall at 16 in OC: floor(144 ÷ 16) + 1 = 10 studs, plus 4 extras for a corner and a door opening = 14 studs. Plates: 3 × 12 = 36 linear ft = 5 eight-foot boards. Sheathing on a 12 × 8 ft wall: 96 ÷ 32 = 3 sheets per wall. Four walls of a 12×12 shed: 56 studs, 20 plate boards, 12 sheets, roughly $350–400 in framing lumber at typical big-box prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why +1 stud? Spacing starts at the beginning of the wall, so a 12 ft run at 16 in OC has studs at 0, 16, 32 … 144 in — ten positions, which is floor(144/16)+1. Corners, wall intersections, and door/window edges need additional studs (typically 2 per corner, 2 per tee).

16 or 24 inch OC? Exterior walls and load-bearing walls are usually 16 in OC; 24 in OC is common for interior non-bearing walls and with modern 2×6 construction. Code may dictate spacing for tall walls.

Why two top plates? The double top plate ties intersecting walls together and overlaps corners, staggering joints for structural continuity. Interior non-bearing partitions sometimes use a single top plate.

What about studs for 9 ft walls? Buy 104-5/8 in precut studs for 9 ft ceilings (92-5/8 in precuts for 8 ft). The count math is identical — only board length changes.
