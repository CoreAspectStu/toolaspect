Free Everyday Calculators for Dates, Percentages, Grades & More

Most math in daily life is not hard — it is just fiddly. Counting days between two dates across a month boundary, splitting a restaurant bill with an 18% tip, converting a military timestamp from a shipping confirmation, working out what a 30% discount actually saves you. This collection of free everyday calculators exists so that none of those moments costs more than a few seconds. Every tool runs instantly in your browser, needs no signup, and explains its result so you can trust it — and defend it — rather than just accepting a number.

The directory is organized into three groups that mirror how these questions arrive. Math & numbers covers percentages, changes, ages, and randomness. Time & dates handles countdowns, time zones, world clocks, and military time. School & work collects the academic and money-adjacent tools: GPA, final grades, word counts, typing speed, reading time, tips, and discounts.

Math & Numbers: Percentages, Ages, and Randomness

The percentage tools are the most-visited in this group because percentage phrasing is genuinely ambiguous. "What is 15% of 80" and "20 is what percent of 50" are different problems, and the Percentage Calculator handles both phrasings in one place. The Percentage Change Calculator answers the third phrasing — how much did a value grow or shrink, expressed as a percent — which is the form price changes, raises, and statistics take. Together the two tools cover essentially every percentage question that comes up in shopping, news, and personal finance.

The Age Calculator solves a subtler problem than it appears to: computing an exact age in years, months, and days requires handling months of different lengths and leap years correctly, which is exactly where mental math fails on official forms and eligibility checks. The Scientific Calculator provides advanced math functions when a four-function calculation is not enough, and the Random Number Generator picks unbiased numbers in a range you set — for drawings, games, sampling, and picking who goes first.

Time & Dates: Countdowns, Zones, and Notation

The date and time group starts with counting. The Date Difference Calculator returns the days between two dates — the number you need for deadlines, age eligibility, project durations, and "how long until" questions of any kind. The Countdown Timer turns a target date into a live ticking countdown, and the holiday countdown pages (Christmas, New Year, Summer) apply the same machinery to dates people actually count down to, preloaded and ready.

When the question is coordination rather than counting, the Time Zone Converter translates a specific time from one zone to another for meeting planning and call scheduling, while the World Clock shows current time across several zones at once — the tool you want pinned during a distributed project. The Military Time Converter handles the 24-hour clock used in logistics, healthcare, and travel itineraries, converting in both directions so "1730" and "5:30 PM" stop requiring a pause.

School & Work: Grades, Words, and Money Basics

The academic tools cover the numbers students actually track. The GPA Calculator weights course grades by credit hours to produce a grade point average on the standard 4.0 scale, so a four-credit course moves the average more than a two-credit one. The Grade Calculator answers the end-of-semester question — what do I need on the final? — by combining your current grade with the exam's weight. The Word Counter, Typing Speed Test (words per minute and accuracy), and Reading Time Calculator (words to reading minutes) round out the study desk.

Two money basics finish the group. The Tip Calculator applies a tip percentage to the bill and splits the total across the party, showing the tip, grand total, and each person's share. The Discount Calculator takes an original price and a discount percentage and returns the sale price and the amount saved — the arithmetic behind every "30% off" sign, done correctly every time.

Tips for Getting Accurate Results

  • For date math, double-check the year — the single most common input error on any date tool
  • When a percentage question confuses you, read it aloud: "X percent of Y" and "X is what percent of Y" go in different fields
  • For GPA, include every course even the ones you'd rather forget — dropping a low grade quietly inflates the estimate you're relying on
  • When planning across time zones, confirm whether each participant is on standard or daylight time — the converter uses zone rules, but your meeting invite may not
  • For countdowns to recurring events, check whether the page targets this year's or next year's date

One Page, Zero Friction

Every calculator here follows the same rules: free, no account, no install, instant results, and all computation in your browser. Nothing you type — bills, birthdates, grades — is stored or transmitted. Bookmark the two or three you use most; the directory will keep the rest ready for the moment a fiddly calculation shows up.
