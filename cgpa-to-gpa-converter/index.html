How the Conversion Works

Indian universities grade on a 10-point CGPA scale; US schools use a 4.0 GPA. The bridge most people use is linear: divide by the top of one scale, multiply by the top of the other. GPA = (CGPA ÷ 10) × 4, which is just CGPA × 0.4. It's honest arithmetic but a simplification — a 10-point scale and a 4.0 scale don't reward identical performance identically, which is why credential evaluators do their own mapping.

The three percentage conventions

Percentage conversion is where it gets genuinely messy, because India has several formulas in circulation:

  • CBSE ×9.5: derived by CBSE from actual Class X score averages — the most widely quoted convention.
  • Simple ×10: used by some universities and, informally, by many students.
  • (CGPA − 0.75) × 10: the formula several technical universities specify on their transcripts.

Pick the one your institution uses where it matters (official paperwork); use ×9.5 or ×0.4 for quick estimates everywhere else.

A worked example

Say you finished engineering with an 8.2 CGPA and an application form asks for a 4.0-scale GPA. Linear conversion: 8.2 × 0.4 = 3.28, which lands in the B+ band. As a percentage, 8.2 × 9.5 = 77.9% — Distinction under the typical classification. Going the other way, a program that states a 3.6 GPA minimum corresponds to 3.6 ÷ 4 × 10 = 9.0 CGPA.

One caution for grad-school applications: if a form lets you report "GPA on a 10-point scale," do that instead of converting. Admissions readers at research universities see Indian transcripts regularly and generally prefer the native number plus your class rank than a self-converted figure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert CGPA to GPA on the 4.0 scale?

Use the linear formula GPA = (CGPA / 10) x 4, which is the same as multiplying by 0.4. An 8.5 CGPA becomes 3.40, a 7.5 becomes 3.00, and a 9.0 becomes 3.60. Most converters use this method; there is no single official conversion because US schools evaluate foreign transcripts themselves.

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

The best-known convention is percentage = CGPA x 9.5, which CBSE derived for Class X results, so an 8.2 CGPA is about 77.9%. Many universities use a plain x10, and several technical universities use (CGPA - 0.75) x 10. Your own university's transcript rule always governs.

What is 8.5 CGPA in GPA?

About 3.40 on the 4.0 scale (8.5 x 0.4). That sits in the B+ band on standard US letter grades. As a percentage, 8.5 x 9.5 = 80.75%, which is Distinction territory on the typical Indian classification.

Is there an official CGPA to GPA conversion?

No. US admissions offices and credential evaluators like WES apply their own analysis to Indian transcripts, and some simply ask for the CGPA on the 10-point scale alongside a class rank or percentage. The x 0.4 formula is the common approximation for your own estimates, applications that ask for a self-reported GPA, and eligibility screens.

What CGPA is a 3.0 GPA?

Working backwards, CGPA = (GPA / 4) x 10, so a 3.0 GPA corresponds to 7.5 CGPA. A 3.5 GPA is about 8.75, and a 3.6 is 9.0.

What is a good CGPA for studying in the US?

For competitive graduate programs, a CGPA of 8.0 and above (3.2 GPA) clears most minimum filters, 8.5-9.0 (3.4-3.6) is competitive, and 9.0+ (3.6+) is strong anywhere. Admissions committees weigh the full transcript: trend, rigor of courses, and university reputation all matter alongside the number.
