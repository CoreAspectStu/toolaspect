How the Soffit & Fascia Calculator Works

Enter your building footprint or the measured eave perimeter directly, the width of the overhang, and how many sides have open eaves. The calculator returns the linear feet of fascia and the square footage of soffit material, plus the number of fascia boards to buy at your chosen board length and a material cost estimate. It works equally for aluminum coil, vinyl soffit panels, and wood — just adjust the prices.

Formulas Used

  • Eave perimeter (rectangular, 2 sides): 2 × length
  • Eave perimeter (all 4 sides / hip roof): 2 × (length + width)
  • Soffit area: perimeter × overhang width (+ waste%)
  • Fascia LF: perimeter (+ waste%) — doubled if installing both fascia and sub-fascia
  • Boards: ceil(fascia LF ÷ board length)

Worked Example

A 40 × 30 house with a gable roof (2 eave sides): perimeter = 2 × 40 = 80 LF. With a 1.5 ft overhang, soffit = 80 × 1.5 = 120 sq ft, plus 10% waste = 132 sq ft. Fascia = 80 LF + 10% = 88 LF → six 16-ft boards. At $2/sq ft soffit and $4.50/LF fascia: $264 + $396 ≈ $660 in materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between soffit and fascia? Soffit is the horizontal underside of the overhang; fascia is the vertical board running along the rafter tails that the gutter mounts to.

Vented or solid soffit? Vented in continuous or panel strips on the eave sides to feed attic intake ventilation — typically 50/50 vented to solid for a balanced system.

What size is fascia board? 1×6 or 1×8 for 2×4/2×6 sub-fascia on most homes; 2×8 or 2×10 on deeper rafter tails. Aluminum fascia is bent from coil stock on site.

How much waste should I add? 10% for straight runs; 15% with corners, rakes, or lots of joints on short stock.
