      How the Title Scoring Works

      This is a rules engine, not a language model. Each title is measured on four dimensions, weighted, and docked for all-caps shouting or outright clickbait phrasing. Same title in, same score out — that determinism is the point: you're comparing drafts against a fixed yardstick, not asking a model to flatter you.

      The formula

      Length score: 100 points for 40-60 characters, prorated below 40, minus 2.5 per character above 60. Power-word score: 40 base plus 20 per hit from a list of 21 proven attention words (secret, mistake, why, how, nobody, truth, stop…), capped at 100. Number score: 100 if the title contains a digit, 50 if not. Structure score: 100 for a bracket or parenthesis tag like "(Full Guide)", 60 without. The weighted sum is 0.35×length + 0.25×power + 0.15×number + 0.15×structure + 0.10 base, minus 20 points per all-caps word (up to 60) and 30 for empty-calorie shock phrasing.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      How long should a YouTube title be?

      Aim for 40 to 60 characters. YouTube truncates titles past roughly 60 characters in search results on most devices, and the mobile home feed can cut even earlier, so the clickable part of your message should land in the first half of the title. Our scoring gives 40-60 characters a perfect length score and deducts 2.5 points per character past 60.

      What makes a YouTube title get clicked?

      The patterns that consistently earn clicks: a specific number, a curiosity gap the video actually closes, a clear who-it's-for signal, and front-loaded keywords so the meaning survives truncation. The scorer weighs length at 35%, power words at 25%, presence of a number at 15%, and structure like brackets at 15%, with penalties for shouting in all caps.

      Do numbers in YouTube titles help?

      Odd, specific numbers tend to outperform round ones and vague claims — 7 reads more concrete than 10, and 1,200 reads more credible than a lot. Numbers also set expectations for list videos, which viewers can start at any item. In the scorer, a title containing any digit earns full credit on that dimension.

      Should I use ALL CAPS in a YouTube title?

      At most one emphasized word. Titles written mostly in caps read as spam, perform worse in tests creators share publicly, and stand out for the wrong reason next to a clean thumbnail. The scorer deducts 20 points per full-word all-caps word after the first, down to 60 points off.

      Is this title generator free and does it need an account?

      Free, no account, and nothing leaves your browser. The tool works from a fixed library of title patterns plus a transparent scoring formula — there's no AI generation step, so results are instant, repeatable, and cost nothing to run.

      How is this different from an AI title generator?

      AI tools invent new phrasing from a model; this tool fills proven title patterns with your topic and scores the results against fixed rules. The advantage is determinism: the same topic always returns the same patterns, and the score for any title is the same every time — useful when you're comparing drafts rather than looking for novel wording.
