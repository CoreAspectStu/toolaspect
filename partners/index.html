Frequently Asked Questions

How can my website or tool become a ToolAspect partner?

Reach out through the contact details on this page. Listings are editorial and free — we feature directories, communities, and tools we genuinely use, and we do not sell placement. Good candidates are free, privacy-respecting utilities with no forced signup.

Can I embed ToolAspect tools on my partner site?

Yes. Most ToolAspect calculators and converters are available as iframe embeds from the embeds page, free and without branding requirements beyond a link back. Embeds run from our CDN so your site stays fast. This is the most common way partners use our tools.

Does ToolAspect do link exchanges or sponsored posts?

No. We do not sell links, accept sponsored posts, or trade links for placement. Partner listings reflect tools and communities we actually recommend. If someone offers you paid ToolAspect placement, it did not come from us.

Where can press find ToolAspect facts, badges, and branding?

Right here: this page carries our badge graphics and the key facts about the site (what we build, how tools are funded, and our privacy stance). Logo files and OG images are available on request via the contact link. We're happy to answer press questions by email.
