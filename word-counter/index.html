Why Word Count Matters

Word count is important for essays, blog posts, social media, SEO meta descriptions, and advertising copy. Different platforms have different optimal lengths:

  • SEO blog post: 1,500-2,500 words
  • Meta description: 150-160 characters
  • Tweet: 280 characters
  • Title tag: 50-60 characters
  • Facebook post: 40-80 characters for engagement
  • Email subject line: 30-50 characters
  • College essay: Usually 250-650 words

Reading Time Estimate

The average adult reads about 200-250 words per minute. This tool uses 225 WPM to estimate reading time, which is the standard for most content platforms.
