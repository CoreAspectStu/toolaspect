How the Epoxy Calculator Works

Enter your garage floor dimensions and this calculator returns the total epoxy needed for a full two-coat system. Epoxy floor coatings are applied thinner than paint — a standard 100%-solids or water-based garage epoxy covers roughly 160 sq ft per gallon per coat, and manufacturers call for two coats (a primer/body coat plus a finish coat) for durability. The calculator also converts gallons to standard 2-car garage kits, which typically cover about 250 sq ft for both coats, so you can buy either bulk gallons or boxed kits.

Formulas Used

  • Floor area: sq ft = length × width
  • Gallons: gal = ceil( sq ft × coats ÷ coverage ) with coverage ≈ 160 sq ft/gal per coat
  • 2-coat kits: kits = ceil( (sq ft × 2) ÷ 250 ) — one standard kit covers ~250 sq ft for a 2-coat application
  • Cost: gallons × price per gallon

Worked Example

A typical 2-car garage at 22 × 22 ft = 484 sq ft. Two coats at 160 sq ft/gal: 484 × 2 ÷ 160 = 6.05 → 7 gallons with a 10% waste allowance. In kit terms: 484 × 2 ÷ 250 = 3.9 → 4 standard 2-car kits. At $75/gallon, material runs about $525 before etching cleaner, primer, and decorative flakes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why 160 sq ft per gallon and not 350–400? Paint covers 350–400 sq ft/gal because it goes on at 1–2 mils dry. Epoxy is applied at 8–12 mils total across two coats, so real-world coverage drops to about 160 sq ft per gallon per coat. Always follow the manufacturer's spread rate on the box.

How much does one epoxy kit cover? Most consumer garage kits (Rust-Oleum EpoxyShield and similar) claim 250 sq ft for a single-coat application on a 1-car garage; for a proper 2-coat 2-car system plan on 3–4 kits.

Do I need to etch the floor first? Yes — concrete must be cleaned and acid-etched (or ground) or the epoxy will peel at the first hot-tire turn. Budget cleaner/etcher at roughly 1 gallon per 300 sq ft.

100% solids vs water-based epoxy? 100%-solids epoxy covers less per gallon (~120–160 sq ft) but builds a thicker, more durable floor; water-based products spread farther but need more coats.
