How to Calculate a Tip

Our free tip calculator makes it easy to figure out the right tip amount for any bill. Simply enter your bill total, choose a tip percentage, and optionally enter the number of people to split the bill.

Quick Tip Calculation Methods

  • 10% method: Move the decimal one place left ($50 → $5)
  • 15% method: 10% + half of that amount ($50 → $5 + $2.50 = $7.50)
  • 20% method: Move decimal left, double it ($50 → $5 × 2 = $10)
  • Double the tax: Quick restaurant method — double the sales tax for ~15%

International Tipping Customs

Tipping is not universal. In Japan and South Korea, tipping can be considered insulting — good service is simply expected. In most of Europe, a service charge is included in the bill, so rounding up or leaving small change is sufficient. Australia and New Zealand don't have a strong tipping culture. In the US and Canada, tipping is essentially mandatory for restaurant service.

Tip on Pre-Tax or Post-Tax?

Technically, you should calculate tip on the pre-tax subtotal. However, most people calculate on the total bill for simplicity. The difference is small — on a $100 bill with 8% sales tax, tipping 20% pre-tax vs post-tax differs by just $1.60. Use whichever feels right.
