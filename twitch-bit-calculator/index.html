How Twitch Bits Work

Bits are Twitch's virtual currency. 1 bit = $0.01 for the streamer. Viewers buy bits at a slight markup (100 bits = $1.40, 1500 bits = $19.95). Streamers receive 100% of the bit value — Twitch takes their cut from the buyer's purchase price, not the streamer payout.
