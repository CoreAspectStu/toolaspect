💍 Marriage Green Card Cost Calculator

Every 2026 form fee on the path from wedding to green card

A marriage green card costs $2,065–$3,005 in USCIS fees when the spouse adjusts status inside the US: I-130 at $675 ($625 online) + I-485 at $1,440, plus $260 for the work permit and $630 for travel parole under the fees effective February 5, 2026. Consular processing from abroad totals about $1,235; the K-1 fiancé route runs about $3,505 by the time the post-wedding paperwork is done. Add a $100–$500 medical exam, and $2,000–$5,000 if you hire an attorney — making a DIY adjustment about $3,200–$3,600 all-in.

How the Marriage Green Card Cost Calculator Works

Government fees are fixed and published; the variables are which forms your route needs, whether you can file the petition online, the medical exam, and whether a lawyer touches the case. This calculator stacks all of it into the number couples actually budget against.

The math

Adjustment of status: I-130 ($675 paper, $625 online) + I-485 $1,440 + optional I-765 $260 and I-131 $630. Consular: I-130 + DS-260 $325 + $235 USCIS immigrant fee. K-1: I-129F ($675/$625) + $265 K visa fee, then — after marrying within 90 days — the full I-485/I-765/I-131 stack plus $235. On top: a medical exam you set ($100–$500 typical), incidentals (photos, copies, postage), and your attorney choice.

How to use it

Pick the route that matches where the immigrating spouse lives, not the cheaper one. Leave the work-permit and travel-parole boxes checked for adjustment cases — filing them later means paying again, and leaving the country without advance parole is treated as abandoning the application. Set the medical to a quote from a civil surgeon if you have one; the band is wide.

A worked example

A DIY adjustment of status, paper filing, all options on: $675 + $1,440 + $260 + $630 = $3,005 in government fees, plus a $250 medical and $75 of photos, copies, and mailing — $3,330 all-in, right in the typical $3,200–$3,600 band. Filing the I-130 online shaves $50. The same couple with a $3,500 flat-fee attorney lands at $6,830, inside the $5,200–$8,600 attorney band. A spouse abroad doing consular processing pays just $675 + $325 + $235 = $1,235 to the government — about $1,560 all-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a green card through marriage cost?

$2,065 to $3,005 in USCIS fees for adjustment of status inside the US — the I-130 at $675 ($625 online) plus the I-485 at $1,440, with $260 more for a work permit and $630 for travel parole. All-in with the medical exam and incidentals, a do-it-yourself case runs about $3,200 to $3,600; with an attorney, $5,200 to $8,600.

What are the USCIS fees for a marriage green card in 2026?

I-130 petition $675 on paper or $625 online, I-485 application $1,440, I-765 work permit $260, and I-131 advance parole $630. For filings under the fee schedule effective February 5, 2026, the I-765 and I-131 are charged separately — they used to be free while an I-485 was pending — which added about $890 to the standard package.

Is consular processing cheaper than adjustment of status?

Yes, in pure fees: about $1,235 — the $675 I-130, a $325 family-based DS-260 visa fee, and the $235 USCIS immigrant fee after entry — versus $2,065 to $3,005 for adjusting inside the US. But you don't really choose: adjustment requires the spouse to be in the United States, generally after a lawful entry, while a spouse living abroad uses the consular route.

How much does a K-1 fiancé visa cost?

About $3,505 in government fees for the full journey: a $675 I-129F petition ($625 online), the $265 K visa application fee at the consulate, then — after marrying within 90 days — the $1,440 I-485, $260 work permit, and $630 travel parole, plus the $235 green card production fee. The K-1 often costs more overall than marrying first and filing an I-130.

How much is the medical exam for a green card?

$100 to $500 set by the civil surgeon, with big metros at the top of the band. Missing vaccination records add charges, so gathering shot documentation beforehand is one of the few controllable costs. The exam results are valid for two years.

How much does an immigration lawyer cost for a marriage green card?

A flat $2,000 to $5,000 is typical at a private immigration firm, with straightforward in-state cases near the bottom. Many couples split the difference with a one-time document review for a few hundred dollars. Straightforward cases can reasonably file without one; prior denials, deportation history, criminal records, or long overstays argue for hiring counsel.

Are marriage green card fees refundable if the case is denied?

No. USCIS keeps filing fees once processing begins, whatever the outcome. That's the strongest argument for getting the package right the first time — and the reason many DIY couples still pay for an attorney review before sending it.
