How the Dog Walking Cost Calculator Works

A walker's price is really three decisions stacked together: how long the walk is, how often it happens, and who else is on it. This calculator prices all three so you can compare a midday group walk against a daily private hour before you commit to either.

The formula

Per-walk base comes from the national band for your walk type, multiplied by your market tier. Additional dogs add $8 per dog per walk (the middle of the $5-$10 range), a prepaid package discount comes off the top, and a platform fee goes back on if you book through one. Weekly = per walk × walks per week; monthly = weekly × 52 ÷ 12; annual = weekly × 52.

How to use it

Pick the walk type and how many walks a week you actually need — most working owners use 3-5 midday walks, not 5 daily ones. Set the market to match where you live, and if you're comparing an independent walker against Rover or Wag, run it twice: once with the platform fee on, once off, to see the booking-fee gap in dollars. The package discount field answers "is the 20-walk pack worth it" directly.

A worked example

A golden retriever in Chicago needs a 30-minute midday walk five days a week. The national typical is $27.50; a major metro runs about 1.3x, so $35.75 a walk. That's $178.75 a week, $9,295 a year over 52 weeks, or $774.58 a month averaged out. Packages stack on top: at the $27.50 national typical, a 20-walk package at 12% off brings the per-walk price to $24.20 — $484 instead of $550, saving $66 every 20 walks, which annualizes to $858 for a five-a-week dog.

The budget path: a group walk at $16, three afternoons a week, runs $48 a week — $2,496 a year — and the dog still comes home tired. And if you have two dogs, one walker taking both on the same 30-minute walk costs $35.50 at the typical plus $8, versus $55 for two separate walks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a dog walker cost per walk?

National rates for 2025-26 run $15-$25 for a 20-minute walk, $20-$35 for 30 minutes, and $30-$50 for a full hour. Group walks with other dogs run $12-$20 per dog. Big metros run 20-40% over these bands and small towns under them, and a second dog from the same household typically adds $5-$10 per walk.

How much is dog walking per week or per month?

At the $27.50 national typical for a 30-minute walk, five walks a week runs $137.50 — about $596 a month or $7,150 a year. The budget path is a group walk three times a week, about $48 a week or $2,496 a year. A one-hour walker five days a week is the premium path at $10,400 a year.

Is Rover or Wag cheaper than an independent dog walker?

On price alone, independent walkers usually win by 10-20% because platforms take a cut from both sides — Rover takes roughly 20-25% from the sitter and adds an owner-side booking fee of around 11%, and Wag sells a monthly pass that covers its walk fees. Platforms earn it back on vetted reviews, insurance, and support if something goes wrong, which matters more for a stranger entering your home.

Do dog walkers charge more for a second dog?

Usually $5-$10 extra per walk for a second dog from the same household, since the walk itself is already happening. Two dogs on one 30-minute walk at the $27.50 typical plus $8 works out to $35.50 — two solo walks would cost $55.

Are prepaid walk packages worth it?

Most independent walkers and many platforms discount prepaid packages 10-15%. At 12% off a $27.50 walk you pay $24.20, so a 20-walk package costs $484 instead of $550 and saves $66. The catch is expiration windows and refunds if you move or the walker quits — buy the smaller package first.

Should I tip my dog walker?

Yes, tipping is standard: 15-20% for routine walks, more around holidays. On a $27.50 walk that's $4-$6, and a holiday card with a week's worth of tip is a common thank-you for a daily regular walker.
