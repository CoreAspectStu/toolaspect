How Twitch Creators Make Money

Twitch has multiple revenue streams: subscriptions (Tier 1 at $4.99, Tier 2 at $9.99, Tier 3 at $24.99), Bits ($0.01 per bit cheered), ad revenue, sponsorships, and donations. Partners get a 70/30 split up to $100K, then 50/50.
