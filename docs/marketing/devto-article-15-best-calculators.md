## Why free tools beat spreadsheets (for most people)

A spreadsheet is more flexible, sure — but flexibility is a tax. You pay it in setup time, formula bugs, and files you can't open on your phone. A purpose-built calculator encodes the correct methodology (amortization schedules, tax brackets, funding-fee tiers) so you can't silently get it wrong.

The pattern worth noticing: every financial decision you'll make this year — a loan, a lease, a sale, a policy — has roughly three numbers you need *before* you negotiate. Ten seconds with the right calculator beats an hour of arguing after you've signed.
