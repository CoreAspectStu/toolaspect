## 2. Realistic CTR by Position (desktop+mobile blended, 2026 SERPs with AI overviews & rich results)

| Pos | CTR | Pos | CTR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25–35% (often <15% with AI overview) | 6 | 3–4% |
| 2 | 12–15% | 7 | 2.5–3% |
| 3 | 7–9% | 8–10 | 1.5–2.5% |
| 4–5 | 4–6% | 11–20 | <1% |

Tool SERPs are brutal: Google often satisfies queries with its **own built-in widget** (calculator, unit converter, color picker, timer) — those SERPs give organic results maybe 10–20% of clicks combined. Plan around this: assume avg CTR of 1.5–3% blended across positions 5–10, and near-zero for widget-SERP keywords (percentage calculator, unit conversions for common pairs).
