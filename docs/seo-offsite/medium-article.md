If you're a developer, content creator, or anyone who uses the web regularly, you've probably experienced "tool fatigue" — bouncing between dozens of sites, each demanding an account, serving ads, and tracking everything you do.

I built ToolAspect to solve this. It's a single site with 1,400+ free tools organized by category. Here are some highlights:

## 🔧 Developer Tools

- **JSON Formatter** — Paste, format, validate. Syntax highlighting included.
- **Regex Tester** — Real-time matching with capture group highlighting.
- **CSS Minifier** — Paste your CSS, get minified output instantly.
- **JWT Decoder** — Decode header, payload, and signature without sending tokens anywhere.

## 💰 Finance Tools

- **Loan Payoff Calculator** — See exactly how much interest you'll save with extra payments.
- **Compound Interest Calculator** — Visual growth chart with monthly contributions.
- **Salary Converter** — Convert between hourly, weekly, biweekly, monthly, and annual.
- **ROI Calculator** — Investment return with inflation adjustment.

## 🤖 AI Tools (Fastest growing category)

- **LLM Pricing Tracker** — Compare token costs across GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, and more.
- **SERP Optimizer** — Audit your page's meta tags and get improvement suggestions.

## 📝 Text & Content Tools

- **Word Unscrambler** — Supports 1M+ English words with definitions.
- **Lorem Ipsum Generator** — Configurable paragraphs, sentences, or words.

## Why Build Another Tool Site?

Most tool sites have deteriorated — heavy ads, popups, misleading "download" buttons, and signup walls for basic functionality. I wanted something clean and fast. Every tool:

- Works instantly in the browser (no server processing for most tools)
- Has no signup requirement
- Runs no tracking scripts
- Works on mobile
