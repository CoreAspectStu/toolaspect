## Long description
ToolAspect is a comprehensive library of more than 700 free online tools, built around one principle: everyday digital tasks shouldn't require an account, a subscription, or handing over your data.

The collection spans four main areas:
- **Calculators & converters** — hundreds of niche finance calculators (401(k) match, 529 plans, alimony by state, mortgage and insurance scenarios), health calculators, and unit/date/text converters.
- **PDF & file tools** — merge, split, convert, and manipulate files directly in the browser.
- **Privacy utilities** — password generators, hashers, text scrubbers, plus plain-English privacy guides that explain how to lock down your accounts and devices.
- **Reference charts & guides** — practical lookup tables and how-to guides for tasks people search for once and just want solved.

Every tool is client-side where possible, loads fast on mobile, works without JavaScript bloat, and asks for nothing: no email, no cookies wall, no upsell. ToolAspect is maintained by a small independent team that ships new tools weekly based on what users actually search for.

## Founder story (2 sentences)
ToolAspect started as a personal scratchpad of browser-based utilities built to avoid uploading sensitive documents to yet another "free trial" PDF site. It grew into a 700-tool library after we realized how many everyday tasks — from a 401(k) check to a privacy audit — could be solved instantly, client-side, with zero signup.
