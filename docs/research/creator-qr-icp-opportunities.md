## Lane Findings

### Creator (gap analysis)
- **Big gaps we don't cover:** engagement-rate calc, content/social calendar, title & thumbnail tools, media-kit/contract templates. `social media calendar` ($24.27 CPC) and `content calendar template` ($11.31) are the standout AdSense plays.
- **Already covered / low incremental:** youtube earnings (110K cluster — existing page), tiktok money (2,900 — exists), onlyfans earnings (30/mo — exists; adult niche is near-zero Adsense volume, keep for direct traffic only), twitch/ugc/podcast sponsorship all ≤110/mo.
- **Zero-volume terms (skip):** brand deal rates, sponsorship pricing calculator, tiktok algorithm checker, kick/instagram reel payout calculators — all 0 in Google Ads data.

### QR (gap analysis)
- We have generator/scanner/batch/logo; the missing high-CPC cluster is **dynamic/tracked QR** (2,400 @ $20.75 + 720 @ $21.98) — requires a redirect backend, which objexi already is. These are conversion tools, not AdSense tools.
- **Static quick wins:** wifi QR (2,900), vcard/business card (12,100 + 1,300), google review/forms QR (3,600 + 1,300), pdf-to-QR (1,600) — all buildable as pure client-side pages in days.
- Note: "scan qr code online" is 14,800/mo but it's a scanner utility (we have one) with weak monetization.

### Objexi ICP (job-role tools)
ICP from qr-aspect docs (`USE-CASES-AND-ONBOARDING-STRATEGY.md`): Manufacturing asset-intelligence managers, Facilities/compliance leads, Construction site managers, Fleet. Their daily tool needs decompose into: checklists (PM 590, JHA 390, facility 110 @ $22.72), reliability math (MTBF 480, OEE 260, TRIR 260), templates (work order 260 @ $13, maintenance schedule 210), and asset labeling ($17+ CPCs). Volumes are modest but CPCs are B2B-grade ($8–$23) and every tool can end in "track this with objexi" — these are lead-gen, not AdSense. The strongest funnel plays: #7 dynamic QR, #11 QR tracker, #12 PM checklist, #14 JHA builder, #21 asset tag generator, #24 downtime cost calculator.
