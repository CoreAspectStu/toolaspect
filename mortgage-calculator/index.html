How the Mortgage Calculator Works

This mortgage calculator estimates your monthly mortgage payment including principal, interest, property taxes, home insurance, PMI, and HOA fees. Enter your home price, down payment, interest rate, and loan term to get an instant, accurate payment estimate.

The Mortgage Payment Formula

The core mortgage payment formula is: M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]

  • M = Monthly payment
  • P = Principal loan amount (home price minus down payment)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12)
  • n = Number of payments (years × 12)

For example, a $320,000 loan at 6.5% interest for 30 years: r = 0.065/12 = 0.005417, n = 360. M = $2,022.62/month for principal and interest.

What Is Included in PITI?

PITI stands for Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance — the four components of most mortgage payments:

  • Principal & Interest: The core loan payment calculated by the formula above
  • Property Taxes: Typically 0.5%–2.5% of home value annually, collected monthly via escrow
  • Homeowners Insurance: Protects against damage; averages $1,000–$2,500/year
  • PMI: Required if down payment < 20%; typically 0.3%–1.5% of loan annually

How Down Payment Affects Your Payment

A larger down payment reduces your loan amount, which lowers your monthly payment and total interest. With 20% down or more, you avoid PMI entirely, saving hundreds per month. Even a 5% increase in down payment on a $400,000 home can save you over $150/month.

Mortgage Calculator Tips

  • Shop around for interest rates — even a 0.5% difference can save tens of thousands over 30 years
  • Consider property tax rates in your area — they vary significantly by state and county
  • Factor in closing costs (typically 2%–5% of the loan amount) when budgeting
  • Use the amortization schedule to see how extra payments can save you interest
  • Get pre-approved before house hunting to know your budget

Current Mortgage Rate Trends

Mortgage rates fluctuate based on economic conditions, inflation, and Federal Reserve policy. 30-year fixed rates have historically ranged from 3% to 8%. Always check current rates from multiple lenders to ensure you're getting the best deal. Even small rate differences compound significantly over a 30-year loan.
