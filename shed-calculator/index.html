How the Shed Calculator Works

Enter your shed's footprint and wall height and this calculator builds an approximate material list: framing lumber (studs at your chosen spacing plus plates for all four walls), siding area in square feet and squares (1 square = 100 sq ft, the unit siding is sold in), and roofing area with pitch and overhang factored in, expressed in roofing squares and shingle bundles at 3 bundles per square. It is the planning takeoff before you price a lumber package — window and door openings reduce siding slightly (enter the door width; standard door is 48 in).

Formulas Used

  • Studs per wall: floor(length × 12 ÷ OC) + 1, × 4 walls, plus ~3 extras per wall for corners/openings
  • Plates: 3 × (2 × (L + W)) linear ft — one bottom, two top plates on each of four walls
  • Siding: wall perimeter × height − door area, × (1 + waste%), reported in squares (÷ 100)
  • Roofing: (length + overhang) × (width + 2 × overhang) × pitch multiplier, in squares; bundles = squares × 3
  • Floor: footprint sq ft (add skids, joists at 16 in OC, and 3/4 in ply separately per your plan)

Worked Example

A 10 × 12 ft shed with 8 ft walls, 16 in OC, gable roof at ~1.10 multiplier, 6 in overhang, 48 in door: wall perimeter = 44 ft, gross wall area = 352 sq ft, minus a 16 sq ft door = 336 sq ft, × 1.15 waste = 387 sq ft ≈ 3.9 squares of siding. Roofing = (10+1) × (12+2) × 1.10 ≈ 169 sq ft ≈ 1.7 squares → 6 bundles of shingles plus underlayment. Studs: 10 studs/wall average × 4 + extras ≈ 50 studs and about 17 eight-foot plate boards (22 ft sides need longer stock or scarf joints).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much lumber do I need for a 10×12 shed? Roughly 50 studs, 132 lf of plate material, 4 sheets of 3/4 in floor ply, 4–5 squares siding, and 2 squares roofing — plus skids, floor joists, header/door framing, and trim. Total framing lumber for a basic 10×12 runs about $700–1,200 depending on market.

Does this include the floor and foundation? The calculator reports floor footprint area; plan on treated skids or a gravel pad, floor joists at 16 in OC, and 3/4 in plywood (footprint ÷ 32 sheets). See the concrete and aggregate calculators for slab and base options.

What's a square of siding/roofing? 100 square feet. Siding and shingles are priced and ordered per square; shingles come 3 bundles to the square for standard-weight architectural products.

Do I need a permit? Most jurisdictions require one above 100–120 sq ft — which is exactly why 10×12 (120 sq ft) is the most popular shed size. Check local setbacks too.
